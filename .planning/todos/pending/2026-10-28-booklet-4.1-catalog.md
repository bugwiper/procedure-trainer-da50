The checklist booklet (Diamond Flight Training, edition 4.1 of 05.10.2022) is binding for the owner. The fire, take-off and icing pages 16, 17, 18 and 23 are implemented (plan 05.1-04). The other pages the owner photographed are a catalog for later scenarios; each would be typed from the photo as printed (number marks, comment rows, boxed references; a follow-on checklist restarts the numbering after a comment row, lint L16):

- p11: Engine Failure on Ground, During Aborted Take-Off, During Continued Take-Off.
- p12: Engine Failure in Flight.
- p13: Restart Engine in Flight.
- p14: Oscillating RPM, RPM Overspeed, RPM Underspeed.
- p15: Defective Cowl Flap, Engine Troubleshooting.
- p20: Complete Failure of the Electrical System.
- p21: Emergency Extension of the Landing Gear, Landing Gear Unsafe Warning, Landing with a Defective Tire.
- p22: Landing with Defective Brakes, Landing with Gear Up.
- p23 (second half): ICE PROTECTION FAILURE is shown as display rows inside the icing scenario; a graded scenario of its own would need aerodynamic effects of ice.
- p24: Loss of Ice Protection Fluid Display, Freezing Rain/Drizzle Exit Procedures, Insufficient Engine Power in Icing Conditions.

The earlier AFM-based photos (`.input/checklist-*.png`, `.webp`) stay as AFM reference. Recheck the wording of the shipped ENGINE FIRE IN FLIGHT, ALTN 1 FAIL, ALTN 2 FAIL and ENG OIL TEMP against the booklet photos when those pages are next touched.
