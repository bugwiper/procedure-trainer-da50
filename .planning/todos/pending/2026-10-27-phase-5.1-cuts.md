Left out of phase 5.1 on purpose; each is a candidate for a later phase or a direct fix.

- [ ] **Two pedal controls with an all-of predicate.** The toe brakes are one latching control drawn as two pedals; real pedals are two separate controls held by the feet, and a predicate "both applied" would grade them.
- [ ] **Indent under sub-headings.** The AFM pages indent the rows under a sub-heading; the AFM form prints every row flat.
- [ ] **A climb-out scene.** The no-abort situation uses the take-off roll scene with a held 70 knots; a scene that shows the climb out over the runway end does not exist.
- [ ] **The fuel valve lift.** "OFF (lift and turn)" is graded as the state off of the FUEL selector; the lift is not modelled.
- [ ] **Aerodynamic icing effects.** Ice on the wings raises the stalling speed, a failed pitot heating loses the airspeed indication (the two CAUTION blocks); the icing scenario has no outcome. Comes with the physics phase.
- [ ] **Cabin smoke cleared by the open door.** Only the electrical master, fresh air and the open window clear smoke in the simulation; a door open in flight would too.
- [ ] **A harmful door open in flight or on the roll.** Opening the door lever at any time is credited and never harmful; unless the owner decides it earlier, a forbidden item "door opened before the aircraft has stopped" is a candidate (needs a stopped predicate).
- [ ] **Booklet marks.** The AFM pages use letters; if the booklet prints numbers, swap the `number` fields of the six files and the typed tables in `tests/checklist-reference.ts` (one edit per row).
