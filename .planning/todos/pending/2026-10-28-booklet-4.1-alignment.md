The booklet (Diamond Flight Training, edition 4.1 of 05.10.2022) is binding: it is what the pilot has in the cockpit. It differs from the AFM pages the phase 5.1 scenarios were typed from. The booklet marks rows with **numbers**, not letters, and is terser. Findings, scenario by scenario:

- **Engine Fire on Ground** (p16): 1 Engine master OFF, 2 Fuel valve OFF, 3 Electric master OFF; comment "After standstill and when engine stopped:"; 4 Doors OPEN; centred line "Evacuate". (AFM version had letters A to E, "After Standstill", "Door ... open", "Airplane ... evacuate immediately".)
- **Electric Fire / Smoke on Ground** (p16): ONE list, no "if the engine is running" branch: 1 Electric master OFF, 2 Power lever GROUND IDLE (GI), 3 Fuel valve OFF, 4 Engine master OFF; After standstill and when engine stopped: 5 Doors OPEN; Evacuate. The two variants engine_running and engine_stopped of the shipped scenario come from the AFM and are not in the booklet: remove them or keep them as a physics variation without a different checklist.
- **Fire / Smoke During Aborted TKOF** (p16): 1 Power lever GROUND IDLE (GI), 2 ECS shut-off PULL, 3 Brakes APPLY, 4 Fuel valve OFF, 5 Engine master OFF, 6 Electric master OFF; After standstill...: 7 Doors OPEN; Evacuate. Order differs from the AFM (fuel valve before engine master) and "apply - bring the airplane to a stop" is just APPLY.
- **Fire / Smoke During Continued TKOF** (p17): 1 ECS shut-off PULL; comment "If possible climb to safe height and land ASAP"; comment "When landing assured:"; 2 Engine master OFF, 3 Fuel valve OFF, 4 Electric master OFF, 5 Emergency front windows OPEN as necessary; Carry out: boxed EMERGENCY LANDING (ENGINE OFF) (page 9); After standstill...: 6 Doors OPEN; Evacuate. No WARNING block.
- **Engine Fire in Flight** (p17): already as shipped in the booklet form (1 to 8).
- **Electric Fire with Smoke in Flight** (p18): comment "If Oxygen System is installed and flight performed above 10,000ft:"; 1 ** Oxygen System OFF; 2 ** Emergency Descent INITIATE; comment "Continue:"; 3 Avionic master OFF, 4 Electric master OFF, 5 ECS - circulating air FRESH AIR, 6 ECS shut-off PULL, 7 Emergency front windows OPEN as necessary; "Land immediately"; "Consider and be prepared for:" boxed EMERGENCY LANDING (ENGINE OFF) (page 9); After standstill...: 8 Doors OPEN; Evacuate. No WARNING blocks.
- **Unintentional Flight Into Icing** (p23) is a different procedure from the AFM page: heading UNINTENTIONAL FLIGHT INTO ICING with the line "Leave icing area, inform ATC, continue with item 1", then the checklist INADVERTENT ICING ENCOUNTER & EXCESSIVE ICE ACCUMULATION: 1 * Ice-protection system HI + MAX, 2 * Prop De-Ice ON, 3 Pitot/Stall heat ON, 4 * Ice light ON as required, 5 ECS - Cabin heat ON, 6 ECS - Cabin air DEFROST, 7 Power lever INCREASE, change periodically, 8 Alternate air OPEN, 9 * Windshield de-ice USE AS APPROPRIATE, 10 Emergency front windows OPEN as required; footnote "* If ice-protection does not work properly: Continue with ICE PROTECTION FAILURE". The AFM page has no ice-protection system rows and has ALTERNATE STATIC; the booklet has no alternate static. The shipped icing scenario must follow the booklet. Follow-on checklist ICE PROTECTION FAILURE (rows 1 to 6 with a flaps/weight table).
- Row marks: numbers 1.. everywhere in the booklet; the letters of the shipped scenarios are wrong for the cockpit copy.

**More booklet checklists the owner photographed (not implemented, a catalog for later scenarios):** p11 Engine Failure on Ground, During Aborted Take-Off, During Continued Take-Off; p12 Engine Failure in Flight; p13 Restart Engine in Flight; p14 Oscillating RPM, RPM Overspeed, RPM Underspeed; p15 Defective Cowl Flap, Engine Troubleshooting; p20 Complete Failure of the Electrical System; p21 Emergency Extension of the Landing Gear, Landing Gear Unsafe Warning, Landing with a Defective Tire; p22 Landing with Defective Brakes, Landing with Gear Up; p24 Loss of Ice Protection Fluid Display, Freezing Rain/Drizzle Exit Procedures, Insufficient Engine Power in Icing Conditions.
