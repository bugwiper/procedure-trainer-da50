## Decisions (assumptions taken in planning; the owner may veto any of them)

All values are instructor-review values, none is an AFM figure. They follow plan 01 (W-01 to W-09).

- **C-01** The declared cue: `onset.cues` (at most four ids). L23 runs inside the Realistic start check, so the variant check L19 inherits it: unknown cue, a cue visible at the Realistic start, a cue not visible within `onset.message_within_s` (else the 12 s of L15) after the onset in a do-nothing run, at both ends of the window. L24: an onset needs at least one annunciation or one cue. Easy and Medium show the cues too (same art, same quantities).
- **C-02** Cabin smoke: hidden quantity `smoke_density` (0 to 100, base 0), a lag (tau 15 s) toward 100 times the parameter `smoke_source` (0 to 1, normal 0); no ventilation effect (the printed checklists of Phase 5.1 decide that); cue `cabin_smoke` 0 to 70. The overlay lies above every region, thicker over the displays, at most about 0.75 opaque so the displays stay partly readable, and never takes a press.
- **C-03** Preview flags for the owner review and for e2e that also run live: `?q=id:value,...` sets start quantities on the free panel (known ids inside their range, the rest ignored), `?flags=name,...` sets flags true. RESET clears them like any run state. No test hook setter is added.
- **C-04** Ice: hidden quantities `ice_pilot` and `ice_copilot` (0 to 100, base 0), parameter `ice_accretion` (0 to 1, normal 0). Per side: +40 per minute times the parameter, warm air -12 per minute (DIRECTION `lv.env_direction` at `defrost` and TEMPERATURE `lv.env_temp` at `t75` or `warm`; no fan control exists), so light icing clears slowly and heavy icing is only slowed. WIND SHIELD (`btn.deice_windshield` pressed) starts the reaction `windshield_spray`, flag `deice.windshield_spray`, 10 s of run time: pilot side (the left half, the seat of the photo) no accretion and -600 per minute, so it is clear after about 10 s; the co-pilot side is untouched. Afterwards the pilot side accretes again while the parameter is above 0 (no exit-from-icing model before Phase 5.1). The fluid quantity is Phase 6.
- **C-05** Ice visuals: cues `ice_pilot_side` and `ice_copilot_side` (0 to 60): a milky veil (up to 0.7 opaque) and crystals, each clipped to its half of the opening; the crystal count grows with the level, positions from a fixed table made with `mulberry32` at module load (no randomness at render); the spray streaks run down the pilot half only while the flag is set.
- **C-06** Extras, cheap and cuttable: `prop_stopped` from rpm (600 down to 0; the blur wedge fades and a crisp blade shows, so a correct ENGINE MASTER OFF in Engine Fire is visible) and oil streaks (hidden `oil_film`, parameter `oil_leak` 0 to 1, +30 per minute while the engine runs, cue `oil_streaks` 0 to 50). What does not fit goes into a todo with the reason.
- **C-07** No shipped scenario uses smoke, ice or oil yet (Phase 5.1). Lint and run behaviour are proven on a fixture scenario under `tests/fixtures/scenarios/`, not under `scenarios/`, so no pack lists it and the blueprint gate does not read it.

</objective>
