## Decisions (assumptions taken in planning; the owner may veto any of them)

Source: the owner spec of 2026-10-27 in `.planning/todos/pending/2026-10-23-realistic-level-visual-cues.md` and the owner's cockpit photo `.input/cockpit-view-reference.jpg` (read, never copied; described in own words).

- **W-01** The panel space grows upward and nothing existing moves. `panel.ts` gets `WINDSCREEN_H` 440 (the opening of the photo is about a fifth of its width) and `EXTENT` (x 0, y -440, w 2180, h 1740); `VIEWBOX` (2180 x 1300) stays the instrument-panel space, so every region, plate and control keeps its numbers. The registry takes an optional `extent`; zoom, pinch and pan clamp to it.
- **W-02** Phones and tablets (veto this one first). The Full panel view is the whole extent on every device, so the windscreen is never hidden (the Realistic cue lives there). Cost: the full view gets smaller (scale desktop 0.398 against 0.532 and phone 0.181 against 0.242, a quarter; tablet 0.361 against 0.433, a sixth). The phone needs a zoom for any control already, and the zoom views of the control regions are unchanged, so the 44 px gates stay as they are. The windscreen is its own zoom region (double tap in, Full panel out). No separate phone layout, no switch that hides the windscreen.
- **W-03** One art layer drawn before the shell (the glare shield covers its lower edge, so the art runs down to y 110, the coaming top lies between y 12 and 96), `pointer-events` none, outside the region groups. The region `windscreen` is invisible geometry for zoom and carries no control. Own paints prefixed `w-` in the layer's own defs; flat vector like the shell (no filter, mask, image, foreignObject); no text (G-0).
- **W-04** Frame after the photo, in own words: a dark canopy frame left, right and above around a wide arched opening, a central strut at x 1130 (over the glare shield hump) from an overhead console strip at the top centre, the silver engine cowl low in the centre with a clearly darker edge and shade (the owner notes it blends into cloud), a translucent propeller blade at about 11 o'clock.
- **W-05** A cue is a quantity read as a level 0 to 1 (`from`, `to`, arithmetic only), visible from `CUE_VISIBLE_LEVEL` 0.1 and drawn only then, so what lint will check in plan 02 is what the pilot sees. The table is aircraft data; a cue id never equals a physics parameter id (the blueprint gate lists those).
- **W-06** `flames` reads `fire_intensity` from 0 to 60: flames and a smoke trail rise from the cowl, size and opacity follow the level, a slow CSS flicker that stops under reduced motion. Easy and Medium start with the fire (intensity 40) and show it at once, Realistic from the onset.
- **W-07** The scene is data: an optional `scene` (absent means the aircraft default `cruise`), the ids in aircraft data, lint L22 for an unknown id. The four shipped scenarios say `scene: cruise`; the briefing sentence stays text.
- **W-08** The scene shows from the briefing until RESET on every level; the free panel shows the default. Cue levels also follow the quantities on the free panel (they stay as a run left them until RESET, like the gauges).
- **W-09** `?scene=<id>` (read once, like `?regions=1`) sets the scene of the free panel, so the owner and the e2e see parked and takeoff roll before Phase 5.1 ships scenarios on them; a session overrides it.
