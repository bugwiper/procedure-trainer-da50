## Decisions (assumptions taken in planning; the owner may veto any of them)

All values are instructor-review values. They follow plans 01 and 02 (W-01 to W-09, C-01 to C-07).

- **A-01** Audio is off on the first run and stays off until the pilot taps the footer control `Sound` (HTML in the footer beside About, a constant label, `aria-pressed`, never a panel label). The audio context is created inside that tap. The choice is kept in storage (`da50.sound`, memory fallback like the acknowledgment); a remembered "on" starts at the first press anywhere (autoplay rules). Off by default is my choice for a first visit: a drone nobody asked for in a classroom is worse than a missing one.
- **A-02** Sound only while a run is live: none on the free panel, none while the run clock waits (a modal open, a hidden tab). The tones are the aircraft's own sounds and never feedback on right or wrong; the exam stays silent in meaning.
- **A-03** Own synthesised tones, not the aircraft's chime or voice: a caution gives one two-note chime when it appears; a warning gives a three-pulse tone when it appears and repeats every 4 s while a warning is active, at most five times.
- **A-04** Engine drone: two detuned oscillators and a little filtered noise (a buffer from `mulberry32`, never `Math.random`) whose pitch and level follow the rpm quantity through one pure function; silent at rpm 0; smoothed with `setTargetAtTime`; low level.
- **A-05** Scenes: `cloud` (IMC cloud for icing, static) and the moving runway on `takeoff_roll` (a constant-speed scroll; Phase 5.1 ties it to the ground speed). Cut and written down as a todo: the dusk and night scenes (they need a panel lighting treatment as well as art).
- **A-06** The documentation is one task and closes the phase documents; the roadmap tick-off stays with the phase verification, as in phase 4.
