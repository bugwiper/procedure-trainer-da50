## Phase goal

Complete the panel replica so that nothing a pilot would find on the real panel is missing before scenarios start (ROADMAP phase 01.2, PNL-12 and PNL-13): every circuit breaker at its real position, the PFD and MFD in the well-known shape with softkeys, and the audio panel, autopilot panel and backup EFIS visible with inert buttons.

<objective>
Give the right-hand side of the panel room: widen the panel space from 2000 to 2180 units by adding only on the right, replace every hard-coded width by one independent source, extend the shell silhouette, prove the whole suite on the wider space, and record the owner decision with its new coordinates in the UI-SPEC.

Purpose: D-01 (the full set of breakers at the real positions with legible labels needs the real grid pitch of 36 units, about 260 units of width, and only about 144 units are free at x 1836 to 1980). The owner chose the wider variant (research option for Q1, UI-SPEC U-01) because individual breakers will matter in later scenarios; the roughly 8 percent scale cost on tablet and portrait phone is accepted.
Output: VIEWBOX 2180 x 1300, the extended shell, PANEL_VIEWBOX and the specs that read it, a green suite, and the amended UI-SPEC.
</objective>
