## Accomplishments

- **Data:** `BREAKER_ROWS` holds the 51 rows (19 in `cb_essential`, 19 in `cb_main`, 10 in `cb_low`, 3 in `cb_strip`; groups 15 / 15 / 8 / 10 / 3). The lower row uses the compact skin (hit 24 x 28, pitch 26), every other breaker hit is 28 x 28, the flap strip has pitch 36, the three plug cells are empty, the ratings 7½ are printed as written. Data comments record the confidence remarks (COM 1 and GPS/NAV 1 carry the digit by default, PEDALS rating assumed).
- **Independent reference:** `CB_REFERENCE` has 51 hand-typed rows (tolerance 8, review true); `CB_ROWS` (A1 to V6), `CB_LOWER_ROW`, `CB_STRIP_ROW` and `CB_COLUMNS` are typed from the printed layout. ORDERING gained the Table C3 rows: row chains (left-of and same-row), the column chains c1, c2 and c7, MASTER CTRL. left of COWL FLAP, ECU A below COM2, ELT left of ECU A, the strip chain `lv.flaps` to `sw.elt`, and all 48 non-strip breakers right of the MFD region (the strip breakers below it). The inventory gates follow without a literal.
- **Relation and containment gate (`tests/breakers.test.ts`, 74 cases):** a literal EXPECTED table (id, label lines, rating, group, region) checked against the registry in table order; flat ids, ratings are numerals; rows share y, columns share x, neighbouring columns are 36 apart (multiples across a plug cell), the lower row 26, the strip 36; the three plug cells hold no breaker (more than 14 units away); every footprint lies in its region and the bus plate (strip: its region) and none overlaps another; every non-pump box breaker lies fully (1 unit grid over the footprint) inside the polygon of its group; the three pump breakers lie inside their own pump boxes; region and group counts.
- **Skin loop:** all 51 breakers in both states and pressed or not: same strings, ring only when out, at most 12 elements, visible extent inside the hit and, region-local, inside the region. The static-text family block now counts against `CB_REFERENCE`.
- **Clearance gate (`e2e/art-fit.spec.ts`, desktop-chromium):** the glyph band of every breaker and plug label contains no sample (every 0.5 units) of any outline or pump box path and does not intersect any breaker socket box. Sensitivity check: widening the band by 3 units on each side makes the gate report 25 touches, so it bites.
- **E2E (`e2e/breaker.spec.ts`, both chromium projects):** all 51 exist and start in with no ring (ids from the reference); every breaker is tapped once (desktop: clicks at the full view; phone: region by region after a background-tap zoom, Full panel between regions), all 51 report out with 51 rings and an unchanged panel text list, a second pass restores all in with 0 rings; with the hook the 102 events are 51 out then 51 in in reference order, all from the user, seq 0 to 101; the Full panel button covers no breaker hit in any of the four breaker regions; the element budget.
