# Phase 01.2 Plan 05: Glass display shape Summary

**Both displays now sit on a 41-unit bezel lip with 12 triangle softkeys each, a header strip, a content band and a 12-slot legend row on the glass; only the MFD page key works, the other 23 keys are inert art, and a zone gate and a widened brand gate guard the result.**

## Accomplishments

- **Bezel (Task 1):** `DisplayBezel` grows 16 on top, left and right and 41 below (PFD and MFD y 184 to 608, x 346..873 and 1253..1780). `tests/layout.test.ts` has the new constants, a literal case for the four bezel edges, and the strip-gear sliver allowance of 28. GO-AROUND is still read exactly twice (shell tests green).
- **Data (Tasks 1 and 2):** `avionics.ts` holds the rows (MFD first centre 1288, pitch 41.5; PFD first centre 401, pitch 37.9; centre y 589; key 30 x 20 rx 3), the three legend tables, `softkeyCentre`, `legendTabCentreX` and the glass zones of Table D4. No UI imports, no maker or model names.
- **Keys:** `SoftkeyCap` draws the key (dark cap, light top edge, white up-triangle, no text). `SoftkeyBody` is now the cap with the 2.5-unit `#e6e9ed` stroke when selected and a 1-unit rim otherwise; the dead `labelWidth` prop and its call sites are gone. `SoftkeysFrame` draws the inert keys of the region it is given, skipping a slot where a control sits (derived from the registry, so mfd_softkeys draws 11 and pfd_softkeys 12), each with a `data-softkey` marker (`mfd:2` to `mfd:12`, `pfd:1` to `pfd:12`), pointer-events none and no handler.
- **Region:** `pfd_softkeys {356, 570, 479, 38}` named PFD SOFTKEYS, ends at x 835 (landing_gear starts at 836). The registry has 24 regions equal to `ALL_REGION_IDS`; README says "92 controls and 13 indicator lamps in 24 regions".
- **Glass (Tasks 1 to 3):** `LegendRow` (12 tabs, 37 wide on the MFD and 34 on the PFD, local y 349, text size 6) is drawn from the key data; the Engine tab is selected (cyan border 1.2, green underline bar) only on the MFD engine page. `GlassHeader` draws the frequency boxes, the labels N1, N2, RX, 1C, 2M and the swap arrow path; the MFD centre shows "Map - Navigation Map" or "EIS - Engine", the PFD centre is empty. Content moves follow Tables D5 and D6.
- **Gates:** `tests/avionics.test.ts` (29 cases: geometry from literals, legend alignment, key counts, inert frames, zone tags, header values, content moves); the e2e zone gate in `e2e/glass.spec.ts` (every painted element of pfd and mfd, both MFD pages, in the zone it is tagged with, tolerance 0.75); `tests/brand.test.ts` rejects G1000, NXi and GMA/GFC/GDU/GTX/GIA/GEA followed by a digit under src/ui/panel and src/aircraft, with a case that the check bites (AV/GDU FAN has no digit and passes).
