# Phase 01.2 Plan 08: Audio panel, backup EFIS and autopilot panel Summary

**The three centre-stack avionics units are drawn as inert artwork in their real positions: the audio panel with 21 keys, lamp slivers, volume knob and red DISPLAY BACKUP button, the backup EFIS with an attitude display, a tape display and a knob, and the autopilot panel with four knobs, a swap button and 12 keys; all inside their plates, nothing operable.**

## Accomplishments

- **Task 1 (tracer):** `AUDIO_PANEL` (Table G1): 21 keys 25 x 20 in two columns and eleven rows (row 9 has no right key), legends split at the space, 21 lamp slivers (COM1 MIC, COM1 and PILOT ICS lit amber `#ffb81c`, the other 18 `#3a3f46`), volume knob r 11 and the DISPLAY BACKUP button (bezel r 9, cap `#c4161f`, highlight `#f0535b`) with the legends DISPLAY and BACKUP. `BezelColumnFrame.tsx` now exports `ColumnPart`, `Key` and `Knob`; the key renderer gained the lamp sliver and two-line legends; a `button` element kind draws the red button (`data-display-backup`). `AudioPanelFrame` replaces the housing-only entry. Tracer gate (vitest, typecheck, lint, format, avionics, art-fit and static-text specs on both chromium projects) passed end to end; logged "tracer verified end-to-end, expanding".
- **Task 2:** `STANDBY_EFIS` (Table G2) with the housing {1029, 298, 114, 264}, upper display {1040, 308, 92, 116}, lower display {1040, 434, 92, 116} and the knob (1134, 429, R 8), stored region-local, plus the tape numerals. `StandbyEfisFrame`: attitude display (sky and ground halves, white horizon, pitch ticks, roll arc with ticks and pointer, yellow fixed symbol), tape display (KTS and FEET labels, readouts 0 and 1400, green 3 wide speed bar, five numerals per tape, BARO box with 1013) in the glass-digit class, and the plain knob. ## Assumptions taken

- **EFIS readouts placed above the tapes:** the plan fixes the idle readouts 0 and 1400 and five numerals per tape but not their rows. A readout in the middle of a tape would overlap a numeral (the gate measures text), so the boxes sit in a row between the KTS/FEET labels and the tapes. Proposal U-18, row in `docs/PANEL-REVIEW.md`.
- **EFIS numerals:** speed 180, 160, 140, 120, 100 (as in Table G2); altitude 1600, 1500, 1400, 1300, 1200 ("around 1400" taken as five steps of 100). Both are exported tables in `avionics.ts`.
- **Key ids:** the legend lower-cased with the space as a dash (`com1-mic`, `nose-up`); the e2e tap case relies on `com1-mic`.
- **Button attribute:** the red button carries `data-display-backup` for every button-kind element because it is the only one.
- Lit slivers, the omitted volume-knob legends and the autopilot column read from the MFD left strip are the stated defaults U-16 and U-17; the EFIS content is U-18. All are rows in `docs/PANEL-REVIEW.md` for the morning review, file `src/aircraft/da50rg/avionics.ts`.
- `UnitHousing` (plan 06) is no longer registered in `Panel.tsx`; it stays exported for its existing unit tests. Removing it would be a cleanup for plan 09.
