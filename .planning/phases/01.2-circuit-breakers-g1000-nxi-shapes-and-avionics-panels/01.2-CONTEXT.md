# Phase 01.2: Circuit breakers, G1000 NXi shapes and avionics panels - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-20
**Status:** Ready for planning (discussed together with Phase 01.1; depends on it)

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Complete the panel replica started in Phase 01.1 so that nothing a pilot would find on the real panel is missing before scenarios start: every circuit breaker at its real position, the G1000 NXi PFD and MFD in their well-known shape with all softkeys, the audio panel, the autopilot panel, the backup EFIS and the other fixed items.

**Not in this phase:** live gauge values, scenario-driven annunciations (Phase 2 and 3), working autopilot or audio functions, G1000 menu structures (PNL-07 stays in Phase 3 as the simplified transponder entry).

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

All decisions of Phase 01.1 (`../01.1-panel-shape-controls-and-indicator-lights/01.1-CONTEXT.md`) apply here: near-photographic replica, brand-mark replacement (GARMIN becomes GO-AROUND, DIAMOND becomes RUBIN, DA50RG stays), redraw only, straight-on close-ups supplied by the owner, instrument panel and centre console scope.

### Circuit breakers
- **D-01:** The **full set** of circuit breakers of the Essential, Main and Avionics bus exists at the real positions with legible labels (the right-hand bus panel). Showing only the scenario-relevant breaker would make the training trivial, so the whole grid is present.
- **D-02:** Circuit breakers are a **new control kind** with states **in** and **out**. All start **pushed in**. A **single tap toggles** in and out. Out shows a **white ring** (the pulled stem) and a small IN or OUT text for readability (D-13 of Phase 1: state never by colour alone).
- **D-03:** Circuit-breaker state is panel state in the store, so Phase 2 and later can grade pulling or resetting a breaker like any other control. Layout tests cover the full grid.

### G1000 NXi and avionics
- **D-04:** The **PFD and MFD** are drawn in the well-known G1000 NXi shape including **all softkeys**. Interactive: the **MFD page softkey** and the **engine page** (as in Phase 1); every other softkey and G1000 knob is visible but inert. No G1000 menu structures.
- **D-05:** The **audio panel** and **autopilot panel** are visible in their real positions with their real button layout; their buttons are **not clickable** for now.
- **D-06:** Static items such as the **backup EFIS** are visible in their real positions as artwork.

### Claude's Discretion
- How circuit-breaker rows and bus grouping are laid out in data (grid description versus individual positions), as long as positions are testable.
- Level of detail for avionics artwork, as long as shapes, button positions and labels are recognisable.

</decisions>
