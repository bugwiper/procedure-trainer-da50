### Region zoom and tap-size budget

Rule (01.1, `tests/zoom.test.ts`): `targetSide(def) x fitScale(zoomRect(region.bounds), profile) >= 44` on phone 734 x 315, tablet 944 x 628, desktop 1280 x 692, with `zoomRect` = bounds plus 10 each side. `targetSide` for the new kind is `min(hit.w, hit.h)`.

| Region | Hit (w x h) | Phone scale | Phone px | Tablet px | Desktop px |
|--------|-------------|-------------|----------|-----------|------------|
| `cb_essential` (view 158 x 123) | 18 x 20 | 2.561 | 46.1 | 18 x 5.11 = 92.0 | 18 x 5.63 = 101.3 |
| `cb_main` (view 158 x 125) | 18 x 20 | 2.520 | 45.4 | 90.4 | 99.6 |
| `cb_avionics` (view 158 x 125) | 18 x 20 | 2.520 | 45.4 | 90.4 | 99.6 |
| `cb_lower_row` (view 154 x 69) | 12.8 x 20 | 4.565 | 58.4 | 12.8 x 6.12 = 78.3 | 12.8 x 8.31 = 106 |
| `cb_flap_strip` (view 134 x 70) | 34 x 30 | 4.5 | 135 | 30 x 7.04 = 211 | 30 x 9.55 = 286 |

Slack on the phone is 1.4 px for `cb_main` and `cb_avionics`: do not enlarge a region and do not add anything to those regions (row pitch 54 to 58 and the label zone are the budget). A breaker region may be at most 105 tall for hit side 18.

**Portrait pinch guarantee (changed constant, U-02).** The existing test "a MIN_VIEW_WIDTH-wide view brings every control to 44 px" (393 x 631) needs every hit side at least 22.4 at `MIN_VIEW_WIDTH` 200. Breakers are 18 and 12.8 wide, so **`MIN_VIEW_WIDTH` in `viewport.ts` changes from 200 to 100** (scale 3.93 in portrait; 12.8 x 3.93 = 50 px). `tests/zoom.test.ts` and `e2e/zoom.spec.ts` follow the constant; pinch zoom may go twice as deep.

**Full view.** On the phone at full view a breaker hit is about 4.5 px wide (scale 0.242 x 18): taps there are unreliable and accidental pulls are possible. No gate is added (literal D-02, U-10); the phone answer is region zoom (01.1 D-04).

**Background clearance (PNL-09).** Row gaps give at least 17 units of free background in every grid region (row pitch 54 minus hit 20 is 34), the lower row has 22, the flap strip 13 (measured with the project's 48 x 48 sampling; region top 656 leaves 19 units above the hit rectangles; a region of 666 would fail the 7-unit rule). Hit rectangles stay inside the cell: hit width equals the column pitch only in the main grid (touching neighbours is allowed by the footprint test), never fill the row gap.

**Tolerances.** Reference rows for the 51 breakers use tolerance 8 and `review: true` (compressed pitch and unconfirmed outlines). Display, knob and key art is not a control and has no reference row; its positions are pinned by `tests/avionics.test.ts` (new) from the tables below.

### Kind, states and art

New registry kind `circuit-breaker`, id prefix `cb.`, flat ids (no bus segment: bus membership is uncertain and ids are permanent for Phase 2 YAML). Fields: `states` exactly `[in, out]` with labels `IN` and `OUT` (accessible name only), `default: 'in'` for all 51, `rating` (printed string, for example `5` or `7½`), `labelLines` (printed lines, data, never computed), `labelTier: 'a' | 'b'`, optional `labelDx` (shifts the label centre), `group: 'essential' | 'main' | 'avionics' | 'lower' | 'strip'`. **A single tap toggles** in and out; no confirmation, no animation (D-02, D-03). The state lives in the store and is logged as a user event like any control (grading is Phase 2).

### Labels, tiers and leaders

Real breakers print one label tier above the row. At pitch 18 that overlaps (labels are up to 32 wide), so labels alternate between two vertical tiers by column parity: odd columns (c1, c3, c5, c7) use tier `a`, even columns tier `b` (U-03). Rule for every printed line: baselines are measured from the breaker centre; the **last** line of a label sits at -10 (tier a) or -25 (tier b); each earlier line sits 7 higher (size-6 pitch). Tier `b` labels additionally draw a **leader line** (stroke `#5a606a` 0.6) from (0, -22.5) to (0, -(collar + 0.4)) so the label stays attached to its breaker; tier `a` labels sit directly above. Lines are centred on the breaker x (shifted by `labelDx` for the two labels that would leave the outline). Line breaks are data: label lines below are exactly what is printed. Lower row: odd positions j1, j3, j5, j7, j9 tier `a`, even positions tier `b`. Flap strip (pitch 36): all three tier `a`, no leaders.

Text boxes must not overlap (gate: pair overlap at most 0.5 square units) and must not touch a collar or a leader. If a pair collides in the real render, move the label to the other tier or split it into two lines; do not shrink below 6.
