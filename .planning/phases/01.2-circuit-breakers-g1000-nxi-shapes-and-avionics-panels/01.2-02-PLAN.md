Complete the panel replica so that nothing a pilot would find on the real panel is missing before scenarios start (ROADMAP phase 01.2, PNL-12 and PNL-13): every circuit breaker at its real position (pushed in by default, pulled out showing a white ring, a tap toggles), the PFD and MFD in the well-known glass shape with all softkeys, and the audio panel, autopilot panel and backup EFIS visible with inert buttons.

Purpose: D-02 (new control kind with states in and out, all start in, a single tap toggles, a pulled breaker shows a white ring) and D-03 (state is panel state in the store, logged like any control; layout tests cover the grid). Output: the kind in the registry, BreakerBody and the CircuitBreaker widget, cb.pitot in region cb_essential, derived inventory gates, an e2e breaker spec, amended ROADMAP and REQUIREMENTS wording.
</objective>

## Data tables

Table A1, the CircuitBreakerDef fields: kind `circuit-breaker`; `states` exactly `[{id:'in', label:'IN'}, {id:'out', label:'OUT'}]` (the labels are accessible names only); `default` `'in'`; `rating` (printed numeral string such as `5` or `7½`, non-empty); `labelLines` (printed label lines, data, never computed, non-empty array of non-empty strings); `group` one of `essential`, `main`, `avionics`, `lower`, `strip` (exported vocabulary BREAKER_GROUPS, re-checked at run time like the skin vocabularies); optional `compact` (true only for the 24 wide lower-row hits); optional `labelDx` (shifts the label centre in units, for a label that would touch an outline edge); the common fields id, label, region, x, y, hit. There is no label tier and no leader line on the wider layout. Invariants of `checkCircuitBreaker`: states ids exactly in then out, default in, rating non-empty after trim, labelLines non-empty and each line non-empty, the label lines joined and stripped of whitespace equal the label stripped of whitespace (guards a typo between the two), group inside its vocabulary, hit sides positive. The id prefix rule: `cb.`.
