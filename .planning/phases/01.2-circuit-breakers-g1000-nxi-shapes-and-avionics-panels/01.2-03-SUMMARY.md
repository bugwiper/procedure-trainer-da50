## Accomplishments

- **Regions and plates (data):** `CB_REGIONS` gained `cb_main` {1884, 368, 260, 166}, `cb_low` {1884, 648, 260, 56} and `cb_strip` {1441, 648, 118, 56}; the registry has 23 regions and none overlaps another. `plates.ts` has the `bus-panel` plate (rx 8) and `strip-flaps` now names flaps, right_panel and cb_strip.
- **Art as data:** `bus-boxes.ts` holds the three outline vertex lists (keyed by `BusId`), the titles, the pump boxes (EMERG, MAIN, XFR), the sub-panel and the plugs. No UI import.
- **Art component:** `BusPanelArt` draws, in the order plate, sub-panel, outlines, pump boxes, plugs with labels, titles. Outlines are closed paths from `roundedPolygonPath` (one move command and one quadratic corner per vertex, radius 4); shared edges coincide exactly because the corner curves at shared vertices are the same curve. Titles carry a plate-colour stroke with paint order stroke so they cut the edge. All shapes are `pointer-events none` through the shell root; no handler, hit or role.
- **Shell:** the old narrow bus rectangle and the three bracket components are gone; the shell draws the plate from PLATES via `Plate` and passes it into `BusPanelArt`. `bus-panel` plate fill added to the shell fill table.
- **Tests:** `tests/bus-boxes.test.ts` (outline literals, axis-parallel edges, vertices inside the plate, title on its own edge, pump boxes, sub-panel containment of cb_low, plugs inside their outlines with a full-disc clearance); layout cases for the four regions, the plate rectangle, strip-flaps and the cb.pitot footprint inside the bus plate; shell cases for the bus layer (plate, outline paths, titles, inert plugs, pump boxes, sub-panel, draw order, label baselines, ADF / DATA / LINK once each, old rectangle gone, rounded-path helper). `BusPanelArt.tsx` joined the font-size scan (sizes 6 and 8 only). `ALL_REGION_IDS` and `FRONT_PANEL_REGIONS` carry the four breaker regions.
- **Documents:** README count sentence now reads "42 controls and 13 indicator lamps in 23 regions" and lists the bus panel frame; `docs/PANEL-REVIEW.md` describes the new geometry, replaces the narrow-bus statements and the Shell table row, and gains P2-04 and P2-05 (the review test now requires them and the number 1874); `docs/PANEL-LABELS.md` has a "Bus panel static text" section.
