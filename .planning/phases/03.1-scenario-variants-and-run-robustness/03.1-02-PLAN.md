## Decisions (assumptions taken in planning; the owner may veto any of them)

- **V-09** The re-trip is aircraft physics, not scenario data: a `trips` list in the physics spec (control, state the sim sets, delay, condition on controls and parameters) and the parameter `alt1_short` (0 normal, 1 = alternator 1 has a short). The ALT 1 breaker is set out 2 s after it is in while `alt1_short` is above 0 (2 s is an instructor-review value). The delay counts from the step that applied the push; leaving `in` resets it; the pop arms no reaction and is a sim event (V-06 of plan 01).
- **V-10** `alternator_shorted` (breaker out, alternator dead, short, weight 15) and `breaker_popped_dead` (breaker out, alternator dead, no short, weight 15) require ALTERNATOR 1 off and ESS BUS on, like `alternator_dead`. Pushing the breaker in is neither required nor harmful in them (the end-state rule could not credit a breaker the simulation pops again, and a sound pilot tries it).
- **V-11** The per-variant replay tests live in tests/variants.test.ts as one table; the ordered copy for the wrong-order case is the shipped file with `ordered: false` replaced by an ordered, order-critical group. recordings.json keeps its 23 goldens.
- **V-12** e2e force the draw with the plan 01 helper `forceVariant`; touch projects zoom into the breaker's region first, as e2e/breaker.spec.ts does.
