## Decisions (assumptions taken in planning; the owner may veto any of them)
Owner decisions D-06 to D-08 and D-10 of `08-CONTEXT.md` apply.
- **L-01 Icon art.** Glass-black ground `#0b0d10`, a rounded bezel outline in the panel's bezel grey `#8a9099`, "DA50RG" large in `#e6e9ed`, "GO-AROUND" small under it in the bezel grey. Colours are read from `src/ui/panel/PanelShell.tsx` and `src/ui/stage.css`, not invented. The maskable icon keeps the whole art inside the central circle of 80 percent of the side (the platform may crop the rest). The apple icon is opaque and full bleed (iOS rounds it itself). The owner may replace the PNGs with other art at any time; the tests only check format and size.
- **L-02 Generator.** `scripts/make-icons.mjs` renders an SVG in the Playwright Chromium (already a dev dependency) with the B612 bold woff2 of `@fontsource/b612` embedded as a data URL inside the generated page (the data URL lives in the throwaway page, not in `src/`, so the brand test on `src/` is unaffected), waits for the font, takes one screenshot per size, and for the maskable icon checks by pixel read in the page that nothing but the ground colour lies outside the safe circle. No new package.
