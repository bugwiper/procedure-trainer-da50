## Decisions (assumptions taken in planning; the owner may veto any of them)
Checklist text is reproduced as printed (owner decision 2026-10-23); the sources are the private screenshots in `.input/` (named in each task, AFM pages) and, as a check, the text of `.input/DA50RG_AFM.pdf` pages 03-41 to 03-49 (pdftotext into a scratch folder, never committed). Everything else is own wording. Owner answers of 2026-10-27 are built in (S-01, S-04, S-07).
- **S-01** Source block as in the other scenarios (owner 2026-10-27: the booklet, edition 4.1, says the same as the AFM pages of the screenshots): document "DA50 C / RG Emergency Procedures checklist", issue "Edition 4.1, 2022-10-05", verified "2026-10-27", section in the AFM numbering with the AFM's own title in capitals: AFM 03-29 ENGINE FIRE ON GROUND, AFM 03-30 ELECTRICAL FIRE WITH SMOKE ON GROUND, AFM 03-31 IF TAKE-OFF CAN BE ABORTED, AFM 03-32 IF TAKE-OFF CANNOT BE ABORTED, AFM 03-34 ELECTRICAL FIRE WITH SMOKE IN FLIGHT, AFM 03-35 UNINTENTIONAL FLIGHT INTO ICING CONDITIONS (read from the AFM table of contents and page headers; the screenshots show no section number). ASSUMPTION for the owner: the booklet's row marks may differ (numbers, as in Engine Fire in Flight); the marks are plain data, so swapping them is one edit of the `number` fields per file plus the typed table in tests/checklist-reference.ts (L16 accepts either style). Each file says so in its header comment. The shipped Engine Fire in Flight (booklet) is not changed.
- **S-02** Row marks: `number` takes a whole number 1 to 99 or one capital letter A to Z; one checklist uses one style (L16 checks the run A, B, C). Heading = the printed words without the AFM structure numeral and without the page title line.
- **S-03** Comment rows get an optional `style`: `subheading` (italic bold row, such as After Standstill), `warning` and `caution` (block with its printed word above the text, up to 70 words). Rows under a sub-heading stay flat (no indent). The table form keeps no branch: conditions are graded by variants (S-05).
- **S-04** WARNING and CAUTION blocks are reproduced verbatim as printed (owner 2026-10-27: checklist text exactly as printed per the decision of 2026-10-23, copyright is no concern, the AFM is public). Task 1 rewords section 2(b) of `docs/CONTENT-POLICY.md` so that it allows the WARNING and CAUTION blocks that stand inside a checklist and keeps the other limits (NOTE prose, figures, charts, performance tables, page images, continuous manual prose, private files).
- **S-05** Electrical Fire on Ground has two variants: `engine_running` (weight 70, the steps A to E apply: shutdown steps, then the door once the engine has stopped) and `engine_stopped` (weight 30, ENGINE MASTER off at the start, only the ELECT. MASTER and Door steps apply). The second printed sub-heading "When the Engine Has Stopped" is shown as printed.
- **S-06** Take-off is TWO scenarios, not variants: the printed pages are two checklists, a variant cannot change the scene or the start speed, and an open branch (`decided_at: run`) would grade nothing. Abort: roll at 40 kt, ground_roll 1, full power. No abort: airborne over the runway, ground_roll 0 (the speed holds at 70 kt, the roll scene keeps moving), scene takeoff_roll (no new art).
- **S-07** The printed rows "Door open" and "Doors open" are graded panel steps (owner 2026-10-27, replaces the earlier no-door decision): done is state `open` of `lv.door_release` (plan 01, P-09), the control starts locked so a do-nothing run never credits it (L5). Severity: critical in the three ground scenarios, where the After Standstill rows end the checklist; optional in the flight scenarios (the no-abort take-off here, Electrical Fire in Flight in plan 03) because the simulation has no landing, the aircraft never stands still, and the verdict must not demand an open door in the air. The door is credited whenever it is opened, not only at standstill (question for the owner). Only the rows without a panel action stay `line` rows, not graded: "Airplane evacuate immediately", the short-cut circuit row and the landing rows. GI is the lever position p0 and "OFF (lift and turn)" is the state off of `sw.fuel_valve` (owner answers, the lift is not modelled); both are printed as is.
- **S-08** New pack `fire` (title Fire and smoke), sorted after `engine`, so the four existing situations keep page 1 of the chooser. Scenario titles (at most 18 characters): ENGINE FIRE GROUND, ELEC FIRE GROUND, T/O FIRE ABORT, T/O FIRE NO ABORT.
- **S-09** Smoke: the source is live only while ELECT. MASTER is on; clearing runs 1 times, plus 1 with CIRCULATING AIR at fresh, plus 1 with the window open (at most 3 times faster). The three engine-fire scenarios copy the Engine Fire constants (forbidden emergency pump, outcome fire_spreads at 85 held 20 s, onset 2 to 6 s); the electrical ones use the outcome cabin_smoke_full (smoke_density 90 held 30 s) and no forbidden item.
