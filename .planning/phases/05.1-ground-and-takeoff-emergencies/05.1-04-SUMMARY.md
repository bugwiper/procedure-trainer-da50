# Phase 5.1 Plan 04: the fire and icing checklists follow the booklet edition 4.1 Summary

Direct rework (no PLAN, owner decision 2026-10-28: the booklet is binding). The six scenarios of the packs `fire` and `icing` now carry the booklet pages as printed: number row marks, booklet wording, plain comment rows, the boxed EMERGENCY LANDING (ENGINE OFF) (page 9) and the centred Evacuate; the AFM sub-headings and WARNING and CAUTION blocks are gone. Source blocks name "Page 16/17/18/23" of edition 4.1, `verified: 2026-10-28`.

## What each scenario says now

- **Engine Fire on Ground (p16):** 1 Engine master OFF, 2 Fuel valve OFF, 3 Electric master OFF; "After standstill and when engine stopped:"; 4 Doors OPEN; Evacuate.
- **Electric Fire / Smoke on Ground (p16):** ONE list: 1 Electric master OFF, 2 Power lever GROUND IDLE (GI), 3 Fuel valve OFF, 4 Engine master OFF; comment; 5 Doors OPEN; Evacuate. Variants removed, engine runs at the start, no cause line in the debrief.
- **Fire / Smoke During Aborted TKOF (p16):** 1 Power lever GI, 2 ECS shut-off PULL, 3 Brakes APPLY, 4 Fuel valve OFF, 5 Engine master OFF, 6 Electric master OFF; comment; 7 Doors OPEN; Evacuate.
- **Fire / Smoke During Continued TKOF (p17):** 1 ECS shut-off PULL; "If possible climb to safe height and land ASAP"; "When landing assured:"; 2 Engine master, 3 Fuel valve, 4 Electric master OFF; 5 Emergency front windows OPEN as necessary (optional); "Carry out:" and the boxed reference; comment; 6 Doors OPEN (optional); Evacuate.
- **Electric Fire with Smoke in Flight (p18):** "If Oxygen System is installed and flight performed above 10,000ft:"; 1 ** Oxygen System OFF, 2 ** Emergency Descent INITIATE (display rows); "Continue:"; 3 Avionic master, 4 Electric master OFF; 5 ECS - circulating air FRESH AIR; 6 ECS shut-off PULL (all critical); 7 Emergency front windows (optional); "Land immediately"; "Consider and be prepared for:", boxed reference; comment; 8 Doors OPEN (optional); Evacuate.
- **Unintentional Flight Into Icing (p23):** heading line comment, the INADVERTENT ICING ENCOUNTER & EXCESSIVE ICE ACCUMULATION title, rows 1 to 10 with the * markers, the footnote, then ICE PROTECTION FAILURE (its six rows and the table) as display rows.

## Grading changes

- Electric Fire / Smoke on Ground: the five rows are always asked for (the "engine stopped" variant that asked for only two is gone). Step ids unchanged.
- Fire / Smoke During Aborted TKOF: row order only (Fuel valve before Engine master); groups stay unordered, so the grade is the same as before.
- Icing: new steps `deice_high` (DE-ICE MODE HIGH, critical), `prop_deice_on` (critical), `ice_light_on` (ICE LIGHT position, optional); `alt_static_open` dropped (not in the booklet), the initial `lv.alt_static` entry dropped; row 9 (WIND SHIELD press) and the whole ICE PROTECTION FAILURE checklist are display rows.
- Not gradable by state: the MAX press of row 1 and the WIND SHIELD press of row 9 are momentary buttons that leave no state behind (grading reads the state after the last event). Row 1 is graded on the mode toggle only.
- Electric Fire in Flight and the continued take-off: same step ids and severities.
