- **P-01** The toe brakes are ONE control `lv.toe_brakes` (detent-lever, tapToggle, detents released and applied, ids with the existing `lv.` prefix because the label table accepts only the known prefixes), drawn as two pedals with a toe pad each under one footprint (accepted by the owner for now). Two pedal controls would need an "all of" predicate in schema, lint and grading; the parking brake `lv.parking_brake` stays separate.
- **P-02** Pedals and plate sit at the floor of the pilot's footwell: region `pedals` x 300, y 1150, 360 x 110, plate `pedals-pad` x 280, y 1130, 400 x 150, centre of the control at global 480, 1205, hit 260 x 90 (not wider: the background gate of tests/layout.test.ts needs a point of the region at least 7 units clear of every footprint inside the 10 percent inset, and a 300 wide hit would cover the whole sample grid). Below the old transponder plate, so the transponder can move in task 2 without a clash.
- **P-03** Ground speed: hidden quantity `ground_speed_kt` (0 to 100, base 0), parameter `ground_roll` (0 or 1, normal 0). Only a scenario that sets it gets the model; every other run holds the speed it starts with, so the cruise scenarios and every recording stay bit for bit. On Realistic the parameter acts from the onset, so the speed holds for the first 2 to 6 s.
- **P-04** The roll scene follows the speed through a new cue `roll_speed` (0 to 60 kt, full rate from 60). The playback rate changes continuously (Animation.updatePlaybackRate) so the marks never jump; rate 0 is the stopped scene. Reduced motion keeps no animation as before.
