# Phase 5.1 Plan 02: AFM-form checklists and the fire and take-off situations Summary

The briefing and the debrief print the AFM page as it is (letters A, B, C, italic sub-headings, the WARNING block), and four ground and take-off fire situations can be flown on their scenes, graded by control state, with a PFD airspeed that follows the take-off roll.

## What changed

- Task 1 (tracer, 7cefc22): schema (`MAX_NOTE_WORDS`, letter marks, comment style), lint L16 (letters, one slip one issue) and L17, view model and renderer, CONTENT-POLICY 2(b) allows WARNING and CAUTION blocks; pack `fire` with Engine Fire on Ground; CHOOSE SITUATION helpers and specs page through the list.
- Task 2 (ac07fb8): smoke physics; Electrical Fire with Smoke on Ground, variants `engine_running` (70) and `engine_stopped` (30).
- Task 3 (14d99a7): two take-off scenarios; PFD airspeed (owner addition); docs/GRADING.md.

## Deviations from Plan

- **[Rule 1 - Bug] Outcome threshold 95, not 90** (elec-fire-ground): `tests/blueprint.test.ts` forbids an outcome value as a number in engine code and 90 is a degree constant in `src/engine/rotary.ts`. At 95 with a hold of 30 s a do-nothing run still ends within about 70 s.
- **[Rule 1 - Bug] Take-off onset.quantities hold only the speeds**, not `fire_intensity` as well: the lint (L23) rejects a flame cue that is already visible at the Realistic start. The fire begins at the onset, the speed is there from t 0.
- **PFD airspeed through a new hidden quantity `airspeed_kt`** instead of reading `ground_speed_kt` in the frame: the blueprint gate bans the parameter id `ground_roll` in `src/ui`, and a stopped aircraft must read 0 and not jump to 140. The rule is in aircraft data: moving means `ground_roll` above 0 or a ground speed above 0, so the airborne no-abort scenario (no `ground_roll`) also reads its 70 knots. The take-off scenarios list `airspeed_kt` next to the speed under `initial` and `onset` so the first frame is right.
- The printed gap in "If  the Engine Is Running" is a single space in the file; the screenshot shows two. The letters A to Z are assumptions about the booklet; each file says so in its header comment.
- The Electrical Fire scenario keeps the registry default CIRCULATING AIR (fresh), so its clearing already runs at 2 times and only the window adds more.
- `e2e/scenario.spec.ts` and `e2e/pack.spec.ts` now expect one page of the list and page through it; `chooseScenario` turns NEXT PAGE until the row shows.
