# Phase 6.1: PFD fidelity - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-01
**Status:** Ready for execution (three plans, lean form per `.planning/WORKFLOW.md`)

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

The PFD looks and reads like the real one, after the owner compared a photo and screenshot of the real display with ours (2026-11-01). The whole glass is too coarse. Scope: the CAS messages as one stacked column (the most important point for the trainer), the simplified outside view as synthetic vision under the whole glass, translucent speed and altitude tapes, a narrow vertical speed scale with a black pointer, the barometer setting in hPa, a plain compass. Message texts, levels, the rule table and the grading do not change. The PFD stays static on the free panel (the synthetic vision is static there too); it is live inside a situation as since Phase 6.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Scope and order
- **D-01:** Plan order: 06.1-01 CAS column (tracer, the owner's priority), 06.1-02 synthetic vision with translucent tapes and the compass backing, 06.1-03 vertical speed scale, QNH in hPa, phase-end documents and gates. Three plans, no research, no plan-checker.
- **D-02 (not needed, stays out):** GPS and DPRT boxes, HDG and DTK boxes, NAV/DME box, wind box, timer, a lateral deviation bar above the compass, a map inside the compass, a live heading. What exists stays as it is (heading box with HDG, the 12-tick compass arc, the bank scale, the empty inset map frame, the KT and FT labels, the glass header, the legend row); nothing of this list is added.

### CAS column (PNL-25)
- **D-03 (owner):** The messages stand in one vertical column at the lower right of the glass content zone, right of the altitude tape. Rows are joined without a gap (adjacent black rectangles, no outline between). The bottom edge is fixed; each further message makes the column grow upward.
- **D-04 (owner recommendation, locked unless contradicted):** Order from top to bottom is the severity order of the rule table: warning (red text) first, then caution (yellow), then advisory (white) at the bottom. Within a level the existing table order stays (`active()` in `src/engine/cas.ts` is unchanged); a newer message of equal level is not placed below older ones. The column draws the `bridge.cas` list in list order, top row first; no sorting in the UI.
- **D-05 (owner):** About half as wide and much finer than before: the chip was 100 wide and 20 high in bold 10 type. Target about 52 wide, about 9 high, type size 6 (the smallest size of the declared glass scale 6, 8, 10, 14), regular weight, black fill, level colour only in the text (colours unchanged: `#f0443c`, `#f2c230`, `#ffffff`). The panel stylesheet sets `font-weight: 700` on every panel text and a CSS rule beats the SVG attribute, so a scoped CSS rule for the chip text is part of the plan. The longest text (12 characters) must fit inside its row with room to spare. Message texts and the six-message limit (`MAX_CAS_CHIPS`, lint) are unchanged.
- **D-06:** Test hooks stay: `data-testid="cas-chips"` and `cas-chip`, `data-cas-id`, `data-level`, the aria label `Warning|Caution|Advisory: TEXT`; the DOM order equals the display order (top to bottom). No flashing, animation or sound is added (the aural rules are not touched).

### Synthetic vision (PNL-26)
- **D-07 (owner):** The windscreen scene is the synthetic vision under the whole glass content zone (x 4 to 491, y 30 to 343 of the PFD region); the header strip and the legend row stay opaque on top of it. It is drawn in the existing horizon group, so pitch and bank (`pitch_deg`, `bank_deg`) move and turn it; the transform `rotate(minus bank, cx, cy) translate(0, shift)` keeps its form. The scene id is the session scene (`session.scene`, the `?scene=` flag on the free panel, the default scene without a session).
- **D-08 (planner, owner may veto):** It reuses the scene ids and the colours of the windscreen layer, not its scene components (those are drawn for the 2180 wide opening with a hangar, clouds and scrolling marks, which do not suit under the tapes). The gradient colours come from one table that the windscreen `SceneDefs` also reads, so there is a single palette. Per scene: `cruise` sky and terrain with a few perspective field patches; `cloud` the same as `cruise` (synthetic vision shows the terrain whatever the weather, and the horizon stays readable in the icing situation); `parked` sky, apron and the yellow taxi line; `takeoff_roll` sky, grass, runway with white edge lines and a static dashed centre line. No text, no hangar, no clouds, no scrolling.
- **D-09 (owner):** The speed and altitude tapes lie translucent over the synthetic vision: dark glass fill at about 55 per cent opacity, one constant; ticks and the white-framed black readout boxes stay opaque and readable.
- **D-10:** The compass stays the plain 12-tick half rose with the heading box (as now). It gets a translucent dark half-disc behind it so that it reads on the synthetic vision. A guard test keeps the PFD free of a deviation bar and of a map (no magenta paint, no deviation or map element; the inset map frame stays an empty frame).

### Vertical speed scale and QNH (PNL-27)
- **D-11 (owner):** A narrow vertical strip right next to the altitude tape replaces the VS box. Linear scale of plus and minus 2000 fpm (the range of the quantity `vertical_speed_fpm`), zero at the height of the attitude centre, a tick every 500 fpm, the static numerals 1 and 2 (thousand fpm) at plus and minus 1000 and 2000, a black pointer (arrow with a thin light outline so that it reads on any background) that follows the quantity and is clamped at the ends. The digital VS number and `data-vs-readout` are removed; tests read the pointer from its transform. A nonlinear scale and a digital number are an owner option (see Deferred).
- **D-12 (owner, decided by the planner):** The barometer setting stands under the altitude tape in turquoise (the turquoise of the glass header): the value in whole hPa and the small unit label HPA, replacing the old baro box. The value is a new static display quantity `qnh_hpa` (hPa, range 950 to 1050, base 1013, one unmarked band, no model, like oxygen and rudder trim). That makes "a situation may set it" free: a scenario can lay it into its start quantities through the existing mechanism and the free panel shows `?q=qnh_hpa:1021`; no shipped scenario sets it and no lint rule is added. Display only: the altitude is not corrected by it.
- **D-13 (G-0):** All new fixed words are static: HPA, and the numerals 1 and 2 of the scale. Numbers that follow quantities (airspeed, altitude, QNH) change by value, never by control state. The new words go into `docs/PANEL-LABELS.md`.

### Layout, tests and verification
- **D-14:** The PFD geometry that the gates need lives in data, `src/aircraft/da50rg/pfd-layout.ts` (content zone, tapes and readout boxes, vertical speed strip, QNH position, CAS column, compass backing, inset map), with an independent hand-written reference `tests/pfd-layout-reference.ts` and the gate `tests/pfd-layout.test.ts` (every rectangle inside the content zone with a margin, no overlap between the furniture, counts of ticks and numerals derived from the reference). A moved part is an edit of both files. Plan 01 creates the module with the zone, the altitude block and the CAS column; plans 02 and 03 extend it.
- **D-15:** Existing specs about the PFD and the chips are updated honestly: `casChipSlots` and its cases in `tests/cas.test.ts` and `tests/avionics.test.ts` go with the slot grid, Table D5 in `tests/avionics.test.ts` follows the moved parts, `tests/pfd-live.test.ts` and `e2e/pfd.spec.ts` stop reading `data-vs-readout`. `e2e/pfd.spec.ts` horizon cases and `e2e/heat.spec.ts`, `fuel.spec.ts`, `icing.spec.ts` chip cases keep their hooks.
- **D-16 (visual check, every plan):** Look at the result, do not trust the numbers. Scratch Playwright spec (never committed, deleted before the commit; the browser pane of the owner is never driven): `e2e/zz-pfd-shots.spec.ts` with `test.use` of a 2200 x 1400 viewport and a device scale factor of 3, desktop-chromium only, that opens the free panel with preview flags (`?scene=<id>&q=pitch_deg:8,bank_deg:20,vertical_speed_fpm:1200,...`, values taken from the quantity data in node), clicks the PITOT/STALL HEAT rocker for chips, and saves `page.locator('[data-region-id="pfd"]').screenshot({ path })` to the session scratchpad directory (outside the repo). Run it with `PW_PORT=<free port> npx playwright test e2e/zz-pfd-shots.spec.ts --project=desktop-chromium` and open the PNGs with the Read tool. Cases: each of the four scenes level, plus pitch up 10 with bank 20 and pitch down 8 with bank -30; 0, 1, 3 and 6 messages (warning, caution and advisory together). Judge against the owner description (stacked fine column at the lower right, scene visible under translucent tapes, compass readable, pointer readable).
- **D-17 (gates):** Targeted vitest and the touched Playwright specs on desktop-chromium and phone-chromium after each plan; at the end of plan 03 only: `rm -rf node_modules/.vitest-cache` and the whole vitest, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint`, `npm run format:check`, `npm run validate`, and Playwright on all seven projects (delegated to a subagent, result verified by the caller). `tests/fixtures/recordings.json` stays unchanged; `.planning/config.json` and `state.json` are not touched; nothing is pushed.
- **D-18 (documents):** `docs/PANEL-REVIEW.md` gets the section "Phase 6.1: owner review" as the new last section (plan 03; `tests/panel-review.test.ts` pins the heading order and is updated), `docs/PANEL-LABELS.md` the new static words, and the sentences that became false (PANEL-REVIEW U-15 row, Phase 6 items 4 and 16, the Phase 6 PFD line of `.claude/CLAUDE.md`) are corrected in place. `docs/GRADING.md` and the README only if a sentence is now wrong (the CAS display order is not a grading rule).

### Claude's Discretion
