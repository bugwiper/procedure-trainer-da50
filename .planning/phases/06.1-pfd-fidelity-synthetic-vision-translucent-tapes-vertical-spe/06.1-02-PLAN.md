## Decisions (assumptions taken in planning; the owner may veto any of them)
Owner decisions D-01 to D-02, D-07 to D-10 and D-14 to D-18 of `06.1-CONTEXT.md` apply. The PFD stays static on the free panel; the view is static there too (default scene `cruise`, the flag `?scene=` changes it). Plan 01 has created `pfd-layout.ts` (zone, altitude block, CAS column) and the reference table.
- **S-01 (D-07) Scene source.** `PfdFrame` reads `session?.scene.value` (the `FrameProps` already carry the optional session; the signal read in the render subscribes, so a scene change redraws) and falls back to `DEFAULT_SCENE` without a session or for an id the art does not know (`isSceneId`, the same safety net as the windscreen layer).
- **S-02 (D-08) One palette.** `src/ui/panel/windscreen/scenes.tsx` exports its gradient colours as one table `SCENE_GRADIENTS` (gradient name to top, middle or none, bottom) and the field colours; `SceneDefs` is rewritten to read the table. The vnode it returns must be unchanged: `tests/windscreen.test.ts` is the gate. The synthetic vision reads the same table, so a change of the palette changes both.
- **S-03 (D-08) The component.** `src/ui/panel/frames/SyntheticVision.tsx` is a pure function component (no hooks: `tests/vnode-walk.ts` cannot expand hooks) with props scene id, centre x, centre y and reach. It draws in horizon-local coordinates, the horizon line at y = centre: a sky rectangle and a ground rectangle with linear gradients in user space (ids prefixed `pfd-svt-`, defined in the component's own defs), a thin haze band on the horizon, and the scene art from `SVT_STYLES`, a record typed by the `SceneId` of the aircraft data (a missing scene fails the typecheck, like `SCENES`). Root group carries `data-svt` and `data-svt-scene` with the id that was drawn. Flat vector art, no text, no animation. Per scene: `cruise` and `cloud` terrain with a few perspective field patches in the windscreen field colours (every third patch left out, converging on the horizon centre, built with `+ - * /` from a short row and fan table); `parked` the apron gradient and the yellow taxi line as a wedge to the centre; `takeoff_roll` the grass gradient, a runway trapezoid in the runway gradient with two white edge lines and a static dashed centre line (dashes as trapezoids that shrink with depth).
- **S-04 Horizon group and clip.** The two flat rectangles of the horizon group are replaced by the component; the white horizon line and the pitch ladder stay; the transform string keeps its exact form `rotate(a cx cy) translate(0 shift)` because `e2e/pfd.spec.ts` reads it. The clip rectangle becomes the whole content zone and stays on the parent group so that it is the nearest clipped ancestor of every view element (the zone gate of `e2e/glass.spec.ts` cuts boxes to it). The reach is 480: the half diagonal of the zone about the centre (about 300) plus the largest pitch shift (30 degrees, 132 units) with the bank turn is under 440. The old thin frame round the attitude area goes. Bank scale, roll pointer and the yellow aircraft symbol stay.
- **S-05 (D-09) Translucent tapes.** `TAPE_OPACITY` 0.55 in the layout data, applied as fill opacity of the dark glass fill `#12181e` of the speed and altitude tapes (the stroke stays). The readout boxes keep their black fill and white frame. If a tick or the readout is hard to read on a scene in the screenshot review, change the constant (data and reference together), do not add a second layer.
- **S-06 Layout data.** `pfd-layout.ts` gains the attitude centre (247.5, 150), the speed and altitude tape rectangles and their y range, the readout boxes (centred on the attitude centre height), the KT and FT label anchors, the compass (centre 247.5, 338, radius 66) with its backing radius, the heading box and the inset map frame, all at today's numbers except those plan 01 moved; `PfdFrame` reads them in place of its inline constants (bank scale radius and the pitch ladder stay where they are). The reference table grows by the same items, written by hand.
- **S-07 (D-10) Compass backing.** A half disc of radius about 70 about the compass centre, dark glass (`#080b0e`) at about 50 per cent opacity, drawn under the ticks and the heading box. A guard test (no magenta paint on the PFD by hue, no element with a deviation or map marker, the inset map frame has no children) keeps D-02 in force.
- **Owner review points** (plan 03 collects them): the look of the four views and their horizon haze; cloud shows terrain; the opacity of the tapes; the compass backing; the aircraft symbol on the new background; the static dashes on the take-off runway.

<objective>
Lay the simplified outside view of the windscreen under the whole PFD as synthetic vision that follows pitch and bank, make the speed and altitude tapes translucent over it, and give the compass a translucent backing.
Purpose: the PFD reads like the real one and the attitude is readable in every situation, including the icing cloud.
Output: `SyntheticVision.tsx`, a shared palette table, the layout data and reference extended, and screenshot-checked art for the four scenes.
</objective>
