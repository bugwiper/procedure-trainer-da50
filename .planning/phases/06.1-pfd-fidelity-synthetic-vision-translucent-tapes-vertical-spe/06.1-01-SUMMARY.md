# Phase 6.1 Plan 01: CAS column Summary

The active CAS messages now stand in one thin column at the lower right of the PFD: black rows of 52 x 9 units joined without a gap, regular type of size 6 in the level colour, the bottom edge fixed so each further message grows the column upward, warnings on top, then cautions, then advisories (the order of the bridge list, nothing is sorted in the UI).

## Accomplishments

- **Task 1 (tracer), e4aceac:** `pfd-layout.ts` (`PFD_ZONE`, `ALTITUDE_BLOCK`, `CAS_COLUMN`, pure `casColumnRows(count)` with `+ - * /` and clamping). `CasChips` redrawn from it (the slot grid `casChipSlots` is gone); no corner radius, no stroke, black fill; hooks kept (`cas-chips`, `cas-chip`, `data-cas-id`, `data-level`, aria label). Scoped rule `.panel-svg [data-testid='cas-chips'] text` in `stage.css` (weight 400, letter spacing 0.02em) beats the bold panel default. The altitude block moved 50 units left through `ALTITUDE_BLOCK.x` (tape 355 to 435, readout, FT label, baro box and VS box with the old offsets); the chips are drawn last in the content group. New `e2e/cas-column.spec.ts` first case: PITOT HEAT then STALL HEAT touch, same x and width, below 60 x 11.
- **Task 2, 7f78c20:** `tests/pfd-layout-reference.ts` (hand-written: zone, speed tape, moved altitude block rectangles, compass box, heading frame, inset map, `CAS_REF` with exact values and the "about half" ranges, old chip 100 x 20 as history) and `tests/pfd-layout.test.ts` (data equals reference, the altitude block as drawn, a full column of six rows inside the zone with margin 2 and overlapping nothing, a self-test of the overlap helper). `data-baro-box` and `data-vs-box` markers added in `PfdFrame` (plan 03 removes both boxes with them). `e2e/cas-column.spec.ts`: order warning, caution, caution, advisory; bottom edge fixed while the top rises one row per message (1, 3, 4 messages); text fit with six messages (desktop-chromium only). `e2e/glass.spec.ts`: PFD zone gate with six messages. `tests/cas.test.ts`: casColumnRows cases, the real rule table lists the levels in display order and `CasChips` draws that list unsorted.
