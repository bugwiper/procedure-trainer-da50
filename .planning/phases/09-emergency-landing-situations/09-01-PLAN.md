## Decisions (assumptions taken in planning; the owner may veto any of them)
Phase decisions D-05 to D-09, D-14 and D-15 of `09-CONTEXT.md` apply. Planner details:
- **P-01 One growth law.** `fieldGeometry(level)` returns, for a level 0 to 1, the near and far edge offsets below the horizon as fractions of the visible ground depth and the half widths at those edges as fractions of the half width the ground has at the same row; both renderers project it with their own perspective helpers. Level 0 is a thin speck at the horizon, level 1 puts the near edge below the picture. The numbers are art values tuned from screenshots.
- **P-02 Shared art.** The sky, the hills and the field patches of the cruise scene are reused by the new scene by extracting them into small components only when the cruise markup stays identical (the existing windscreen specs prove it); otherwise the few lines are repeated. The field palette is one exported table read by both renderers.
- **P-03 Fixture.** `landing-fixture.toon` is synthetic (own words, header marks it as a test fixture and says its rows are not printed text), under `tests/fixtures/scenarios/` only. It uses the powered regime with a descent so this plan needs no physics change.
