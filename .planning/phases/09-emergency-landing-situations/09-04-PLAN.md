## Decisions (planner choices; the owner may veto any of them)
The owner's rework of 2026-11-03 is in `09-CONTEXT.md` as R-01 to R-09 and is binding. D-02 (Realistic not offered), D-03 (single target) and D-04 (routes) of that file are superseded where R-04, R-05 and R-07 say so. Three tasks on purpose: the machinery first (it is the riskier change and every reader of `follow_on` moves with it), then the printed situation with its view, then the wiring, the e2e flows, the documents and the gates.
- **P-01 End state of 09-03.** This plan is written against the tree as 09-03 leaves it: `scenarios/landing/` holds `pack.toon` and `landing-engine-off.toon` only (no engine-power landing: page 10 is the ditching page), the catalog has the entry EMERGENCY LANDING (ENGINE OFF) first in its group, the two fire sources name `landing-engine-off` in the single-object form of 09-02, ELEC FIRE FLIGHT accepts `conduct_precautionary` and names no follow-on, and `e2e/landing.spec.ts`, `tests/shipped-landing.test.ts` and `tests/shipped-further.test.ts` read `follow_on` values as objects. Before the first edit run `git log --oneline -8`, `git status --short` and read these files; where they differ (a second landing file, a changed group order), adapt the edit, keep the intent, and say so in the SUMMARY.
- **P-02 One shape, no second form.** `follow_on.<conduct>` becomes a list; the 09-02 object form is replaced everywhere (schema, two shipped files, fixture, README fragment, tests, the wire patch of `e2e/follow-on.spec.ts`), never accepted next to the list: it was never published and one shape keeps every reader simple. Weight is required (like a variant weight), 0 to 100, so the owner's lint rule "sum above 0" has meaning.
- **P-03 Where the draw lives.** `src/engine/follow-on.ts` (new) holds `followOnFromSeed`; `variants.ts` stays untouched so every variant draw stays bit-identical. The fallback of a rounding edge is the last target of positive weight; an empty list or a zero sum throws RangeError (L33 prevents it; the session turns it into the existing load failure of reason invalid, never a silent default).
- **P-04 Water values and the engine-out start (instructor review).** The owner's correction of 2026-11-03: a ditching is almost always without engine power, so the file starts like `landing-engine-off` of 09-03 and `landing-fixture-off` of 09-02: profile `engine_stopped` 1 and the glide regime, rpm, load, oil pressure, fuel flow and fuel pressure 0 in the initial quantities, the messages the lint reports listed under `annunciations`; the situation text may say the engine is out. `WATER_LEVEL_FT` 0 (sea level), `WATER_SIGHT_FT` 600, start 450 ft with `glide_speed_kt` 77 (the printed Vref) and `glide_vs_fpm` -300 (the rate of row 10). The glide parameters are constants and cannot vary with height, so minus 300 applies from the start to the water; a faster early descent with 300 ft/min only near the water would need a height-dependent rate, which is not built (noted for the owner as an instructor-review value). A do-nothing run reaches the water in about 90 s, the water already shows waves at the start (cue level above 0.2) and nothing waits. If altitude 0 trips a model or a clamp, say which and use the lowest value that does not. All values go into `docs/PANEL-REVIEW.md`. Row 10 is not a power setting: it means no full flare over water (height is hard to judge, a stall from too high is the danger), a reduced speed with 300 ft/min in the last part and touchdown at that rate; the row stays a plain printed line.
