## Phase Goal

**As a** DA50 RG pilot or student, **I want to** open the deployed trainer on my desktop, tablet or phone and operate the panel's switches, guarded switches, levers and buttons by mouse or touch, **so that** I can later rehearse emergency procedures by actually operating the controls.

This plan delivers the lever slice: the pedestal, front-panel and under-panel levers at their real positions, by drag or by tap, on every device, with the emergency gear extension protected against stray taps.

<objective>
Prove one lever (POWER, 0 to 100 % load) end to end with drag-and-snap and tap markers, then pin the detent math test-first and add COWL FLAP, GEAR and FLAPS at their real positions, and finally add the under-panel levers including the drag-only EMERGENCY GEAR EXTENSION.

Purpose: PNL-01 (levers by mouse or touch), PNL-02 (levers reach only their defined detents), PNL-06 (the emergency gear extension cannot be actuated by a stray tap) and PNL-08 (levers at their real positions). Decisions D-05 (drag along a track, snap on release, Pointer Events plus setPointerCapture, tap a detent marker to jump), D-05a (single POWER lever, no separate propeller lever), D-07c (COWL FLAP OPEN/CLOSED on the pedestal; under-panel levers), D-07d (FLAPS and GEAR on the front panel, POWER 0 to 100 % without a ground-idle gate, in-flight defaults, confirmed under-panel positions) and D-19 (positions follow the real aircraft).
Output: src/engine/detents.ts, pedestal, gear/flaps and under-panel data, the DetentLever widget, unit and e2e tests.
</objective>
