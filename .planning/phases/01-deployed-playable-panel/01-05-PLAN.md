## Phase Goal

**As a** DA50 RG pilot or student, **I want to** open the deployed trainer on my desktop, tablet or phone and operate the panel's switches, guarded switches, levers and buttons by mouse or touch, **so that** I can later rehearse emergency procedures by actually operating the controls.

This plan delivers the lever slice: the pedestal's detented levers, by drag or by tap, on every device.

<objective>
Prove one detented lever (POWER) end to end with drag-and-snap and tap-to-detent, then pin the detent math with test-first unit tests and add the remaining pedestal levers.

Purpose: PNL-01 (detented levers by mouse or touch) and PNL-02 (levers reach only their defined detents). Decisions D-05 (drag along a track, snap on release, Pointer Events plus setPointerCapture, tap a detent marker to jump) and D-05a (single POWER lever, no separate propeller lever; levers are POWER, FLAPS, GEAR, COWL FLAP).
Output: src/engine/detents.ts, pedestal data, the DetentLever widget, unit and e2e tests.
</objective>
