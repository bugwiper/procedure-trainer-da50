## Phase Goal

**As a** DA50 RG pilot or student, **I want to** open the deployed trainer on my desktop, tablet or phone and operate the panel's switches, guarded switches, levers and buttons by mouse or touch, **so that** I can later rehearse emergency procedures by actually operating the controls.

This plan delivers the CNT-03 slice of that goal: the written rule set that every later scenario author follows before any AFM-derived procedure text enters this public repository.

<objective>
Write and commit the content policy for AFM-derived material (CNT-03), link it from a new README, and enforce its repository-hygiene rules against the tracked tree. The concrete limit for "short verbatim checklist step lines" (RESEARCH Open Question 3, assumption A4) is decided by the project owner at a checkpoint before it is written into the policy.

Purpose: the policy must exist and be decided before Phase 2 commits the first procedure text (ROADMAP success criterion 5; D-14, D-15, D-16).
Output: docs/CONTENT-POLICY.md and README.md.

This plan needs no npm packages: every gate is a shell or git command, so it runs in parallel with plan 01. Plan 06 later surfaces the policy inside the app and adds a Vitest hygiene test.
</objective>

  <name>Task 2: Decide the concrete limit for verbatim checklist step lines</name>
  <decision>How long may a verbatim AFM checklist step line be, and in what unit is that counted?</decision>
  <context>D-15 allows "short verbatim AFM checklist step lines" but does not say how short (RESEARCH assumption A4, Open Question 3). This limit decides what verbatim text Phase 2 and later commit to a public repository, where history is permanent: text committed under a limit that later proves too loose cannot be withdrawn. Both options keep the rules already fixed in section 2 (no verbatim WARNING/CAUTION/NOTE text, no figures, tables or page images, no whole procedure as continuous prose). Both count length in whitespace-separated words of a single printed checklist line and treat a line as verbatim when its wording matches the AFM line apart from whitespace and letter case. No legal review has happened yet (recorded in section 6).</context>
  <options>
    <option id="option-a">
      <name>One checklist line: item plus action or target state, at most 12 words</name>
      <pros>Matches the research proposal; lets a scenario show the checklist line as pilots know it; easy to check automatically in Phase 2 (word count per step field)</pros>
      <cons>More verbatim text in the public repo than option B; 12 words is a judgment, not a legal threshold</cons>
    </option>
    <option id="option-b">
      <name>Item name and position keyword only, at most 6 words; everything else paraphrased</name>
      <pros>Lowest copyright exposure while still using real control names and positions, which are facts</pros>
      <cons>Checklist display reads less like the real AFM; authors paraphrase more of each step</cons>
    </option>
  </options>
  <resume-signal>Select option-a or option-b, or state your own limit and its unit</resume-signal>
