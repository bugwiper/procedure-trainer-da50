## Phase Goal

**As a** DA50 RG pilot or student, **I want to** open the deployed trainer on my desktop, tablet or phone and operate the panel's switches, guarded switches, levers and buttons by mouse or touch, **so that** I can later rehearse emergency procedures by actually operating the controls.

This plan delivers the glass-display slice (PFD, two-page MFD with a working page softkey), the in-app content-policy surfacing, and the responsive behaviour on every device class.

<objective>
Make the MFD PAGE softkey switch real MFD pages end to end, then add the static PFD with self-hosted B612 typography, and finally the footer About dialog with the content policy, the portrait rotate hint, and the cross-device layout checks.

Purpose: PNL-05 (fits every screen without horizontal scroll; regions are data and visible), CNT-03 surfacing (D-16), PNL-01 (MFD softkey operable). Decisions D-02, D-04, D-04a, D-04b, D-10, D-11, D-12, D-13, D-16.
Output: frame and gauge components, About dialog, styles, a Vitest hygiene test and three e2e specs.
</objective>
