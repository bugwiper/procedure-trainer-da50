## Phase goal

Users can run all three initial emergencies (Oil Temp High, Electrical Failure, Engine Fire) and see each one react to what they do: gauges move, ignored failures escalate, handled failures recover. (ROADMAP Phase 3, Mode mvp, goal kept verbatim.) This plan puts the two controls of the Engine Fire checklist that the panel lacked on the panel, at the positions the owner (the DA50 instructor) supplied on 2026-10-22, so plan 03-08 grades every panel step of AFM 03-33 (D-08, CNT-01).

Purpose: owner decision of 2026-10-22 (03-RESEARCH.md Open Question 3, resolved), D-08 (Engine Fire as a full scenario of the initial pack), CNT-01. Layout fidelity is the core value: positions and looks follow the owner's description, and what he marked as uncertain is listed for review.

</objective>
