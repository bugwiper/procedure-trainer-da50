## Phase goal

Users can run all three initial emergencies (Oil Temp High, Electrical Failure, Engine Fire) and see each one react to what they do: gauges move, ignored failures escalate, handled failures recover. (ROADMAP Phase 3, Mode mvp, goal kept verbatim.) This plan closes the UAT gap of test 1: on Realistic the failure takes too long to start and then one watches the temperature rise for ever.

<objective>
Make the Realistic level quick for ENG OIL TEMP without touching Easy and Medium: a shorter onset window (2 to 6 s, all three scenarios), a start reading just below the yellow band, and a faster rise so that yellow comes within seconds of the onset and red with the ENG OIL TEMP message within 10 s. The mechanism is scenario data plus one aircraft parameter: the onset block of a scenario gains the start quantities and the extra physics parameters of the Realistic run; the aircraft gains the parameter oil_temp_rate that scales the step of the oil temperature lag.

Purpose: UAT test 1 (owner, 2026-10-23): "Medium time constants fit; on Realistic the start takes a bit long and then one watches the temperature rise slowly; it should begin earlier, perhaps already in yellow, and reach red with the CAS message within about 10 seconds."
Output: schema fields, the rate of a lag model, the shared start function, the oil scenario and the other two windows, lint L15 (and L14 on the new start), unit and e2e cases, the grading and review documents.
</objective>

## Defaults used by this plan

| ID | Default | Why |
|----|---------|-----|
| C-58 | The Realistic setup lives in the existing optional `onset` block of a scenario: `quantities` (the start values of the Realistic run, laid over the aircraft's base values, replacing `initial.quantities` on that level) and `parameters` (physics parameters in force from the onset, laid over the top-level `parameters`). Both default to empty; a run without an onset never reads them | The block is already the Realistic-only data (a scenario without it does not offer the level); additive and optional, format stays 1; D-01 "scenarios set parameters, initial quantities and onset" |
| C-59 | `simSetup(env, scenario, { onsetAt })` builds the faulted parameters as normal, then the scenario's `parameters`, then `onset.parameters`; without `onsetAt` the faulted record is exactly what it is today | Easy and Medium stay byte-identical; the sim stays the one stepper of the live run and the replay |
| C-60 | A lag model may declare an optional `rate` (a function of the physics inputs, a multiplier of the step length); the step is `(target - current) * (STEP_MS * rate) / tauMs`, and a model without `rate` or with rate 1 gives the same bits as today. A rate that is not finite or not above 0 throws an Error naming the quantity. The aircraft gets the parameter `oil_temp_rate` (min 1, max 10, normal 1) and the oil temperature lag reads it | Faster rise and recovery with the same target (the recovery stays reachable, unlike a stronger cooling fault); pure arithmetic; the effective time constant is the 60 s divided by the rate |
| C-61 | `realisticQuantities(scenario, quantities)` in src/engine/predicates.ts returns the base values laid over by `onset.quantities` (an unknown id throws PredicateError like initialQuantities). The session (start quantities and the bridge reset), `grade` (the replay of a recording with an onset) and lint L14 call it | One definition, so live run, replay and lint cannot drift apart (D-02) |
| C-62 | Proposed values, ENG OIL TEMP: `window_s` 2 to 6 s, `onset.quantities` oil_temp_c 114, `onset.parameters` oil_temp_rate 3 (time constant 20 s). Instructor-review items | UAT test 1: begin earlier, start just below yellow, red within about 10 s |
| C-63 | The other two scenarios get the window 2 to 6 s as well and no Realistic start or parameter: the ALTN 1 FAIL caution already shows about 4 s after the onset, and the Engine Fire cue is the separate windscreen phase | "A shorter onset window" is scenario data; scope of the gap is the oil temperature rise |
| C-64 | L15 has two clauses. Speed: in a do-nothing Realistic run (from `realisticQuantities`, onset at the lower and at the upper end of the window) every listed annunciation shows no later than REALISTIC_SHOW_WITHIN_MS (12 000 ms) after the onset; the bound is the owner's "about 10 s" with a margin. | C-65 | Nothing new is drawn or recorded: the Recording keeps its shape and its seed, the Realistic setup is scenario data selected by the presence of `Recording.onset` (C-33). WR-01 is left alone: the lag step at rate 3 is three times larger, so the 0.01 rounding stalls the oil temperature only 1 degree short of a target (instead of 3), and no band edge or threshold of this plan lies within that gap of a settle value (correct answer p60 with the cowl open settles near 111, below 115) | The gap asks for no rounding change |
