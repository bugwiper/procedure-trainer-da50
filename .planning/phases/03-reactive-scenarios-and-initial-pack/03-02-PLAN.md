## Phase goal

Users can run all three initial emergencies (Oil Temp High, Electrical Failure, Engine Fire) and see each one react to what they do: gauges move, ignored failures escalate, handled failures recover. (ROADMAP Phase 3, Mode mvp, goal kept verbatim.) Locked decisions: `03-CONTEXT.md`.

<objective>
Make every engine-page gauge follow the simulated aircraft: move the AFM 02-05 ranges and bands out of the MFD frame into the quantity data, give every quantity a base value (cruise at the registry defaults) instead of an engine-off idle value, and model every quantity, including the electrical bus that the Electrical Failure scenario needs.

Purpose: D-12 (all engine-page gauges respond, the physics covers them), D-01 (generic aircraft behaviour), D-10 (bus effects only as far as needed), RESEARCH Pitfall 4 (base values so new CAS rules never fire on the free panel).
Output: one quantity per MFD engine gauge plus two hidden ones, their models, the MFD bound to all of them, readouts at the gauge's resolution, tests and e2e cases.
</objective>

## Table P2: model behaviour (constants are the executor's choice inside these constraints)

Terms: running means ENGINE MASTER on; load is the POWER travel percent while running, else 0; ambient is 15 °C. Alternator 1 delivers while running with ALTERNATOR 1 on, `cb.alt1` in, ELECT. MASTER on and the parameter `alt1_output` above 0 (normal 1, range 0 to 1). Alternator 2 runs while running with ALTERNATOR 2 on, `cb.alt2` in and ELECT. MASTER on; it supplies the main bus only with ESS BUS on (AFM 07-33 fact recorded in RESEARCH).

| Quantity | Mode | Required behaviour |
|----------|------|--------------------|
| rpm | lag, tau 1 to 3 s | base at the defaults; rises with load and stays at most 2340 at POWER 100 %; target 0 with the engine off |
| load_pct | lag, tau about 1 s | target equals load |
| oil_pressure_bar | lag, tau 1 to 3 s | base at the defaults; rises a little with rpm; target 0 with the engine off |
| oil_temp_c | the plan 01 model | unchanged; its P1-a target equals the base |
| coolant_temp_c | lag, tau 20 to 60 s | base at the defaults; rises with load; lower with the cowl flap open; toward ambient with the engine off |
| gearbox_temp_c | lag, tau 30 to 90 s | base at the defaults; rises with load; toward ambient with the engine off |
| fuel_temp_c | lag, tau 60 to 180 s | base at the defaults; a small load effect |
| fuel_pressure_bar | lag, tau about 1 s | base while running; EMERG FUEL PUMP on raises it, still inside green, also with the engine off; 0 with the engine off and the pump off |
| fuel_qty_lh_gal | rate | while running burns in proportion to load (about 0.1 gal per minute at cruise, not compressed); with FUEL TRANS on, fuel moves from RH into LH while RH is above 1 gal and LH below its green bound |
| fuel_qty_rh_gal | rate | minus the transfer |
| alt1_amps | lag, tau about 2 s | 30 times alt1_output while alternator 1 delivers, else 0 |
| alt2_amps | lag, tau about 2 s | while alternator 2 runs: 10 with ESS BUS off, 35 with ESS BUS on; else 0 |
| battery_pct | rate | with ELECT. MASTER on: charges toward 100 while the main bus has an alternator supply; discharges while it has none (compressed: empty in about 4 min from full); no change with the master off |
| bus_volts | lag, tau about 1 s | 28 with an alternator supply on the main bus (alternator 1 delivering, or alternator 2 running with ESS BUS on); 22 plus 4 times battery_pct / 100 on the battery alone; the scale minimum with ELECT. MASTER off |
