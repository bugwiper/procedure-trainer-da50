## Decisions (assumptions taken in planning; the owner may veto any of them)
Owner decisions D-08 and D-09 apply: airspeed in flight, altitude, attitude and vertical speed come from simulated quantities (heading not included), the 140 kt static value goes away in situations while the free panel stays static, and the profile is scripted per scenario (climb, descent, level), not an aerodynamic model, deterministic and replayable. The take-off roll airspeed of phase 5.1 stays. All numbers are instructor-review values; arithmetic is `+ - * /`, clamping and quantising only.
- **V-01 Where a scenario scripts its flight.** Parameters in force "from the onset" (`parameters`, `onset.parameters`) would leave the first 2 to 6 s of a Realistic run on the normal values; a climbing take-off must not drift to 140 kt before its onset. So a new optional top-level section `profile` (record of parameter id to number, default empty, TOON format 1) holds parameters that are in force from the first second at every level. `simSetup` folds the profile into the normal parameters (so the faulted set, which starts from the normal set, carries it too), then the scenario parameters and the onset parameters lay over it as before. An aircraft parameter is a flight-profile parameter only when its definition carries `profile: true` (new optional field of `ParameterDef`); lint rule L25 checks every key of `profile` against that set and the parameter's range. Start values (altitude, speed) stay in `initial.quantities` and, for the Realistic start, `onset.quantities` (the established pattern of the airspeed on the roll).
- **V-02 Quantities (hidden, no gauge; the PFD draws them).** `altitude_ft` 0 to 20000 base 1400, `vertical_speed_fpm` -2000 to 2000 base 0, `pitch_deg` -30 to 30 base 0, `bank_deg` -60 to 60 base 0, each with one unmarked band; `airspeed_kt` exists. The bases are the static readings of the free panel today, so the free panel does not change and every model is anchored at the normal parameters (C-09).
- **V-03 Flight-profile parameters (all `profile: true`).** `flight_speed_kt` 0 to 200 normal 140, `flight_vs_fpm` -2000 to 2000 normal 0, `flight_bank_deg` -60 to 60 normal 0 (powered flight), `glide_speed_kt` 0 to 200 normal 90 and `glide_vs_fpm` -2000 to 2000 normal -700 (engine stopped), `terrain_ft` 0 to 10000 normal 1400 (the altitude never sinks below it). The glide defaults are instructor-review values, not the AFM best-glide figure (owner review point).
- **V-04 Regimes.** Ground regime: the parameter `ground_roll` is above 0 (set by the ground scenarios from their onset); the airspeed follows the ground speed as in phase 5.1 (a lag with a rate that makes it follow within one step, the existing behaviour), vertical speed, pitch and bank target 0, the altitude holds. Flight regime otherwise: with the engine running (`running()`) the targets are the `flight_*` parameters, with the engine stopped (ENGINE MASTER off or the selector OFF or an empty drawn tank) the `glide_*` ones; nothing else, in particular not the POWER lever, moves them (D-09: no aerodynamic model). The airspeed lag has a time constant of 5 s in flight, the vertical speed 3 s, pitch and bank 2 s. Altitude is a rate model whose rate is the vertical speed quantity; it stops at `terrain_ft` (the vertical speed target is held at 0 or above while the altitude is at or below it). Pitch target = flight-path angle (vertical speed divided by the airspeed floored at 40 kt times 1.7675, the ft/min per kt per degree) plus `PITCH_PER_KT` 0.06 degrees for every knot below the cruise speed 140 (the quantity base), so a level cruise reads 0, a 70 kt climb of 500 fpm about +8 degrees and a 90 kt glide of -700 fpm about -1.4 degrees; bank target = `flight_bank_deg`.
- **V-05 What changes in a run, which shipped scenario scripts what.** Cruise scenarios keep 140 kt level and only start at their altitude: ENG OIL TEMP 4500 ft, ALTN 1 FAIL and ALTN 2 FAIL 3500 ft, ELEC FIRE FLIGHT 4500 ft, ICING 7500 ft, ENGINE FIRE in flight 5500 ft. When the pilot stops the engine in ENGINE FIRE in flight, the aircraft glides (90 kt, -700 fpm by the defaults) and the PFD follows; ELEC FIRE FLIGHT has no engine stop in its checklist and stays level. T/O FIRE NO ABORT starts at 1450 ft with `profile` flight_speed_kt 70, flight_vs_fpm 500 (climb-out), glide_speed_kt 70, glide_vs_fpm -300 and keeps its held ground speed for the runway marks. The three ground scenarios script a stopped or rolling aircraft in `profile` (ENGINE FIRE GROUND and ELEC FIRE GROUND: flight_speed_kt 0, glide_speed_kt 0, glide_vs_fpm 0; T/O FIRE ABORT: flight_speed_kt equal to its start speed, glide values 0), which also removes a glitch of the Realistic level: before the onset the parked aircraft read 140 kt for a few seconds.
- **V-06 The PFD.** Pure helpers beside `speedTickOffsets` in `avionics.ts`: the altitude readout (nearest 10 ft), the altitude tape ticks (100 ft per 30 px, seven ticks wrapping like the speed tape), the vertical speed text (nearest 10 fpm, a plus sign for a climb, 0 plain) and the pitch and bank to pixels and degrees. `PfdFrame.tsx`: the attitude art is clipped to its rectangle; sky, ground, horizon line and pitch ladder (every 5 degrees to 30) sit in one group that is shifted by the pitch and rotated by minus the bank about the attitude centre (SVG `rotate(angle cx cy)`); the bank scale stays fixed, the roll pointer rotates with the bank; the aircraft symbol is fixed; the altitude tape ticks slide and the readout shows the altitude; a small readout box under the barometer box prints the vertical speed with the static label VS (the text changes by value, never by control state, as with every gauge); the heading block stays static. New data attributes `data-altitude-readout` and `data-vs-readout` beside `data-airspeed-readout`. The quantity ids reach the frame through exported constants of `avionics.ts` (the way `PFD_AIRSPEED_QUANTITY` does), so `src/ui` names no id of a scenario.
- **V-07 Not built here.** Heading, a flight director, a speed trend, altitude and speed bugs, and any aerodynamic effect of POWER on speed. The emergency landing continuation (todo 2026-10-28) is not in this plan: it needs the landing decision of Phase 7 and new scenes; the profile of this plan is the prerequisite it will build on. Pitot and stall heat cautions are plan 04.
- **Owner review points:** the altitude and speed of every shipped scenario; the glide defaults (90 kt, -700 fpm); the climb-out of 500 fpm in T/O FIRE NO ABORT; the pitch formula; the position and look of the vertical speed box; whether POWER should move the speed (it does not).
