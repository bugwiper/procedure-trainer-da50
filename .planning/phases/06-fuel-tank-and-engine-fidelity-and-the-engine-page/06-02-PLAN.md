- **E-05 Static display values are quantities.** `rudder_trim_pct` (-100 to 100, base 0, one unmarked band), `oxygen_psi` (0 to 2000, base 1270, bands red to 250, yellow to 400, green to 1700, unmarked to 2000), `deice_qty_gal` (0 to 8.3, the tank maximum of D-11, base 6.8, bands red to 1, green to 8.3, scale 1e-7 like the fuel quantities because a NORM step uses only 0.00008 gal and the default scale 0.01 would round it away), `service_hours` (0 to 99999, base 249.5, one unmarked band). The base values and the oxygen and de-ice bands are layout assumptions, not AFM figures (the de-ice tank maximum is the owner's figure); all four are listed in PANEL-REVIEW.md. A quantity without a model keeps its value, so they stay static; a scenario may set them.
- **E-06 Labels.** The frame keeps its convention of upper-case labels on the glass (the width table of the gauge boxes is measured for capitals). Static texts: ELECTRICAL (exists), FUEL, FUEL QTY, FUEL TEMP, FUEL PRESS, FFLOW GPH, FUEL CALCULATOR, GALLONS REMAIN, GALLONS USED, ENDURANCE, RANGE NM, RUDDER TRIM, OXYGEN, PSI, DE-ICE, US GAL, NORM:, HIGH:, TOTAL SERVICE, HRS, the end marks L and R, the pointer marks L and R and the scale numerals. The old heading ENGINE at the top left of the page goes: the real page has none inside the glass (the header already reads EIS - Engine). Every text is listed in `docs/PANEL-LABELS.md`.
- **E-07 Values.** FFlow GPH one decimal from `fuel_flow_gph`. Calculator rows from `fuelCalculator` (remain and used one decimal, endurance by `formatEndurance`, range in whole NM). De-ice Norm and High from `deiceEndurance(quantity)` = quantity divided by the flow of the mode times 60, in minutes, formatted H:MM; the flows are the owner's `DEICE_NORM_GPH` 3 and `DEICE_HIGH_GPH` 6 (D-11; at 6.8 gal they give 2:16 and 1:08, within a minute of the real page). Total service one decimal and HRS.
- **E-08 De-ice fluid model (D-03, D-11; todo items 1 and 3 drive it).** A rate model on `deice_qty_gal`. Owner data (TKS): tank 8.3 gal, NORM 3 gal/h, HIGH 6 gal/h, MAX 0.4 gal per activation, WIND SHIELD spray 40 ml per activation. The mode flows apply while `sw.deice_mode` is NORM or HIGH (OFF uses nothing). A MAX activation is the run the existing reaction already times (120 s, flag `DEICE_MAX_ACTIVE`, only on HIGH as the reaction requires): its 0.4 gal are spread evenly over the run (the rate is `DEICE_MAX_ACTIVATION_GAL` divided by the run length in minutes, 12 gal/h) and take the place of the HIGH flow during the run, so the activation volume is the total of the run (the owner may mean it on top of the HIGH flow: a one-line data change; owner review point). The spray takes `DEICE_SPRAY_ML` (40) divided by `ML_PER_US_GAL` (3785.41) gal, spread over the 10 s of the spray (flag `WINDSHIELD_SPRAY_ACTIVE`) on top of the mode flow (a separate nozzle). Every volume is a named data constant with its unit in the name; the conversion uses `+ - * /` only. The spray and the pilot-side protection do not depend on the fluid quantity; an empty de-ice tank belongs with the later de-ice scenarios.
- **E-09 ANNUN TEST (todo item 2).** While `sw.deice_ice_light` is at `annun_test` the three de-ice lamps light (`deiceLamps` gets a third argument; the indicators read the switch). The todo says which lamps is unknown; taking the three lamps of the system is an assumption the owner confirms (question Q-02 of PANEL-LABELS stays open about whether the position springs back). It works on the free panel too (lamps derive from control state).
- **E-10 Deferred with reasons.** ICE LIGHT effect: it only matters at night, and the dusk and night scenes are a deferred todo (`2026-10-27-night-dusk-scenes.md`), so there is nothing to show yet. ELT lamp in RESET and TEST: the lamp behaviour of the remote switch is in no source of the project (missing information); `lt.elt` stays lit in ON only. Both stay in the de-ice todo with these reasons.
- **E-11 Map strip unchanged.** The Map page keeps its eight bars (FUEL QTY LH last); the combined gauge is the engine page only (owner review point: should the strip show both tanks?).
- **Owner review points:** the layout against the photo (spacing, the arc size, the dual bars, the red ends of the fuel temperature bar from plan 01); the oxygen and de-ice bands and base values; whether the MAX activation volume of 0.4 gal replaces or adds to the HIGH flow; the ANNUN TEST lamp set; the strip.
