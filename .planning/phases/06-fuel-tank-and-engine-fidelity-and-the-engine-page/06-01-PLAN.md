## Decisions (assumptions taken in planning; the owner may veto any of them)
Owner decisions D-01 to D-05 of `06-CONTEXT.md` apply (D-04a to D-04d are the tank topology, the transfer, the fuel temperatures and the limits). Every constant below that the owner did not give is an instructor-review value and stays data in `src/aircraft/da50rg/` (GRADING.md gets one list in task 3). No new fuel scenario is written (D-05); the free panel stays static (owner decision 2026-10-27). Pure engine, aircraft and scenario code uses only `+ - * /`, clamping and quantising (ESLint forbids `Math.random`, `Math.exp`, `Math.pow` and `**` there).
- **F-01 (D-04a) Topology.** `supply` is a constant pump flow `FUEL_SUPPLY_GPH` = 30 gph (instructor-review value, above the burn of 14.6 gph at full POWER so the return is never negative), present only while the engine runs. `burn` is the existing fuel-flow line (3.3 plus 0.1133 times the load in percent, fitted to the two owner readings; unchanged). `returned` = supply minus burn while the left tank has room (quantity below the capacity), else 0. The drawn tank (selector NORMAL = left, EMERGENCY = right, OFF = none) loses `burn + returned`; the left tank gains `returned`. Net: NORMAL leaves the right tank untouched and the left tank at minus burn (exactly as before); EMERGENCY drains the right tank at supply while the left tank rises by supply minus burn; with the left tank full on EMERGENCY the right tank loses only the burn, so fuel is neither created nor lost. A leak (rate on top of the loss) is NOT built: D-04a mentions it for later fuel scenarios; the rate model reads one `perMinute` function per tank, so a later parameter adds one term.
- **F-02 (D-04) Starvation.** When the drawn tank is empty (quantity 0) no fuel reaches the engine: the helper `fuelCut` becomes true (it already means selector OFF) and every consumer follows (running, the fire feed, the pump pressure, the alternators through `running`). The engine comes back at once when fuel is available again; no relight procedure is modelled (the empty-tank scenario of a later phase adds it).
- **F-03 (D-04b) Transfer.** `FUEL_TRANSFER_PER_MIN` = 1 gal/min is exactly 60 US gal/h. It runs while `sw.fuel_trans` is `on`, the right tank is above 0 and the left tank is below the capacity (the old floor of 1 gal and the green-band top are replaced). It does not read the electrical state (the pump bus and breaker are not modelled; the breaker-effects todo keeps that item).
- **F-04 (D-04b) FUEL TRANS message.** Rule-derived in `cas.ts`, not a flag: the predicate vocabulary has no AND, so physics sets the hidden quantity `fuel_trans_active` (0 or 1, bands idle and active, lag of one step like the alternator fault flags) from the same condition as the transfer, and the rule reads the band. New CAS level `advisory`: white, listed after warnings and cautions, no aural tone (`aural.ts` counts warning and caution only). The chip goes into the existing CAS chip area of the PFD (`CasChips.tsx`), in the next free slot; the owner may move it (owner review point). The text FUEL TRANS fits the 12-character CAS pattern.
- **F-05 (D-04c, D-04d) Fuel temperatures and OAT.** The quantity `fuel_temp_c` is replaced by `fuel_temp_lh_c` and `fuel_temp_rh_c` (range -25 to +60 C, lag 60 s). A new parameter `oat_c` (-40 to 50, normal 15, the value of the old constant `AMBIENT_C`) is the outside air temperature; targets: left = oat + `FUEL_TEMP_LH_OFFSET_C` (8), right = left + `FUEL_TEMP_RH_STEP_C` (-2). The old load term of the fuel temperature is dropped, because D-04c states the rule from the OAT alone. `AMBIENT_C` is replaced by the parameter in the three engine-off targets (oil, coolant, gearbox) and in the oil heat formula: at the normal value every result is bit for bit the same. Bases: 23 and 21. Bands inside the range: yellow to -20, green to 55, yellow to 60; the red end zones of the real bar are not drawn because the range ends at the limits (open question for the owner).
- **F-06 Tank quantities.** `fuel_qty_lh_gal` and `fuel_qty_rh_gal` run 0 to `TANK_CAPACITY_GAL` (26, exported from `quantities.ts`, read by physics from the quantity data), base 20 each, one shared band table (red to 1, green to 26) so that the combined bar of plan 02 has one scale (the same rule as the Amps quantities). The `FUEL_SCALE` of 1e-7 stays.
- **F-07 Fuel flow quantity.** `fuel_flow_gph`: 0 to 30, base 11.23 (the cruise equilibrium of the fitted line, 3.3 + 0.11333 times 70), lag of one step, one unmarked band, no model of its own beyond the target; the engine page of plan 02 draws it as FFlow GPH.
- **F-08 Gallons used.** `fuel_used_gal`: 0 to 1000, base 12 (the 52 gal capacity less the 40 gal of the two base tanks), rate model plus burn per minute, scale `FUEL_SCALE`. Refuelling and the RST Fuel softkey are not modelled (the softkeys stay inert).
- **F-09 Calculator.** A pure function `fuelCalculator(quantities)` in `fuel-calculator.ts`: remain = left + right tank; used = `fuel_used_gal`; endurance in whole minutes = remain divided by the flow times 60, or null while the flow is below 1 gph or the result would exceed 99 h 59 min; range in NM = endurance hours times `airspeed_kt` (no wind: the ground speed equals the airspeed; it reads 0 on the apron where the airspeed is the ground speed). `formatEndurance(minutes)` returns H:MM or `--:--`. No quantity of its own: everything derives from the tank, flow and airspeed quantities.
- **F-10 Existing consumers keep working.** The old engine page of the frame stays as it is until plan 02 replaces it; only its Fuel temp bar reads `fuel_temp_lh_c`. The Map-page strip keeps its eight bars (FUEL QTY LH last). `tests/fixtures/recordings.json` stays unchanged (no quantity of this plan is read by a graded predicate); no scenario file is edited.
- **Owner review points** (go into docs/PANEL-REVIEW.md "Phase 6: owner review" and GRADING.md): the supply flow of 30 gph; the right-warmer-than-left question (the owner's photo of the real page shows the right fuel temperature a few degrees above the left one while D-04c says right = left - 2: built as D-04c, both offsets are data); red end zones of the fuel temperature bar; where the white FUEL TRANS chip sits; the pump works without electrical power; the engine restarts at once when the empty tank is switched away.
