## Decisions (assumptions taken in planning; the owner may veto any of them)
Owner decisions R-01 to R-06 of the section "Rework decisions R-01.." in `07-CONTEXT.md` apply; they supersede D-02 to D-05. The removal lists of R-01 and R-05 are the checklist for what disappears. This plan changes nothing in `grade`, in the scenario format or in any scenario file, and leaves the FURTHER ACTIONS button and modal alone (plan 07-05 replaces them).
- **S-01 (R-01) Session API after the plan.** Gone: everything on the R-01 and R-05 lists. Kept: `level`, `levelPicked`, `setLevel`, `checklistShown`, `runBranches`, `toggleChecklist`. New: `checklistView` (`'list' | 'sheet'`, Realistic only; Easy and Medium are always `'sheet'`), `checklistChoice` (`{ ref, heading } | null`, display state of the run), `checklistList` (`{ status: 'idle' | 'loading' | 'ready' | 'failed', entries }` with `ref`, `heading` and the parsed scenario per entry), `checklistMarks` (the `GradeResult` of the partial recording while the sheet shows the scenario's own checklist and the Realistic onset has passed, else null), `hintShown`, `toggleHint()`, `showChecklistList()`, `chooseChecklist(ref)`, `retryChecklistList()`. Every one of them is cleared wherever a run starts or ends (start, Retry, Next, leave, fail, complete, close of the debrief); `Recording` and `grade` are unchanged.
- **S-02 (R-02) Live marks.** `currentRecording()` stays the single source of the partial recording. The session subscribes once to the store (log events and retracts) and to its own `checklistShown`; while the sheet is shown on the scenario's own checklist and `completedAt` is not before the onset, each event refreshes `checklistMarks` from `grade(current, env, currentRecording())`. The first implementation does exactly that; the executor times one call on the longest shipped scenario log at 30 minutes and adds a trailing coalescing of the refresh only if it costs more than about 30 ms. `buildOutline` gets the mode `live` (needs the result, like the debrief form; steps are plain lines with verdict Done/tone done when credited, otherwise no tag and no verdict; no order lines, nothing muted that the briefing form does not mute). The sheet's step rows must not carry the debrief row test id.
- **S-03 (R-03) The Realistic list.** `showChecklistList()` loads, once per run, `listChoices()` and then `openScenario` for each entry (allowed packs only, through the pack seam; it does not use the `loading` wrapper, `busy` or `loadFailure`, so presses and the run are untouched); a failure sets `failed` and the sheet shows an own-wording message with a Try again button. Entries are in listing order; the heading is `checklistHead(scenario).heading` or the title when no heading is declared. `chooseChecklist(ref)` (running, Realistic only) sets the choice and the view `sheet`; the sheet shows the chosen scenario's outline in the briefing form with `branchStatuses(foreign, env)`, marks only if the ref equals the opened ref (the session keeps it: `openedRef`), and has a button back to the list. The CHOOSE CHECKLIST press: sheet hidden opens it (the list when nothing was chosen, else the chosen checklist), sheet shown hides it.
- **S-04 (R-04) The hint box.** `HintBox.tsx` replaces `HintLine.tsx`: a `role="status"` box over the windscreen, `pointer-events: none`, test ids `hint-box`, `hint-box-title` (the file's title) and `hint-box-situation` (the file's `briefing.situation`), text children only. The CSS keeps the placement logic of the old hint line (clear of the Full panel button and the rotate hint, moves aside while the checklist sheet is shown). `toggleHint()` does nothing outside a Realistic run or before the onset.
- **S-05 (R-06) Pad geometry.** Starting values are in R-06; the final numbers live in `SESSION_LAYOUT` and in the hand-written tables of `tests/session-run-view.test.ts` and `tests/zoom.test.ts`, edited together. Every run-view height is at least 64 units and every rectangle stays inside the 440 x 480 region without overlap. The main view of the free panel narrows to 280 wide (x 80); the choose view is unchanged. A static label never changes with a control state: CHECKLIST versus CHOOSE CHECKLIST is fixed by the level when the run starts.
- **Owner review points of this plan:** the mode-free briefing note, the Realistic list showing headings only, the sheet opening before the onset, LEAVE as the only way out of a run, the narrower free-panel buttons.

<objective>
Take the mode out, give every run a checklist button whose sheet marks the done steps green, let Realistic choose its checklist from all of them and ask the situation as a hint box, and slim the pad in a run down to what the run needs.
Purpose: fewer clicks and decisions before the run, and the checklist is at hand the way a pilot has it.
Output: no mode concept in code, tests, e2e and docs; `live` outline mode and the live marks; the checklist choice with the debrief line; `HintBox`; the new run view with LEAVE; rewritten session, outline, run-view and e2e specs; the corrected README, GRADING and PANEL-LABELS text.
</objective>
