## Decisions (assumptions taken in planning; the owner may veto any of them)
Owner decisions R-07 to R-14 of the section "Rework decisions R-01.." in `07-CONTEXT.md` apply; they supersede the modal and clock-pause parts of D-06 and D-07 and the single-use sentence of R16. Roles, accept lists, the ground rule, the reason order and the outcome cut (D-08 to D-13) stay as built. Plan 07-04 is done: no mode, the pad has a temporary FURTHER ACTIONS button and the old modal, which this plan deletes.
- **S-01 (R-08) The model.** `RecordedDecision` gets an optional `on` (absent means on). `effectiveSelection(decisions, set)` in `src/engine/further.ts` returns `{ actions, conduct }`: the set of action ids whose last entry is on, and the conduct selection replayed in order (an on entry of a conduct option selects it; an off entry clears the selection only when it names the selected option). `gradeFurther` runs it on the counted decisions (after the outcome cut): an action is `done` when selected at the end, `picks` stays the list of on entries of conduct options in order (history for the debrief), `last` is the final selection (null when cleared), `status` and the reasons are computed as before. The existing cases of `tests/further.test.ts` and the golden fixture stay untouched and green: that is the compatibility proof; new cases only add.
- **S-02 (R-07, R-11) Aircraft data.** `FurtherOption` gets an optional short `label` (caps): BRIEF PASSENGERS, MAYDAY, PAN PAN PAN, CONTINUE NORMALLY, ALTERNATE LANDING, EMERGENCY LANDING; `text` stays for the debrief. `src/aircraft/da50rg/further-pad.ts` exports `FURTHER_PAD` (region size and one slot per option id, region-local, starting values in R-07: columns x 0, 205, 410 of width 190; rows y 0 and 103 of height 87; top row brief_passengers, atc_mayday, atc_pan_pan; bottom row the three conduct options). `plates.ts` gets the plate `further-pad` and `panel.ts` the region `further_area` (starting values in R-07), both named exceptions of the legroom gate. If the group titles and the conduct title in `further.ts` have no reader left after the modal goes, remove them with their test cases; otherwise leave them.
- **S-03 (R-08, R-09, R-10) Session.** Gone: the pick method, the modal signal and its open and close methods and every place that clears them. New: `toggleDecision(id)` (running only; ignored before the Realistic onset, for an unknown id and for a conduct id when the scenario offers no conduct; for an action it appends `{ t, id, on: !selected }`, for a conduct option `{ t, id, on: true }` or, when it is the selected one, `{ t, id, on: false }`) and the computed `furtherSelection` (effectiveSelection of `decisions`) that the buttons read. `currentRecording()` keeps carrying `decisions`. No modal id means the clock driver no longer waits for the decisions.
- **S-04 (R-07, R-11) The buttons.** `SessionButton` gets an optional `selected` prop (tint of the face, pressed rim, `aria-pressed`, `data-selected`; the label never changes; after the screenshot review the tint must tell selected from not selected at a glance on the phone). `FurtherAreaFrame` is registered in `Panel.tsx` for the region `further_area`; it draws nothing unless a run is live; it draws the slots of `FURTHER_PAD`, leaving out the three conduct slots when the scenario offers no conduct; ids `further.<option id>` (`data-session-hit`, `data-session-button`); no option id is named in `src/ui` (the loop reads the aircraft data; `tests/blueprint.test.ts` also gets the six labels as forbidden literals for `src/engine`, `src/scenario` and `src/ui`). `zoom.ts` adds the six slot rectangles to the Full panel footprints.
- **S-05 Debrief wording.** A conduct that was selected and then cleared shows its picks line and the verdict Missing (the reason line stays "The flight decision was missing."); an action selected and cleared reads like one never selected. The emergency-landing note follows the final selection.
- **S-06 e2e helpers.** `pickFurther(page, hasTouch, ids)` keeps its signature (every spec that finishes a scenario uses it): it presses each id's button once and expects it selected; new `toggleFurther(page, hasTouch, id)` presses once without an expectation.
- **Owner review points of this plan:** the six labels, the footwell place and size, an empty plate on the free panel, conduct hidden on the ground and without a conduct, no clock pause, the debrief wording for a cleared conduct.
