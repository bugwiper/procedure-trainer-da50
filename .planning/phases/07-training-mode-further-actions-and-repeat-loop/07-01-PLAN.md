## Decisions (assumptions taken in planning; the owner may veto any of them)
Owner decisions D-02 to D-05, D-16 to D-18 of `07-CONTEXT.md` apply. This plan changes nothing in the scenario format, in `grade` or in any scenario file.
- **H-01 (D-04) The hint rule.** `src/engine/hint.ts` exports `nextOpenStep(scenario, env, recording)` returning `{ id, number?, text, action? }` or null. It calls `grade(scenario, env, recording)` (pure, unchanged), takes the first entry of `result.steps` (declared order, depth first, only applicable steps) whose severity is critical or standard and whose `credited` is false, and looks the step node up in the scenario by id to return its stored `number`, `text` and `action` untouched. No step open gives null. It reads no clock and keeps no state. Because it is `grade`, rule R12 applies: a step that was already true at the onset and not redone is still open.
- **H-02 Session API.** `Mode` is `'training' | 'exam'` with `MODES`, `MODE_NAMES` and `MODE_HINTS` next to the level constants of `session.ts`. New signals `mode` (default exam), `modePicked`, `hint` (null or the open step, or a flag for "nothing open"). New methods: `setMode(mode)` (briefing screen only, before the level is picked, sets `modePicked`), `askHint()` and, in task 2, `toggleChecklist()`. `openBriefing` resets `modePicked` and `levelPicked`; `toFreePanel`, `failRun` and `closeDebrief` clear the hint (and later the sheet). `completeRun` and `askHint` share one extracted `currentRecording()` (the store events with source user, `completedAt` the run clock now, the onset and the variant); do not duplicate the filter. `askHint` does nothing unless the screen is running and the mode is training, and on a recording with an onset it does nothing while the clock is before `onset.at`.
- **H-03 Run view (D-05).** `SESSION_LAYOUT.run` holds region-local rectangles for `further` (full width, top; drawn from plan 07-02, reserved here), `checklist` and `hint` (a pair of half widths), `new` and `choose` (a pair of half widths) and `reset` (full width). Starting values: x 35 and width 370 (half width 177, gap 16 as the choose view), rows of height 80 at y 20, 116, 212 and 308. `sessionFootprints` includes them (the Full panel corner chooser must still avoid every button). The executor may adjust numbers after the screenshot review and edits data and reference together. Button ids: `session.hint`, `session.checklist` (new), `session.new`, `session.choose`, `session.reset` (unchanged); the confirm for the three old ones is unchanged.
- **H-04 Hint line and sheet are not modals.** Neither holds a modal id, so the run clock keeps running and the panel stays operable. The hint line is a `role="status"` strip over the windscreen (art only, takes no press: `pointer-events: none`), test ids `hint-line`, `hint-mark`, `hint-text`, `hint-action`. The sheet has its own hide button (the pad button can be under it), is scrollable, never covers the footer and takes at most about 40 per cent of the panel area.
- **H-05 Fixed sentences (own wording).** Mode card title "Choose a mode"; hints under the buttons for Training ("Checklist and hints on demand during the run.") and Exam ("Silent. No checklist, no hints."); the hint line when nothing is open "All required steps are done. Press Procedure complete when you are ready."; the briefing note for Training "Training mode: show the checklist and ask for a hint during the run. When you are done, press Procedure complete." The Exam note stays as it is (test id `briefing-exam`).
- **Owner review points of this plan:** the card order (mode before level), the run view of the pad, the hint rule (optional steps never named), the hint line and sheet placement.

<objective>
Add the Training/Exam axis: its own card, a HINT and a CHECKLIST button in the session area during a Training run, a hint line that names the next open checklist step in the printed wording, and the checklist sheet. Exam stays silent.
Purpose: a learner can get help without the grade ever knowing about it.
Output: `hint.ts`, the mode state in the session, `ModePick.tsx`, `RunChecklist.tsx`, the run view of the pad, e2e helpers that pick the mode, tests and the grading note.
</objective>
