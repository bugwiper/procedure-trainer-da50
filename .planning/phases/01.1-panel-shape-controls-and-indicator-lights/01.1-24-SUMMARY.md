# Phase 01.1 Plan 24: Shared e2e zoom helper Summary

A shared e2e module, e2e/support/zoom.ts, now holds the background-point finder, the single and double click or tap helpers, zoomIntoRegion and leaveZoom; the breaker and zoom specs use it and the suite is unchanged in behaviour.

## What was done

- Task 1 (tracer, commit c05101e): created e2e/support/zoom.ts by moving the finder (logic unchanged) and the tap helpers out of the two specs. `zoomIntoRegion` leaves any zoom, taps once at a background point of the region, waits 120 ms, and taps the same point a second time only if the viewBox is still the full one; it then expects a viewBox that is not the full one. `leaveZoom` taps the Full panel button when it exists. breaker.spec.ts lost its private finder, its private activate helper and its zoom-in steps (`reach`, `zoomInto` now call `zoomIntoRegion`).
- Task 2 (commit d7db949): zoom.spec.ts lost backgroundPoint, activateAt, doubleActivateAt and activate. The setup-only zoom-ins (every region at full tap size, Full panel button above the footer, lever drag while zoomed, setup click of the background drag test) call `zoomIntoRegion`. The tests that assert the single-tap behaviour keep their steps and assertions and call `activateAt` and `doubleActivateAt` from the module.

## Compatibility branch (exists only until plan 25)

The helper contains one compatibility branch, marked with the line `COMPAT-UNTIL-PLAN-25`. Today a single tap already zooms, so the second tap is never sent in practice (the breaker states and event counts in the specs would show a stray toggle). Plan 25 changes the source to double click or double tap and then removes this branch, so the helper becomes strict.
