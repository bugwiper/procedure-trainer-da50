# Phase 01.1 Plan 33: Bus box titles readable on Safari Summary

ESSENTIAL BUS, MAIN BUS and AVIONICS BUS are now plain filled text over a plate-coloured rectangle that cuts the outline edge behind them, so no shell text carries a stroke any more (owner finding R3-1.1, confirmed on iPhone Safari).

## What and why

The titles used `stroke` (plate colour, width 5), `stroke-linejoin` and `paint-order="stroke"` to cut the outline edge. On WebKit a stroked text is painted glyph by glyph, so each stroke ran over the previous letter. The cut is now a rectangle:

- `bus-boxes.ts`: `BUS_TITLE_STYLE` is `{ fill, cut }` (no stroke width). New constants `BUS_TITLE_SIZE` 8, `BUS_TITLE_ADVANCE` 6, `BUS_TITLE_MARGIN` 4, `BUS_TITLE_CUT_ABOVE` 8, `BUS_TITLE_CUT_BELOW` 3 and the pure `busTitleCut(title)` returning `{x, y, w, h}` of Table B4.
- `BusPanelArt.tsx`: after outlines, pump boxes and plugs, each title is a pair: one `rect` with `data-shell="bus-title-cut"` in `#15171a` (no stroke) immediately followed by the `text` (fill `#c9d0d8`, size 8, anchor middle, no stroke, stroke width, stroke-linejoin or painting-order).

Final cut rectangles (global units, Table B4 numbers unchanged, no widening needed):

| Title | x | y | w | h |
|-------|---|---|---|---|
| ESSENTIAL BUS | 1953 | 218 | 86 | 11 |
| MAIN BUS | 1896 | 370 | 56 | 11 |
| AVIONICS BUS | 1920 | 472 | 80 | 11 |

Anchors (1996/226, 1924/378, 1960/480), outlines, plates, plugs, pump boxes and breakers are unchanged; counts stay 92 controls, 13 lamps, 30 regions (element count of the built panel 2043 to 2043 + 3 rectangles, budget 2500).

## Gates added

- `tests/bus-boxes.test.ts` (describe "bus panel title cuts"): width per Table B4 (86, 56, 80), edge line inside the cut with at least 2 units each side, centred on the anchor, wider than the glyph estimate by at least 6, no outline corner touched (edge minus corner radius minus 2), inside the bus-panel plate, no overlap with any of the breaker hit rectangles of the registry or a plug disc, style has no stroke width.
- `tests/panel-shell.test.ts`: whole expanded shell has no text element with `stroke`, `stroke-width`, `stroke-linejoin` or `paint-order`; titles are plain filled text at the earlier anchors; each title is preceded by exactly one cut rectangle with the plate colour (equal to the fill of the bus-panel plate in the shell) and no stroke; draw order plate, sub-panel, outlines, pump boxes, plugs, then the cut and title pairs.
- `e2e/art-fit.spec.ts` test "R3-1.1 ...": every shell text has computed stroke `none` and no `stroke` or `paint-order` attribute; the measured text box of each title lies inside its cut with at least 2 units each side, and the edge it cuts lies inside the cut. The test runs on every landscape project (the file's `beforeEach` skip for non-desktop-chromium projects was made title-aware; the portrait phone skips; no viewport change for this test) and needs no hook, so it also runs against the live site.

The unit gates were red first (25 failures), then green after the data and art change.
