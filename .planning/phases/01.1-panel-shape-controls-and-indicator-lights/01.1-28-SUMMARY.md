# Phase 01.1 Plan 28: FAN direct click spots Summary

The FAN now has four direct click spots, one per position, as transparent rectangles over the unchanged knob art: a tap on a position's mark sets that position at once. Half-stepping is gone for the FAN; dimmers and FUEL TRANS keep their stepping and drag.

## What was done

- Tests first for both tasks (registry rules, tapStop cases, FAN data, e2e spot taps, then layout, tap-size, mark and body-tap gates).
- Task 1 (tracer, commit 617d4bd): `RotaryDef.spots` (list of `{ stop, x, y, w, h }`, knob-local) with `checkRotarySpots` in `defineRegistry` (one spot per stop, no unknown stop, finite and positive sides, inside the footprint, pairwise disjoint, touching allowed). `tapStop` and `MIN_RADIUS` in `src/engine/rotary.ts` (the constant moved out of the widget). The Rotary widget keeps `RotaryBody` untouched, draws one `data-detent="sw.env_fan:<stop>"` rectangle per spot with the same pointer handlers as the base hit, and on a tap under 4 px of travel actuates `tapStop` of the pointer-down point (null does nothing; the store rejects an unchanged state, so no event). `targetSide` returns the smallest spot side for a knob with spots. The tracer gate (`npx vitest run`, three-project rotary e2e, typecheck) was green before Task 2.
- Task 2 (commit bbedb9d): layout gates (footprint inside env_control and the plate, at global x 964..1076 and y 889..975, no overlap with any other control footprint, every spot inside the footprint and holding its mark, no spot reaching another footprint), the tap-size gate per spot on all three profiles, the e2e naming of the four spot rectangles in the every-region test, tap-by-coordinates e2e for each mark and body point, and the art-unchanged assertions in the knob text gate.
