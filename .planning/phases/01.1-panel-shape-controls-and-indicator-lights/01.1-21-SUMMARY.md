## Deferral: the 120 s MAX run (engine phase)

`lt.deice_max` lights only while the flag `deice.max_active` is true and the mode is HIGH. Nothing sets the flag in this phase (the bridge's flag record is empty, exactly like `gear.in_transit`), so the top lamp never lights. Reason: the run lasts 120 s and phase 01.1 has no engine clock; the engine and the UI may not hold a timer. Intended engine behaviour, recorded in the comment of `DEICE_MAX_ACTIVE` in `src/aircraft/da50rg/indicators.ts`: pressing `btn.deice_max` while the mode is HIGH sets the flag for 120 s on the virtual clock, then clears it and ignores further MAX presses until MAX is pressed again; leaving HIGH clears it.
