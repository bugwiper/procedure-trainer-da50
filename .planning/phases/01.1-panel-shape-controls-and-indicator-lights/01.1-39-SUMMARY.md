# Phase 01.1 Plan 39: Momentary controls commit after 100 ms, drag controls keep their gesture Summary

The six momentary controls (ENGINE START, FADEC TEST, STALL TEST / GEAR TEST, DE-ICE MAX, WIND SHIELD, LEVEL) no longer own a pointer: a press commits after MOMENTARY_HOLD_MS (100 ms) held still, a quick lift is press then release, and a press that moves beyond the slop or gains a second finger before the commit is discarded and becomes a pan or a pinch with no event. The lever handles and knob bodies are registered as own targets with the shared DRAG_START_PX, so they keep their drag but a second finger can still pinch before the drag has travelled that far (owner finding R3-Z2 items 2, 3 and 5).

## What changed

- `MomentaryButton.tsx`: `activePointer`, `setPointerCapture`, every pointer handler and the local release are gone. `makeMomentaryPress({def, isLeftHalf, actuate})` returns a `momentary` target: `press(info)` actuates the first pressed state, or for the three-state spring toggle the state of the half under `info.down` (`isLeftHalf(info)` decides, left is the first side) and returns the event sequence number; `release()` actuates the rest state; `discard()` does nothing. The hit rect registers it by ref callback in the render body. The pressed side is still read back from the store state; the push-button skins are untouched.
- `zoom.ts`: one `timer`. After every machine call (down, move, up, cancel and the tick itself) `schedule(now)` clears it and, if `nextDeadline()` reports a deadline, sets one `setTimeout` with delay `max(0, deadline - eventTimeStamp)` whose callback calls `arbiter.tick(deadline)` (the deadline value, not the wall clock), applies the effects and reschedules from the deadline. Detach clears it. New effect cases: `press` calls `target.press({down, up: down, hit})` and resets the double-tap pair, `release` calls `target.release()`. `discard` already reached `target.discard()` for every class. `syncCapture` skips the svg capture for a pending own pointer (see decisions).
- `DetentLever.tsx`: `MIN_DRAG_PX` is gone, `DRAG_START_PX` is imported from `press-arbiter.ts`. `LeverPress` gains `handle`, the ref callback of the handle hit, which `DragLever` fills with an own target `{cls: 'own', dragStartPx: DRAG_START_PX, discard: clearDrag}`. The handle keeps its own handlers and capture.
- `Rotary.tsx`: `MIN_DRAG_PX` is gone, the body hit registers `{cls: 'own', dragStartPx: DRAG_START_PX, discard: clear}`; handlers and capture unchanged.
- Tests: `tests/button-skins.test.ts` adds the `makeMomentaryPress` describe (a momentary target without tap or setPending, two-state press and release for every two-state button, left half stall_test and right half gear_test and release neutral, the info of the press reaches `isLeftHalf`, discard changes nothing, a rejected dispatch gives null). `tests/lever-skins.test.ts` passes the new `handle` ref in the body-asks-for-refs test.
- e2e (`e2e/press.spec.ts`, all via `data-state` and the viewBox except the `@hooks` describe):
  - Table M1, all six controls, per control: mouse and touch hold gives the pressed state and the lift rests; a press moved beyond the slop before the hold pans the zoomed view and never presses (mouse, touch); a second finger before the hold pinches and never presses (touch); after the commit a far move does not pan, the state stays pressed and the lift releases (mouse, touch); after the commit a second finger on the background is ignored and the view stays the full one (touch).
  - `@hooks`: a quick tap records pressed then released (desktop, phone, tablet), a moved or pinched press records no event, two quick ENGINE START presses record pressed, released, pressed, released and leave the view unchanged.
  - Drag controls: mouse drag from the throttle handle moves it to p90 without panning the zoomed view; mouse drag from the INSTRUMENT LIGHT knob body turns it to s75 without panning, a tap steps it on release; phone: an unmoved finger on the throttle plus a second finger on the background pinches and the throttle and its handle transform stay at p70; phone: a knob body dragged past DRAG_START_PX plus a second finger leaves the view full and the knob snaps to s75 on the lift.
  - Classification gate (Table M2, every project except phone-portrait, live-safe): every `[data-hit]` and `[data-detent]` has the `data-press` class of its control kind, none missing, each of momentary, own and latch non-zero.
- `e2e/button.spec.ts` needed no change: its held cases already use polling assertions, its click and tap cases end at rest.

## Assumptions taken

- Quick lift before the hold commits press and release together (the plan's planner decision).
- A drag control can be taken from its finger by a second finger only before DRAG_START_PX of travel (the plan's planner decision).
- INSTRUMENT LIGHT (a plain body-hit knob) and the throttle lever lv.power stand for the knob and lever drag cases; the classification gate covers every other drag control.
- A lift of only the first finger cannot be sent with the shared touch helper (it numbers touch ids by index), so the "after the commit a second finger is ignored" case lifts both fingers with `touchEnd []` and asserts the release; the machine's rule that the first pointer's lift releases is covered by tests/press-arbiter.test.ts.
- The "still rest right after the press" assertion of the mouse hold case runs only when that read really happened within half the hold time, so a slow runner cannot flake it; the same rule is covered deterministically by the machine unit tests.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Svg capture of a pending own pointer would drop its record**
- **Found during:** Task 2 design (reading `syncCapture` for the own class).
- **Issue:** `syncCapture` captured every pending pointer with a target on the svg. For an own target the widget then calls `setPointerCapture` on its hit element in its own pointerdown handler; the capture moving off the svg fires `lostpointercapture` on the svg, which the layer treats as a cancel and the machine forgets an own record. A second finger would then no longer pinch.
- **Fix:** a pending pointer with an own target is not captured by the layer; it is captured only when the machine takes it over as a view pointer.
- **Files modified:** src/ui/panel/zoom.ts
- **Commit:** 6ebc13a

**2. [Test adjustment] lever body test asks for the handle ref too**
- **Issue:** `LeverPress` gained a required `handle` field, so the test object failed the type check.
- **Fix:** the test passes `handle: () => undefined`; the assertions are the same (one ref per marker, none for the drag-only lever).
- **Files modified:** tests/lever-skins.test.ts
- **Commit:** 6a882fb
