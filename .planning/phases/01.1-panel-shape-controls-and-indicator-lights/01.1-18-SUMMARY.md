# Phase 01.1 Plan 18: Three-position toggle, plate placards and the ELT Summary

The ELT is now the owner's three-position small red rocker (RESET / TEST bottom, ARMED middle, ON top) with three static placards beside it and a small red lamp on its left that lights in ON; three-position toggles and data-driven plate placards exist as general mechanisms for plans 19 to 22.

## What was built

- **Task 1 (tracer, commit 3bd3195):** `ToggleDef.states` is a two- or three-element tuple (`ToggleStates`). The registry rejects three states on the softkey skin, any state count other than two on a guarded toggle, four states, duplicate ids and an illegal default. `targetSide` of a three-state toggle is `min(w, h / 3)`. The Toggle widget derives the drawn position from the state index (`low`; `mid`; `high`) and takes the target state of a tap from where the pointer went down inside the hit element's own bounding rectangle (top third the top state, middle third the middle one, bottom third the bottom one); a two-state toggle still flips on any tap, and a tap on the position already held calls no `actuate` (no event). `data-pressed-half` is `upper`, `level` or `lower`. `sw.elt`: three states, default `armed`, rocker S red, hit 34 by 78 at the unchanged centre, reference row still 1776, 656. Pointer contract unchanged (primary pointer, capture on down, actuate on up within the slop, no click listener).
- **Task 2 (commit de93a77):** `placards.ts` holds `PlacardDef` and the three ELT placards (global start x 1798, size 6: ON at y 630, ARMED at 656, RESET / TEST as two lines at 679 and 686, aligned with the three tap thirds); `PanelShell` draws them after the plates with one `data-placard` text per line. `IndicatorSpec.caption` accepts `none` and `LampBody` draws no caption for it; the halo radius is `haloRadius(r) = r + 6` (13 for r 7, 10 for r 4). `lt.elt` (red, r 4, local 32, 43, reads `sw.elt`, lit when `on`) is the eighth lamp. Reference row (1752, 656, tolerance 8, review true) and the ordering rows lt.elt left of and same row as sw.elt added. `right_panel` left the art-fit open list and the layout footprint open list.
