## Phase goal

The panel looks like the real DA50 RG with every control in kind-of real shape and colour, and the owner's live-site review (01.1-OWNER-FINDINGS-R2.md) is closed: a click or tap never zooms by accident, the Full panel button never covers a control, and the parking brake, the FAN, the engine strip and the light switches match the aircraft.

<objective>
Replace the single-tap zoom behaviour by a double click or double tap on the background (owner finding R2-Z1): a lone click or tap does nothing, a pair on the background zooms into the tapped region and zooms out again, a pair on a control is two control taps, pinch and pan stay.

Purpose: R2-Z1 (a tap that just misses a switch zoomed immediately and a following tap zoomed out again, which is disruptive while operating controls), PNL-15, PNL-06 and Phase 1 criterion 4 (neutral taps change nothing). This supersedes D-12 (single tap while zoomed zooms fully out) and the single-tap-to-zoom-in rule of Phase 1 plan 09 and of plan 01.1-07.
Output: pure double-tap rules with a tracker, the gesture layer using them, unit tests for the rules, a rewritten zoom spec, a strict e2e helper, adapted avionics and neutral specs.
</objective>
