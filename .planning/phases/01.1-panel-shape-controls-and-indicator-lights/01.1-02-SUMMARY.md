# Phase 01.1 Plan 02: Panel shell, brand marks and type scale Summary

**The panel now sits on a static, handler-free flat-vector shell (face plate, leather coaming with the glareshield hump, console, bezels, plates, bus brackets, vent, placards) with GO-AROUND, RUBIN and DA50RG as the only brand text, and all glass text uses the four sizes 6, 8, 10, 14.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 8 min (2026-10-20T15:16Z to 15:24Z)
- **Tasks:** 3 (Task 1 tracer, Task 2 auto tdd, Task 3 auto)
- **Files modified:** 13 plus the new vnode helper

## Accomplishments

- `Defs.tsx` defines eleven paints once (`g-plate`, `g-console`, `g-coaming`, `g-chrome`, `g-brushed`, `g-silver`, three radial halos, `p-carbon`, `p-hatch`), each with at most three stops. Later plans only reference them through `paint()`.
- `PanelShell` draws layers 2 to 12 of the UI-SPEC plus the environmental-control plate, under the regions, with `pointer-events="none"` and no handler, `data-hit` or `data-detent`. `Panel.tsx` renders it after the background rect (still the first child) and `Defs`.
- Region boxes and captions are no longer visible: each region keeps an invisible `data-region-frame` rect; `?regions=1` draws the Phase 1 frames and 10-unit captions.
- Brand marks: GO-AROUND on both bezels at region-local (w/2, -6), RUBIN text only, DA50RG nameplate. `e2e/brand.spec.ts` passes on all six projects (counts 2, 1, 1, no real brand name, no image element). `tests/brand.test.ts` scans `src/ui/panel`, `src/aircraft` for the real names and `src` for images and data URLs.
- `tests/panel-shell.test.ts` (50 tests): shell purity, forbidden tags (image, filter, mask, blur, drop shadow, foreignObject), only defined `url(#id)` references, the eleven ids, at most three stops, all layers and placard texts, and the font-size scale scan.
- Playwright takes `PW_PORT` (default 4173), builds the e2e bundle and starts `vite preview` on that port with `--strictPort`, and reuses an existing server only when neither CI nor PW_PORT is set.
- Glass sizes: 9, 11, 12 are now 10 and 13, 16, 20, 22 are now 14 in the four frame files.
- `docs/CONTENT-POLICY.md` section 7 states the brand replacement rule without naming the real brands.
