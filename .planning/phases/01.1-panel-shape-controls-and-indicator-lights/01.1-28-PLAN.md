## Phase goal

The panel looks like the real DA50 RG with every control in kind-of real shape and colour, and the owner's live-site review (01.1-OWNER-FINDINGS-R2.md) is closed: a click or tap never zooms by accident, the Full panel button never covers a control, and the parking brake, the FAN, the engine strip and the light switches match the aircraft.

<objective>
Make the FAN easy to operate (R2-4.1): its four positions become direct click spots, so a tap on a position's mark sets that position at once, while the knob art, the pointer angle and the stops stay.

Purpose: R2-4.1 (rotary tap-half stepping and drag are hard to hit), PNL-11, D-09, D-07 (works by mouse or touch), D-21 and PNL-09 (every control reaches 44 px when its region is zoomed). Three upper spots side by side need 3 x 36.4 units, so the FAN footprint grows from 80 by 40 to about 112 by 86 (Table F1); the planner checked that it still lies on the environmental plate and clear of the DIRECTION and TEMPERATURE footprints.
Output: the spots field with its checks, the pure tapStop rule, the Rotary widget with spot rectangles, the FAN data, the tap-size rule for spots, updated unit, layout and e2e tests.
</objective>
