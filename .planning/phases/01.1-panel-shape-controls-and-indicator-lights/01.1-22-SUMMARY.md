# Phase 01.1 Plan 22: De-ice unit, lower row, ALTERNATE and heading Summary

The de-ice unit now follows the owner's photo from top to bottom: framed DE ICE heading, MAX, the HIGH / NORM / OFF toggle and three lamps, ALTERNATE as stacked letters beside a normal toggle under a red guard, and a lower row of a black WIND SHIELD button, a three-position ICE LIGHT / ANNUN-TEST toggle and a two-way PUMP toggle. Spray, self-test and ice light have no simulated effect (they come with the scenarios).

## What was built

- **Task 1 (tracer, commit d045230):** `sw.deice_ice_light` is a nameless three-position bat (annun_test, off, ice_light bottom to top, default off, hit 30 x 69 at region-local 86, 35). `btn.deice_annun_test` is gone. `BAT_THROW` (14) replaces the footprint-dependent throw. Placards ICE LIGHT (above), OFF (right of the middle) and ANNUN- / TEST (two lines below). Reference rows, ordering rows, unit and e2e cases follow; the review document names the new rows.
- **Task 2 (commit e8c433e):** `sw.deice_pump` (pump1 down, pump2 up, default pump1, nameless, hit 30 x 69 at 142, 35) replaces the two pump toggles; `btn.deice_windshield` (black-dome, hit 36 x 36 at 28, 35) replaces the WIND SHIELD toggle; `buttonTextLayout` splits a label that is still too wide at size 6 at its space; PUMP 2 / PUMP 1 placards; the compact bat variant and `COMPACT_BAT_HEIGHT` are removed. 41 non-guard controls.
- **Task 3 (commit 163dfa6):** `sw.deice_alternate` has no rocker size and colour, so `GuardedToggle` draws `BatBody` (`data-skin="bat"`); states on, off (off, the up position, is the default); hit 52 x 100; the guard cover is rebuilt (closed: red block over the footprint inset 2 with three hatch bars; open: a flap above the footprint with the hinge on the footprint top); ALTERNATE as nine stacked letters (placard with `pitch` 7.2); `PLATE_HEADINGS` and `HEADING_FRAME` in `plates.ts`, drawn by `PanelShell`. Red gate: rockers are exactly ELECT. MASTER and ELT, the red of the unit is the guard cover only.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Tap thirds of 22 units fail the pinch-in tap-size gate**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** with the starting hit of 66 the toggle's thirds are 22 units; `tests/zoom.test.ts` ("a MIN_VIEW_WIDTH-wide view brings every control to 44 px") gave 43.2 px. The region-zoom gate passed, the pinch-in gate did not.
- **Fix:** hit 30 x 69 for both toggles (thirds of 23, 45.2 px), centre y 35 (region-local); Table J1 allows adjusting the numbers until the gates pass. `MIN_VIEW_WIDTH` was left alone (it is shared by every control).
- **Files modified:** src/aircraft/da50rg/controls/deice.ts and the tests naming the size
- **Commit:** d045230

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] The fixed bat throw changes the engine-area bats**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** the old throw was min(14, (h - 37) / 2 - 1); a footprint-independent throw is what the plan asks for, and bats of 60 tall (FADEC, alternators, ENGINE MASTER) had 10.5.
- **Fix:** one constant `BAT_THROW = 14` for every bat; the handle and cap still lie inside every footprint (asserted for the 69-tall toggles).
- **Files modified:** src/ui/panel/skins/Bat.tsx
- **Commit:** d045230

**3. [Rule 3 - Blocking] `PlacardDef` needed a line pitch of its own**
- **Found during:** Task 3
- **Issue:** the stacked letters are 7.2 apart, the placard mechanism only knew 7 (size 6) and 10 (size 8).
- **Fix:** optional `pitch` on `PlacardDef`, used by `placardLineY`; all other placards unchanged.
- **Files modified:** src/aircraft/da50rg/placards.ts
- **Commit:** 163dfa6

**4. [Rule 3 - Blocking] The panel review completeness gate and a toggle e2e case**
- **Found during:** Tasks 1 to 3
- **Issue:** `tests/panel-review.test.ts` requires every review-flagged row and every UI-SPEC unconfirmed id (`btn.deice_annun_test`) to be named in docs/PANEL-REVIEW.md; `e2e/toggle.spec.ts` flips every plain toggle with one centre tap, which a three-position toggle does not do.
- **Fix:** de-ice rows of docs/PANEL-REVIEW.md rewritten (removed ids named in one line, PD-08 kept as superseded); `sw.deice_ice_light` excluded from the plain-toggle case next to `sw.elt` and `sw.deice_mode`.
- **Files modified:** docs/PANEL-REVIEW.md, e2e/toggle.spec.ts (not in the plan's file list)
- **Commits:** d045230, e8c433e, 163dfa6
