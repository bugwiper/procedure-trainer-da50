# Phase 01.1 Plan 29: Engine strip FADEC and ALTERNATOR groups Summary

The engine strip now reads as in the aircraft (R2-5.2): FADEC and ALTERNATOR each have a curved bracket line with a heading, AUTO, FORCE B, TEST, ON and OFF stand around the switches, and the alternators carry framed numerals 1 and 2, all as static plate text while the four controls print nothing themselves.

## What was done

- Tests first in each task (layout geometry, shell drawing, button and bat text gates, e2e on the built panel), then the implementation.
- Task 1 (tracer, commit ec70971): `sw.fadec` and `btn.fadec_test` are nameless; `RoundBody` of the push button skins prints no label for a nameless button (every other button is byte for byte as before; `buttonTextLayout` is untouched). `sw.fadec` states are ordered `force_b`, `auto` (default `auto`). Placards `fadec.auto`, `fadec.force_b`, `fadec.test`. `BRACKET_GROUPS` plate data and the shell drawing (`bracketPath`, `glyphWidth`, a `BracketGroups` group in the left strip, the title text carries `data-shell="bracket-title"`). The tracer gate (vitest, four-spec three-project e2e, typecheck) passed before Task 2.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Plan latitude, geometry] ON and OFF reach into the two alternator hit footprints**
- **Found during:** Task 2 test design
- **Issue:** The plan says ON and OFF may stand between the footprints "but over none". The alternator footprints are 70 wide at x 134 and 210, leaving a 6 unit gap (x 169 to 175); OFF is 11.2 units wide at size 6, so its glyph box reaches 2.6 units into each footprint (ON 0.7 units). Without shrinking a hit rectangle (prohibited) or moving a control (prohibited) the two words cannot be narrower.
- **Fix:** The gate checks the real intent: ON and OFF cover no visible bat art (ring r 7 and handle throw 14), no footprint of any other strip control, and reach at most 3 units into an alternator footprint. The shell takes no pointer events, so a tap on a word still reaches the switch.
- **Files modified:** tests/layout.test.ts
- **Commit:** f57e667

**2. [Test fixture] The e2e word counts are taken on the engine plate**
- **Found during:** Task 1 e2e run
- **Issue:** TEST also appears elsewhere on the panel (STALL TEST, GEAR TEST, ANNUN- TEST, RESET / TEST), so a panel-wide count gave 5.
- **Fix:** The e2e counts texts whose centre lies on the engine plate and checks each lies inside it.
- **Files modified:** e2e/static-text.spec.ts
- **Commit:** ec70971
