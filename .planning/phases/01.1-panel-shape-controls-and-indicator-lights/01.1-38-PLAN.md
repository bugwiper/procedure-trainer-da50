## Owner decisions this plan builds (R3-Z2, 2026-10-21, all approved by the owner)

1. Latching controls (toggles, rockers, bats, circuit breakers, the flap levers' marker taps, FAN spots, the guard cover, the tap-toggle parking brake) act on RELEASE, not on press: a press is pending; if the pointer lifts within the slop (10 px touch, 5 px mouse) it is a tap and actuates; if it moves beyond the slop it is a pan (when zoomed) and the control discards the press; if a second pointer lands (pinch) the pending press is discarded and the pinch or pan proceeds, also over controls.
3. Drag controls (throttle, EMERGENCY GEAR EXTENSION, the gear and flaps lever handles, knob drags) keep their gesture; panning starts on the background or with two fingers.
4. Mouse: the same arbitration with the 5 px slop.
5. Two-finger pinch and pan work everywhere, including over controls.
Planner reading of "flaps" in item 1 versus item 3: the marker rectangles of a lever (tap to jump to a detent) are latching taps; the handle rectangle (drag along the track) is a drag control. Both belong to one widget; the split is by element. Recorded as an assumption for the owner's Round 5 review.
