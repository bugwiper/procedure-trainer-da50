## Overall finding: zoom gestures (supersedes the single-tap behaviour of plan 01.1-07 and the tap-to-zoom-region of Phase 1)

- **R2-Z1:** A single click or tap on the panel must NOT zoom (neither in nor out). Reason: a tap that just misses a switch's hit area zooms immediately (and a following tap zooms out again), which is disruptive while operating controls.
- **Wanted gestures:** the normal two-finger pinch zoom (and pan), plus a **double click** (mouse) / **double tap** (touch) to zoom in on the tapped area, and a double click / double tap again to zoom back out. The "Full panel" button stays.
- Effects: `src/ui/panel/zoom.ts` and `tap-intent.ts` (single-tap arbitration removed, double-tap/double-click introduced; careful not to swallow control taps: a double tap on a control still counts as two control taps, so zoom-by-double-tap should apply on the background and between controls only, or decide and record), `tests/zoom.test.ts`, `e2e/zoom.spec.ts`, ROADMAP success criteria wording and REQUIREMENTS PNL-15 ("single tap zooms out" must be reworded), README, `docs/PANEL-REVIEW.md`.
- **Decision (owner):** double click / double tap zoom applies ONLY on the background (between controls, not on a control's hit area). A double tap on a control keeps counting as two control taps and never zooms.
