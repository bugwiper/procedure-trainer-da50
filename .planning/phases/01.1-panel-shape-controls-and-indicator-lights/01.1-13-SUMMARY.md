# Phase 01.1 Plan 13: Static toggle family and rocker side perspective Summary

Every rocker, bat, softkey and guard cover is now free of state-dependent text and the green status dot; rockers show their position by perspective (flat pressed half, standing raised half with a darker side wall), and tests/static-text.test.ts gates that the text of the toggle family never changes with state.

## What was built

- **Task 1 (tracer, commit 01a6b5a):** `TogglePosition` in the registry; `RockerBody` takes `position` (low, mid, high) and prints only its name (below the bezel on the LIGHT size, above on the others). The raised half is drawn displaced with a `polygon` wall tagged `data-rocker-wall="upper|lower"` in the wall tone (`#8a897f` white, `#5a0a0f` red); the middle position draws both halves at half lift with two walls. Toggle and GuardedToggle derive the position from the store state; `data-state`, `data-pressed-half`, `data-skin` and the aria-label are unchanged. A screenshot at 4x scale confirmed ON and OFF light rockers differ at a glance.
- **Task 2 (commit 7ef10d8):** `BatBody` takes `position` (down, up, or the short stub for mid), loses its state text and the green dot; `SoftkeyBody` prints its legend only; `GuardCover` prints nothing; `sw.mfd_page` is labelled ENGINE with states NOT SELECTED and SELECTED (Q-10 default). The text gate covers rockers, bats, softkey and guard cover. The e2e specs assert `data-state` instead of the state text.

## Deviations from Plan

None in behavior. Two small implementation notes:

1. **[Design] rockerExtent and the lift:** adding the lift on top of the 4-unit bezel pad pushed the LIGHT rockers (hit 36 wide) past their hit rect. The extent is now `max(bezel, face + lift)`, so the 3-unit lift is absorbed by the existing 4-unit bezel pad and every rocker stays inside its footprint. Test asserts the extent is at least face plus lift.
2. **[Test] e2e switch-skins:** the one remaining state-text assertion was replaced by `sw.locator('text')` having count 1 (the name), so `grep -c data-state-text` is 0 as required.

## Assumptions taken

- Q-10 default of docs/PANEL-LABELS.md for the MFD softkey (label ENGINE, states NOT SELECTED and SELECTED); ids and default unchanged.
- The data-pressed-half value for mid is not produced yet (plan 18 adds it); two-state toggles never reach mid.
