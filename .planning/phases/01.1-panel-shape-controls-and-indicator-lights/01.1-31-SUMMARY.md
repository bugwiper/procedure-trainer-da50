## WebKit findings (recorded in Round 4 item 7, not fixed)

1. **Full panel button catches the second tap (8 failures, phone-webkit only).** After the second tap of the double tap on the background of `cb_essential` the view does zoom in (viewBox 1874 205 280 172), then WebKit delivers a synthesized `click` to the Full panel button that just appeared at the top right, 9 px from the tap point, and the view returns to full. Pointer timing was fine (pointerup at 115 and 240 ms). Affects `e2e/breaker.spec.ts` (five cases and the button gate) and `e2e/zoom.spec.ts` (two cases). desktop-webkit, tablet-webkit and Chromium pass. Possible real-iPhone effect for a double tap within about 10 px of where the button appears: owner to try. Fix options: the button ignores a click within about 400 ms of appearing (`src/ui/App.tsx`), or the e2e background point keeps away from the future button corner (`e2e/support/zoom.ts`).
2. **FAN f3 glyph centre on a spot edge (4 failures, all four WebKit projects).** `@hooks FAN: a tap on each mark and on the body` taps the exact glyph centre (19.1, -11), 0.6 units (0.3 px) from the f2 and f3 boundary; WebKit resolves it to f2, Chromium to f3. A point 5 units further out gives f3 everywhere. A test-point fragility, not a usability defect; the fix is two numbers in `e2e/rotary.spec.ts`.

Both were left unfixed: the plan allows changing only `e2e/glass.spec.ts` and no `src/` file, and the one-line-fix clause of the WebKit step does not override that.
