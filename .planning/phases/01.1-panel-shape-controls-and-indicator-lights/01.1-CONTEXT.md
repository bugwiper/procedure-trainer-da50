# Phase 01.1: Panel shape, controls and indicator lights - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-20
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Turn the Phase 1 demo panel into a near-photographic 2D replica of the DA50 RG panel's shape and **hardware controls**: the instrument panel outline, the centre console, the under-panel areas, and every switch, rocker, push button, lever and knob at its real position, in kind-of real shape and real colour. The light switches are white rockers and movable. The gear and flap indicator lights work. Brand marks are replaced. The two zoom usability findings from the Phase 1 UAT are fixed.

**Not in this phase (Phase 01.2):** circuit breakers, the G1000 NXi PFD/MFD shape with softkeys, audio panel, autopilot panel, backup EFIS and other static avionics. **Not in this phase (Phase 3):** gear/flap transition timing and live gauge values.

The reason for the split: every later phase must build on a settled layout. Putting all controls in place now, instead of adding them scenario by scenario, avoids heavy panel rework and keeps scenarios honest (a pilot who sees only one circuit breaker to pull learns nothing).

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Target and source material
- **D-01:** The target look is a near-photographic replica of the real panel, as in the DA62 photo `.input/DA50_default_view_template.jpeg` ("same layout but engine stuff is double"). The DA50 RG has one engine, so the DA62's second-engine controls and gauges are NOT replicated. This **supersedes D-10 (flat schematic look)** and **D-07 (small demonstrative control set)** from Phase 1.
- **D-02:** Panel shape scope is **instrument panel and centre console only**: panel outline, glareshield lights, left switch strip, the bus panel on the right, the under-panel row, and the centre console (environmental control, power lever, parking brake, alternate air). No sidesticks, no canopy or cockpit surroundings.
- **D-03:** Accuracy source: the owner provides **straight-on, well-lit close-ups** (right bus panel, left switch strip, centre column, pedestal) in `.input/`. Until they arrive, the planner works from the existing photos in `.input/` and lists every label or position it is unsure of for the owner to correct after the first render. Positions that rest on guesses must be marked as unconfirmed in the layout reference.
- **D-04:** Layout fidelity remains the core value (D-19, D-21, PNL-08): true relative positions and proportions win over tap size at full-panel view; the phone region zoom stays the answer for small targets.

### Brand marks and IP
- **D-05:** Garmin and Diamond marks are replaced, not copied: **GARMIN becomes GO-AROUND** (as an easter egg), **DIAMOND becomes RUBIN**, **DA50RG stays**. No logos or wordmarks of the real brands appear in the repo or the live site.
- **D-06:** Reference photos stay private in `.input/` (git-ignored). The owner has said copyright is not a concern for now and will double-check before any commercial activity. Redraw everything as original SVG; do not embed, trace or auto-vectorise the photos (D-04b stands). The hygiene gate (no PDF or `.input/` file tracked) stays in force.

### Controls and interaction
- **D-07:** All hardware controls are **interactive**: every switch, rocker, toggle, push button, lever and knob changes state and shows it by position plus small state text (D-13). The G1000 knobs and the audio and autopilot panels are visible but inert (Phase 01.2). The only guarded switch remains the de-ice ALTERNATE (D-07b).
- **D-08:** Light switches (landing, taxi, strobe, position, and so on) are **white rocker switches and movable**. Rocker size and colour follow the aircraft: ELECT. MASTER big red rocker, ELT small red rocker, ESS BUS white rocker (owner corrected 2026-10-20 after photos: only ELECT. MASTER and ELT are red), the others white; the alternator switches are already accurate and serve as the style reference.
- **D-09:** Controls get their real physical **kinds and proportions** (rocker, toggle, push button, knob, slider/lever) with kind-of real shape and real colour. The registry's typed kinds (toggle, guarded-toggle, detent-lever, momentary-button) are extended only as needed; each new kind needs a widget, legal states and layout tests.
- **D-10:** The full control inventory goes in **now** (all hardware switches, levers, buttons and knobs that exist on the real panel), not scenario by scenario. Controls the owner has not yet photographed are listed as open items rather than guessed.

### Indicator lights
- **D-11 (corrected by owner 2026-10-20):** The gear indicator is **three separate green lights (each green or off; all three green = down and locked) plus one separate red light (red or off; on for unsafe or in transition); all off = up and locked.** The flap lights follow flap state. Phase 01.1 draws all light states and binds them to the gear and flap state. **Transition timing (the red phase while moving) is deferred to Phase 3** together with scenario timing; until then a lever change settles immediately.

### UX fixes (from Phase 1 UAT)
- **D-12:** A single tap on the panel background **while zoomed zooms fully out**, including from intermediate zoom levels, in addition to the existing double-tap, pinch-out and button. Drags and pinches must not trigger it; taps on controls still belong to the control. Update `tests/zoom.test.ts` and `e2e/zoom.spec.ts`. (Todo: `2026-10-20-single-tap-zoom-out.md`.)
- **D-13:** The "Full panel" button must never be covered by the footer notice on any device, including a landscape phone; add a hit-test check on the landscape phone projects. (Todo: `2026-10-20-full-panel-button-hidden-landscape.md`.)

### Claude's Discretion
- Exact SVG construction, gradients and shading used to make shapes look near-real, as long as they stay original vector art and state remains readable without colour.
- How the layout reference table in `tests/layout.test.ts` grows to cover the full inventory (grouping, tolerances).
- Whether the existing coordinate space is rescaled to fit the real panel aspect ratio, provided the region data model and zoom behaviour keep working.

### Folded Todos
- **Single tap on panel background while zoomed should zoom fully out** (D-12).
- **"Full panel" button is covered by the footer disclaimer on a landscape phone** (D-13).
- **Rework switch appearance to match the real DA50** (D-08, D-09).

<code_context>
## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets
- `src/engine/registry.ts` (`defineRegistry`): typed control kinds with invariants (kind-prefix rule sw./lv./btn., detents, legal states); new kinds hook in here.
- `src/ui/panel/ControlWidget.tsx`: exhaustive switch over kinds ending in `unsupported(def: never)`, so adding a kind is a compile error until a widget exists.
- Widgets: `Toggle.tsx` (exports `SwitchBody`), `GuardedToggle.tsx`, `MomentaryButton.tsx`, `DetentLever.tsx` — the shapes to rework for real rocker/knob/slider looks.
- `src/ui/panel/zoom.ts` and `viewport.ts`: region zoom, pinch, pan and gesture arbitration; the single-tap zoom-out change goes here.
- `tests/layout.test.ts`: reference table that fails when a control is misplaced; grows with the inventory.

### Established Patterns
- State lives in the store, not the DOM; every control has a stable id and defined legal states; taps on neutral areas record no event.
- One root SVG viewBox scaled to fit; regions are data (`{id, name, bounds, controlIds[]}`).
- Hit-area `[data-hit]` and `[data-detent]` DOM contract; widgets expose `data-state-text`.
- B612 / B612 Mono fonts self-hosted; no third-party requests.

### Integration Points
- The registry and `panel.ts` feed the renderer; new regions and controls plug in there.
- Indicator lights need a **derived-indicator mechanism** (an output computed from other control state, with no user input), which does not exist yet.
- `e2e/*.spec.ts` projects (desktop, tablet, landscape and portrait phones) must keep passing; the Playwright live check runs against the Pages URL.

</code_context>
