## Phase goal

The panel looks like the real DA50 RG: instrument panel outline, centre console, and every switch, rocker, push button, lever and knob at its real position in kind-of real shape and real colour; light switches move; gear and flap lights work; brand marks replaced; the two zoom findings fixed. Every later phase builds on this settled layout.

This plan delivers the two zoom usability fixes from the Phase 1 UAT.
