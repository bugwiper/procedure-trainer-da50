# Phase 01.1 Plan 37: Toggles, breakers and guards act on release Summary

Toggles, rockers, bats, softkey toggles, circuit breakers, the guard cover and the guarded switch no longer own their pointers: a press is pending (the pressed ring shows, nothing is recorded), a lift within the slop (10 px touch and pen, 5 px mouse) actuates once, movement beyond the slop turns the pointer into a pan (zoomed) or nothing (full view), and a second finger discards the press and pinches, also over controls (owner finding R3-Z2 items 1, 4 and 5).

## What changed

- `src/ui/panel/press-targets.ts` (new): the registry of Table T1. `registerPressTarget` stores the target in a WeakMap and writes `data-press` (latch, momentary or own) on the element, ignoring a null element; `pressKeyOf` gives data-detent, else data-hit; `seqOf` maps a dispatch result to its sequence number or null. Only a type import from the store.
- `src/ui/panel/zoom.ts`: a pointerdown is classified by the registered target (an unregistered hit stays own with dragStartPx 0, the rest is background). A per-pointer record keeps target and hit element until the machine drops the pointer. Effects: pending calls `setPending`; discard calls `setPending(false)` and `discard`; a tap with a key calls `tap({down, up, hit})` and resets the double-tap tracker. The pointer is captured on the svg while its press is pending (registered targets only) or it is a view pointer. Everything else of the gesture layer is unchanged.
- `Toggle.tsx`, `CircuitBreaker.tsx`, `GuardedToggle.tsx`: pointer handler props, `setPointerCapture`, local slop constants and `PointerStart` are gone. Pure exported factories build the targets; thirds of a three-state toggle are measured on `info.hit.getBoundingClientRect` at the down point. The guarded switch hit still exists only while the guard is open; the store interlock still rejects a switch tap with the guard closed (the factory returns null).
- Tests: factory cases in `tests/switch-skins.test.ts` (two-state flip, three thirds, current third null, sequence numbers, rejection, setPending untouched, source greps) and `tests/breakers.test.ts` (in to out, out to in, rejection, real bridge, source greps); guard targets against a real bridge (cover, switch, blocked tap with no event, pairKey).
- e2e: `e2e/support/touch.ts` holds `touchSender` and `pinch` moved verbatim out of `e2e/zoom.spec.ts`. `e2e/press.spec.ts` has the case table (engine master toggle in switch_bank, cb.pitot in cb_essential, sw.deice_alternate.guard in deice) with, per case: mouse act on release with the pending ring, touch act on release, mouse and touch drag pan with unchanged state, slop boundaries for mouse and touch derived from the constants of `tap-intent.ts` (exactly the slop is a tap, one pixel more a pan), a drag at the full view changing nothing, a second finger on the background starting a pinch; plus a two-switch pinch, the guard flow (drag from cover and switch pans, tap lifts on release, tap flips on release) and the `@hooks` cases (a drag records nothing, a tap exactly one event).

## Assumptions taken

- Guard cover has no pressed visual (the plan names none and art must not change), so its `ring` flag is false in the case table and the pending state is not asserted for it.
- The case table also drives the breaker and the guard cover from task 1's helpers; the full-view hit centres are rounded to whole px so the slop boundary distances are exact.
- Mouse cases run on any `desktop-*` project (so the plan 41 WebKit desktop run exercises them), touch drag cases on `phone-chromium` only, as the plan says. The tablet project therefore skips the press cases.
- In the pinch-from-a-control case the second finger starts on a background point of the case's region and spreads away; the pinch with two fingers on switches uses the first two latch hits of switch_bank.
- Pointer capture for pending presses is applied only to registered (latch or momentary) targets, never to own pointers, so unmigrated levers, knobs, buttons and FAN spots keep their own capture.
- The stale-record routing: effects emitted during a down are applied before the new record is stored, except the new pointer's own "pending on", which is routed to it explicitly (so a stale record with the same pointer id still receives its discard).
