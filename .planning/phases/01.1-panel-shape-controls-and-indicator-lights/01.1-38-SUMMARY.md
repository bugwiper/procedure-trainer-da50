# Phase 01.1 Plan 38: Brake flap, lever markers and FAN spots act on release Summary

The tap-toggle PARKING BRAKE flap, every lever marker rectangle (the flaps lever and the others with markers) and the four FAN click spots no longer own their pointers: a press is pending (the brake shows its pressed ring), a lift within the slop (10 px touch, 5 px mouse) actuates once, movement beyond the slop becomes a pan (zoomed) or nothing (full view), and a second finger discards the press and pinches (owner finding R3-Z2 items 1, 4 and 5). After this plan every latching control of the panel is migrated.

## What changed

- `DetentLever.tsx`: `makeTapToggleHandlers`, `TapToggleHandlers`, `TAP_SLOP_PX`, `TapStart`, `onMarkerDown`, `onMarkerUp`, `clearTap` and the marker capture are gone. `makeTapTogglePress({def, stateOf, actuate, setPending})` returns a latch target whose tap actuates the other detent (`tapToggleTarget`) and returns the sequence number or null. `makeMarkerPress({def, detent, actuate})` returns a latch target that actuates its detent (null when the store rejects an unchanged state). `LeverBody` takes an optional `press: LeverPress` and puts `press.brake` on the brake hit and `press.marker(detentId)` on each marker rect as ref callbacks; a drag-only lever renders no marker and asks for none. The handle keeps `LeverHandlers` (down, move, up, clearDrag) and its single `setPointerCapture`. Targets are built in the render body and registered by ref callback on every render, as in plan 37.
- `Rotary.tsx`: `makeSpotPress({def, toLocal, actuate, setPending})` returns a latch target whose tap converts the pointer-down point of the press info to knob-local units and sets the stop `tapStop` gives (null: nothing). The spot rects register it by ref and carry no pointer handler. The body hit rect and its handlers (tap steps, drag rotates) are unchanged; plan 39 registers the body.
- Tests: `tests/lever-skins.test.ts` replaces the old handler test by factory cases (off to set and set to off, sequence number, null for a rejected dispatch, a real bridge, marker detent id, null for an unchanged flaps detent, the body asks for one ref per marker and none for the drag-only EMERGENCY GEAR EXTENSION, source greps). `tests/rotary.test.ts` adds the `makeSpotPress` describe (each spot's stop, the dead zone, an unconvertible point, sequence number and rejection, the current stop passed through to the store, a real bridge, no pending call by the factory, source greps).
- e2e: `e2e/press.spec.ts` case table gets `selector` and `id` fields (the hit element is no longer always `data-hit`) and the rows of Table T3: parking brake flap, flaps marker T/O, FAN spot f2. Per row: act on release (mouse, touch), pan over it (mouse, touch), the slop boundary (mouse, touch), a drag at the full view, a second finger starting a pinch, and the `@hooks` event-log case. New: a tap on the handle, the middle and the far end of the drag-only EMERGENCY GEAR EXTENSION track changes nothing (mouse and phone touch).

## Assumptions taken

- Marker rects are latching taps and the handle is a drag control (the plan's planner reading), recorded for the owner's Round 5 review.
- The case table "second finger" case covers the second finger landing on a pending brake press (the generic per-row case, with the brake ring asserted); no separate brake-only case was written.
- The boundary cases now run the press one pixel beyond the slop first and the exactly-slop tap second (see Deviations); the pan direction comes from `panVector` so a clamped view edge cannot hide the pan.
- The marker case starts from the flaps default `up` and taps T/O, a different state, as Table T3 asks.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 2 - Missing critical functionality] data-pressed on the parking brake group**
- **Found during:** Task 1 e2e (the generic case table asserts the pending ring through `data-pressed`).
- **Issue:** The brake group exposed no `data-pressed`, so the "press is pending" state of the brake could not be asserted (toggles and breakers expose it). The ring itself was already drawn by `FlapBody`.
- **Fix:** `TapToggleBody` writes `data-pressed="true"` on the group while the press is pending (attribute only, no art change).
- **Files modified:** src/ui/panel/widgets/DetentLever.tsx
- **Commit:** a8e37e6

**2. [Test adjustment] Slop-boundary cases reordered**
- **Issue:** After a tap on the flaps T/O marker the lever handle sits over that marker, so a second press at the marker centre lands on the handle (an own drag), not on the marker under test.
- **Fix:** for every row the press one pixel beyond the slop is run first (state unchanged, view moved), then the exactly-slop tap (state changes, view unchanged). Assertions are the same, none loosened; the earlier rows keep the same coverage.
- **Files modified:** e2e/press.spec.ts
- **Commit:** a8e37e6 (the FAN row in 4b0c1c5)
