## Owner decisions this plan builds (R3-Z3, 2026-10-21, supersedes the "background only" decision of R2-Z1)

A double click or double tap anywhere, including on a latching control, zooms (in on the tapped area or out again) and must not leave the switch toggled twice. Owner decision: no delay; the first tap actuates immediately; when the second tap within 350 ms and 24 px of the first tap on the same control is recognised, the zoom is executed and the first actuation is RETRACTED, i.e. removed from the action log (not a counter-event appended), and the state recomputed deterministically from the log (engine replay). Applies to latching controls only; momentary and drag controls keep their gesture (a double press stays two presses, no zoom). A tap pair that crosses two different controls is not a double tap. Requires a log-level retract (only the last event of that control, only within the pair window, never an event that a time-driven reaction has already consumed): built in plan 35; the pair window is this plan's tracker.

Planner decisions (recorded in the SUMMARY and by plan 41): the pair key is the hit element (the data-hit or data-detent value), so two different spots of the FAN or two markers of one lever are different keys, except that the guard cover and the guarded switch share one key (the guarded toggle's id), so a double tap on the closed guard cover zooms and leaves the guard closed and the switch off instead of lifting the guard and flipping the switch (a deliberate lift-and-flip within the pair window is therefore a zoom: pause between the two taps); both taps must be quick (within the existing 300 ms tap limit); a refused retract turns the pair into an ordinary second tap; a pair whose point lies in no region at the full view retracts the first actuation and zooms nowhere (a control always lies inside a region, so this cannot happen in practice).

Consequence the owner should know (Round 5 list): two quick taps on one switch, for example flipping it on and straight back off within 350 ms and 24 px, are now a zoom and leave the switch unchanged; flipping it twice needs a short pause.

## Data tables

Table D1, the pair contract for taps (client px and ms; the constants are the existing ones of tap-intent.ts: TAP_MAX_MS 300, DOUBLE_TAP_MS 350, DOUBLE_TAP_PX 24, all inclusive; the pair is measured lift to lift):

| Situation | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| background tap, then background tap within the limits | zoom (in at the full view, out while zoomed), as since plan 25 |
| latching tap, then a tap on the same hit key within the limits, both quick | the first actuation is retracted, the second tap is not actuated, the view zooms (in: the region under the second tap; out while zoomed) |
| latching tap, then a tap on a different hit key (or the background) within the limits | no pair: the second tap actuates (or is a background tap) and becomes the remembered tap |
| a tap on the closed guard cover, then a tap on the same spot (now the guarded switch) | the same pair key: the guard-open event is retracted, the switch is not flipped, the view zooms |
| latching tap, then a tap on the same key beyond the time or distance limit | no pair: ordinary taps |
| the second tap lasts longer than TAP_MAX_MS | not a pair member: it actuates normally and ends the pair |
| momentary or drag control pressed between the two taps, a pan, a pinch, a cancel, a view change by the Full panel button | the pair ends |
| a recognised pair, then a third quick tap | the pair was consumed: the third tap is the first tap of a new pair and actuates |
| the first tap recorded no event (unchanged or blocked) | the pair zooms, nothing is retracted |
| the retract of the first event is refused | the second tap actuates as an ordinary tap, no zoom, and it is remembered as a new first tap |
