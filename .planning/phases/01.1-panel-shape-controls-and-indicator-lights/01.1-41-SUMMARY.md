## The one WebKit failure

`e2e/rotary.spec.ts:228` "@hooks FAN: a tap on each mark and on the body sets one stop and records one event" on phone-webkit: expected f1, received f3. Cause: the last two taps of the test hit the same FAN body point within the pair window (350 ms, 24 px). Under R3-Z3 that is a double tap, so the first actuation was retracted and the state returned to f3. On the Chromium projects the awaits between the taps happen to take longer. This is a test timing fragility introduced by the double click contract (plan 40's sweep of "taps on one control twice quickly" ran on Chromium only), not a defect of the app. Fix (commit 182e422): `afterPairWindow` before every step of the loop and before the repeated tap, with a comment; no assertion loosened or removed. The risks that this plan named for WebKit (pointer capture on the svg for mouse and touch, two touch taps in 350 ms on a control, the 100 ms hold of a held mouse press, the Full panel stray click after a control double tap, the bus title gate) all passed.
