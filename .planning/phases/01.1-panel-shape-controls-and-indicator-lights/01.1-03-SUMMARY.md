# Phase 01.1 Plan 03: Knobs and the skin vocabulary Summary

**A new `rotary` control kind (pure stop logic, widget with tap halves and drag snapping, registry invariants) carries FUEL TRANS, INSTRUMENT LIGHT, FLOOD LIGHT and FAN, and the registry now accepts every toggle, push-button (incl. three-state spring-bat) and lever skin the wave-3 plans use.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 12 min (2026-10-20T15:26Z to 15:38Z)
- **Tasks:** 3 (Task 1 tracer, Task 2 auto tdd, Task 3 auto tdd)
- **Files:** 5 created, 7 modified

## Accomplishments

- `RotaryDef` (kind `rotary`, prefix `sw.`) with three invariants: 2 to 5 stops, strictly increasing angles with a sweep below 360, and 2 x r inside the smaller hit side. `legalStates` returns the stop ids, so the store's legality rule covers knobs unchanged.
- `src/engine/rotary.ts` is pure (no DOM, `RangeError` on non-finite input): `angleOf`, `stopAngle`, `stepStop` (two stops always toggle, more clamp, no wrap), `nearestStop` (circular distance modulo 360, ties to the lower index).
- `Rotary.tsx`: Phase 1 pointer contract (primary pointer, capture on down, no click, cancel and lost capture clear without dispatching). Under 4 px of travel is a tap (right half = next stop, left half = previous); 4 px or more is a drag whose angle lives in a UI-local signal and commits `nearestStop` on release. Only stop ids reach the store; a drag back to the same stop is rejected as unchanged. `RotaryBody` is pure art (disc, rim, 24 knurls as one path, pointer, printed scale, placard, state text), at most about 16 nodes; the rim turns `#8fb8e6` only while pressed.
- Knobs at their table positions: FUEL TRANS (189, 664), INSTRUMENT LIGHT (1047, 238), FLOOD LIGHT (1126, 238), FAN (1032, 896). All default OFF.
- Registry skin vocabulary: toggle `bat | rocker | softkey` with `size`, `colour`, `ball`; guarded toggle `size` and `colour` (both or neither); momentary button `chrome-round | blue-dome | small-black | spring-bat` with a rest-first three-state form; ten lever skins. `Toggle.tsx` now defaults to `bat` (the Phase 1 `switch` value is gone). No data file sets a skin yet.
- `targetSide`: rotary is the whole footprint for two stops, otherwise half the width; a three-state momentary button counts half its width.
