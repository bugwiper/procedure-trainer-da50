# Phase 01.1 Plan 26: Full panel button placement and the tablet chromium project Summary

The Full panel button now picks the top right, bottom right or bottom left corner by a pure rule over the control footprints, so it covers no control in any zoomed region on any landscape profile, and a tablet-chromium project makes the tablet case part of the chromium gate.

## What was done

- Reproduction first (before any source change): with only the new `tablet-chromium` project added, `PW_PORT=4226 npx playwright test e2e/breaker.spec.ts -g "Full panel button" --project=tablet-chromium` failed with `Error: cb_main` and received `["cb.main_pump"]`, as the planner predicted.
- Task 1 (tracer, commit 9bf14cc): unit tests first, then `viewport.ts` gained the pure rule (`controlFootprints`, `buttonBox`, `buttonOverlap`, `chooseButtonCorner`, constants). `zoom.ts` gained `panelScreen` (set from the svg box at attach, kept by a `ResizeObserver` that the detach function disconnects) and `buttonCorner` (computed from `viewRect`, `panelScreen` and the footprints of the attached registry, remembering the last corner, starting at `tr`; the corner memory is reset to `tr` on each attach). `App.tsx` sets `data-corner`. `stage.css` fixes the button at 112 px wide, 44 px high, and positions it by `data-corner` (8 px from the two nearest edges plus the safe-area insets on the right, left and top). zoomRect, regions, controls and hits are untouched.
- Task 2 (commit 00440eb): `buttonCoverage(page)` in `e2e/support/zoom.ts`; the breaker spec uses it; a new zoom spec test goes through every `[data-region-id]` (none excluded) on every project except phone-portrait and asserts no covered hit, topmost at the centre, no footer or visible rotate hint overlap, inside the viewport and `data-corner` in tr/br/bl.

## Measurements

- The planner's measurement reproduced: the unit gate over all 30 regions and the four landscape sizes finds a free corner everywhere; the top right corner is covered in the tablet cb_main case (and the others the planner listed) and the chosen corner moves to a bottom corner. No pair needed the least-overlap fallback, so the landscape known list is empty.
- Portrait phone 393 x 631: exactly `deice_switches` and `engine_controls` have no free corner; this is the known-list unit test (it fails on a stale entry or a new uncovered region). Portrait stays outside the e2e gate by the existing portrait note.
