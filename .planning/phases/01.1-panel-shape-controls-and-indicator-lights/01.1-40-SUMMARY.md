# Phase 01.1 Plan 40: Double click on a latching control zooms and retracts the first actuation Summary

A double click or double tap on a latching control (toggle, rocker, breaker, guard cover, guarded switch, parking brake flap, lever marker, FAN spot) now zooms like a double tap on the background and leaves the control exactly where it was: the first tap actuates at once, the recognised pair removes that event from the action log (engine replay, plan 35) and the second tap is not actuated. PNL-15, ROADMAP criterion 4 and the README state the contract (owner finding R3-Z3, superseding the background-only decision of R2-Z1).

## What changed

- `tap-intent.ts`: `TapPoint` gains an optional `key`; the tracker pairs two taps only when their keys are equal (absent equals null); limits and the consumed-pair rule unchanged, the seven older tracker cases pass without edits. New `noteActuation(seq)` and `takeFirstSeq()`. New pure `resolveControlTap(tracker, point, zoomed, ops)` returning `actuated`, `zoom-in` or `zoom-out` by Table D1, including the refused-retract fallback (reset, act, remember this tap as a new first tap) and the first-tap-recorded-nothing case (pairs, nothing retracted).
- `zoom.ts`: one `onTap` applies the zoom for both paths (region lookup, zoom out, the Full panel stray-click stamp, the reset after a view change). The background path feeds the tracker directly; a latching tap goes through `resolveControlTap` with the target's tap as `act` and the injected `retract` option; a long second tap (not quick) is an ordinary tap and ends the pair. `attachZoomGestures` takes `retract?: (seq) => boolean`; the header comment states the control pair rule. No bridge import.
- `Panel.tsx`: passes `retract: (seq) => bridge.retract(seq).ok`.
- Tests: tracker cases with keys and `resolveControlTap` cases with fakes (tests/zoom.test.ts, red first, then green); arbiter cases for the join key; `makeHandlePress` cases; wording tests of tests/panel-review.test.ts rewritten to the new contract (red first, then the three text edits).
- e2e: `zoom.spec.ts` case rewritten (a double click on the engine master zooms in and out, state unchanged, log empty). `press.spec.ts` gets the R3-Z3 matrix (see below). `afterPairWindow` moved to `e2e/support/zoom.ts` (DOUBLE_TAP_MS plus 100 ms, so the same 450 ms as before); `zoom.spec.ts` imports it.
- Wording: scoped Edit calls on the PNL-15 line (REQUIREMENTS.md), success criterion 4 of phase 01.1 (ROADMAP.md) and the README zoom sentence. The checkbox state, the traceability table, the plan 25 history line and the round-3 plan list are untouched; the supersession notes and the Round 5 section follow in plan 41.

## The R3-Z3 matrix (e2e/press.spec.ts)

- Every latching case (engine master toggle, circuit breaker, parking brake flap, flaps marker T/O, FAN spot f2): a double click or double tap at the full view zooms (not the full viewBox) and the control keeps its start state; zoomed, the same pair zooms fully out with the state unchanged (all three chromium projects).
- Guard cover of the de-ice ALTERNATE switch: zooms, guard stays closed, switch stays off.
- Three quick taps on a toggle: the pair zooms and leaves it unchanged, the third tap (at the control's new place after the zoom) actuates.
- The background double click still zooms in and out.
- `@hooks` (log): every latching control and the guard cover leave an empty log after the double click, at the full view and zoomed; crossing pairs (FAN spots f1 then f2, flaps markers T/O then LDG) are two actuations and no zoom; a second tap held longer than the tap limit does not pair (desktop and phone); the far ends of the parking brake footprint (desktop) do not pair; two quick ENGINE START presses record pressed, released, pressed, released, two quick taps on the INSTRUMENT LIGHT knob body step it twice, two taps on the throttle handle record nothing, and none of them zoom.
- Skipped with a reason: ENGINE MASTER then ESS BUS (the two hit centres are further apart than the distance limit on every project, so the pair precondition does not hold; the FAN spot and flaps marker crossing cases cover the rule), the held-second-tap case on the tablet project (the CDP touch sender is the phone project's), the far-ends case on touch projects (footprint shorter than the limit or not desktop).

## Assumptions taken

- Unattended run: no checkpoint was needed; no decision was left to the owner. The plan's defaults were used everywhere (pair key = hit key, both taps quick, refused retract becomes an ordinary tap).
- A pair whose point lies in no region at the full view retracts the first actuation and zooms nowhere (the plan's rule; cannot happen in practice).
- The held-second-tap e2e case holds the second press TAP_MAX_MS + 10 ms. If the lift-to-lift time exceeds the pair window by timing jitter the case would also pass without the quick rule, so the quick rule itself is pinned by the glue branch and, for the pure part, by the unit cases; the case can never fail spuriously.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] A double tap on a lever marker did not zoom: the handle covers the tapped marker**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (the matrix case for the flaps marker T/O failed on all three projects).
- **Issue:** After the first tap the lever handle rests on that marker, so the second press landed on the handle hit (an own, drag class target). Plan 36 and 39 make an own press end the pair at its down, so the pair never formed and the lever marker, which the owner names as a latching control, did not zoom.
- **Fix:** an own descriptor may carry a `joinKey` (press-arbiter.ts). Such a press does not end the pair at its down; a still, quick lift is reported as a join-only tap with that key; a drag past DRAG_START_PX, a cancel, a long press or a second finger ends the pair. The gesture layer never remembers a join-only tap as the first tap of a pair, so a double tap on the handle alone stays two presses and never zooms (the throttle case of the matrix). `makeHandlePress` in DetentLever.tsx gives the handle of a marker lever the key of the marker under it (`lv.flaps:to` while at T/O); a drag-only lever has no pair key. Arbiter tests and `makeHandlePress` tests were written before the code (red first).
- **Files modified:** src/ui/panel/press-arbiter.ts, src/ui/panel/press-targets.ts, src/ui/panel/widgets/DetentLever.tsx, src/ui/panel/zoom.ts, tests/press-arbiter.test.ts, tests/lever-skins.test.ts
- **Commit:** 7aa326c

**2. [Test adjustment] The e2e case for the engine master read the start state instead of a literal**
- **Issue:** the rewritten zoom case assumed the switch starts off; the in-flight default is on.
- **Fix:** the case reads the start state first and compares against it.
- **Commit:** 2b7df61
