## Phase goal

The panel looks like the real DA50 RG: every switch, rocker, push button, lever and knob at its real position in kind-of real shape and real colour; the gear and flap lights work; the owner's first-render review (01.1-OWNER-FINDINGS.md) closes the remaining gaps: no dynamic text, labels as in the real aircraft, rockers readable at a glance, nothing sticking out of its plate, and the de-ice, gear, environmental, pedestal, flap, FADEC, ELT and LEVEL corrections.

<objective>
Produce the table "control, assumed static label" that owner finding G-0.2 asks for, in docs/PANEL-LABELS.md, gate it with a test, and make the inventory count assertions derived so the following gap plans can change the inventory freely.

Purpose: owner findings G-0.1 and G-0.2 (static real-aircraft labels only; list unknown labels for correction) with D-10 (full inventory) and D-09 (kinds and proportions). The owner asked to review labels before they are baked in; the run is unattended, so the table is written with the best assumption for every label and handed over as a morning-review item. Later plans read their placard text from this document.
Output: docs/PANEL-LABELS.md, tests/panel-labels.test.ts, derived counts in two existing tests.
</objective>
