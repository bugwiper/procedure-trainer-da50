# Phase 01.1 Plan 09: Lever, slider and pull handle shapes Summary

**All twelve detent levers now render in their real shape from a pure LeverBody: silver fluted GEAR knob on a bat stem, black FLAPS block in a notched slot, carbon-hatched POWER grip with no emblem, red T-knob and red tab pulls, black ALTERNATE AIR boot, small chrome MODE bat, parking latch, cowl slider and the three environmental sliders with original icons, with every Phase 1 drag, tap and dragOnly behaviour intact.**

## Accomplishments

- `DetentLever.tsx`: `LeverBody` renders the whole group (name, track art, one tick path, legends, state text, marker rects, handle, handle hit) from props with no hooks; `DetentLever` keeps `groupRef`, the drag and tap signals and the pointer handlers. Groups carry `data-skin`, `role="img"` and `aria-label` "LABEL: STATE". Fonts are 6 (names, legends) and 10 (state text; 8 for the compact MODE bat); the 6.5 and 9 of Phase 1 are gone. A lever without a skin still draws the Phase 1 handle.
- Handle-hit widening: the handle hit rect is now the full footprint width (`across`) by one handle square along the track, so the whole visible grip (gear disc, flaps block, power grip) can be grabbed. The vertical centre follows the handle exactly as before, so the existing centre-of-hit drag specs pass unchanged. Marker rects, `MIN_DRAG_PX`, `TAP_SLOP_PX` and the dragOnly rule (no markers) are untouched.
- `LeverHandles.tsx`: handle and track art for `gear-knob`, `flaps-block`, `bat-small`, `pull-t`, `pull-tab`, `pull-boot`, `power-grip`, `cowl-slider`, `parking-lever`, `env-slider` (Table V1), plus five original line icons (defrost, face, feet, fresh, recirc). Only shared ids of Defs.tsx are used (`g-silver`, `g-chrome`, `p-carbon`); no filters, no per-widget defs. Red `#c4161f` appears only in the pull-t and pull-tab bodies. A blue grab ring `#8fb8e6` shows only while dragging.
- Horizontal CIRCULATING AIR has its own placement: name above the slot, icons at the slot ends (outside the handle), legends under the ends (hidden under the handle), state text below.
- Data: `skin` set on lv.gear, lv.flaps, lv.deice_mode, lv.alt_static, lv.emerg_gear (dragOnly kept), lv.alt_air, lv.power, lv.cowl_flap, lv.parking_brake, lv.env_direction, lv.env_temp, lv.env_circ, with comments naming the photos and the unconfirmed ALTERNATE STATIC handle look.
- Tests: `tests/lever-skins.test.ts` (23 tests: every lever at every detent, dragging or not, at most 40 elements and exactly one state text equal to the detent label; POWER 21 detents with one tick path; skin assignments; red only on the two pulls; FLAPS geometry; POWER grip p-carbon with no text on the handle and no emblem or idle gate; horizontal layout inside the region; font sizes only 6, 8, 10, 14). `e2e/lever-skins.spec.ts` on all projects (skins, GEAR marker taps and mouse drag from the disc edge, FLAPS and MODE taps, pull taps, no EMERGENCY marker, node cap in the real DOM, console skins, POWER mouse drag, CIRCULATING AIR taps).
