- `Rocker.tsx`: bezel (4 padding, rx 5), optional L ears 8 x 40, two half paths (raised lighter with a 1-unit highlight, pressed darker with the outer end inset 1 and a 2-unit shadow band toward the groove), 1-unit groove, end placards, name placard, state text (10, or 8 for LIGHT and S). The second state in registry order presses the upper half (ON, and ELT ON). At most 14 elements per body. The bezel rim is `#8fb8e6` only while a pointer is down.
- `Bat.tsx`: Phase 1 geometry unchanged for the alternators, FADEC and ENGINE MASTER (pivot r 5, throw min(14, (h-37)/2-1), label 8, lamp dot, state text now 10), repainted with a bezel ring r 7, a chrome polygon bat (`g-chrome`, 1-unit dark outline) and a r 3.2 ball tip on ENGINE MASTER.
- `Toggle.tsx`: rocker and bat skins dispatch to the pure bodies; `SwitchBody` removed; `SoftkeyBody` exported; selected softkey stroke is 2.5 `#e6e9ed` (no `#5ab0ff`); group attributes `data-skin`, `data-size`, `data-colour`, `data-pressed-half`, `data-pressed`, role img, aria-label "LABEL: STATE". Pointer contract and hit rect untouched.
- `GuardedToggle.tsx`: rocker M red via `RockerBody`, `GuardCover` (rect 40 x 54 rx 4 `#c43030` at 35 percent, border `#e04040` 1.5, three hatch bars filled `p-hatch`; open: cover lifted with hinge line). Per-instance `<pattern>` removed. Hit rects, guard-open hit and interlock unchanged (T-01.1-17).
- Data: Table S1 applied (ELECT. MASTER L red, ELT S red, ESS BUS / PROP DE-ICE / EMERG FUEL PUMP / AV MASTER M white, PITOT/STALL HEAT W white, four LIGHT white, ENGINE MASTER `ball: true`, ALTERNATE M red). `rowSwitch` in switch-bank.ts takes the skin fields.
- Tests: `tests/switch-skins.test.ts` (22 tests: node cap and one state text per body for every size, colour, state and pressed combination; ring only when pressed; half tones swap with state; face, ears and bezel inside every rocker hit rect; D-08 colour and size table; exactly three red parts; bats and ball; compact variant; guarded cover with `p-hatch` and no pattern or defs; softkey stroke; font sizes only 6, 8, 10, 14 in the four toggle-family sources). `e2e/switch-skins.spec.ts` on all projects (pressed half follows state for all 11 rockers, red set, light rockers flip, guarded ALTERNATE half, transient ring on desktop-chromium).
