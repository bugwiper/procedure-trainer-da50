# Phase 01.1 Plan 36: Press arbitration core Summary

One pure state machine now decides what every pointer on the panel is (view pointer, pending latch or momentary press, held press, owned pointer, ignored), and the zoom layer is glue over it. No widget was touched: every hit target is still classified as own, so the machine's latch and momentary rules are built and unit tested but not yet used by the panel (plans 37 to 39 register the targets).

## What changed

- `src/ui/panel/tap-intent.ts`: `TAP_SLOP_PX` is replaced by `TOUCH_SLOP_PX` (10), `MOUSE_SLOP_PX` (5), `PointerKind` and `slopPx(kind)`. `isBackgroundTap` takes the pointer kind. `TAP_MAX_MS`, the double-tap tracker and the Full panel guard are unchanged.
- `src/ui/panel/press-arbiter.ts` (new): `createPressArbiter()` with `down`, `move`, `up`, `cancel`, `reset`, `tick`, `nextDeadline`, `stateOf`; effects `pending`, `tap`, `press`, `release`, `discard`, `pan`, `pinch`, `pair-break`. Constants `MOMENTARY_HOLD_MS` (100) and `DRAG_START_PX` (4). Imports only tap-intent.
- `src/ui/panel/zoom.ts`: the controlPointers set, the background map and the per-pointer flags are gone. Capture-phase listeners (pointerdown, move, up, cancel, lostpointercapture) feed the machine; a target inside `[data-hit], [data-detent]` is classified own (dragStartPx 0, key from the attribute), anything else background. Effects: pan uses the existing `panRect`/`fitScale` rule while zoomed, pinch the existing `pinchRect`/`panRect` rule, a tap with key null feeds the unchanged double-tap tracker and zoom-in/zoom-out code, pair-break resets the tracker. `viewChangedByTapAt`, the stray Full panel click guard, `lastCorner`, footprints and the resize observer are untouched. The header comment lists the new rules.
- `tests/zoom.test.ts`: slop cases use `TOUCH_SLOP_PX`/`MOUSE_SLOP_PX`; new `slopPx` describe; `isBackgroundTap` cases keep all they had and add the per-kind boundary.
- `tests/press-arbiter.test.ts` (new): 55 cases, numbers derived from the exported constants and `slopPx`.

## The two documented behaviour changes

1. The background tap slop of the mouse is 5 px (one slop per pointer kind, also for the double click on the background); touch and pen keep 10 px.
2. A second finger that lands on a control while a view pointer is down now starts a pinch with both; before, it ended the view gesture and was ignored by the zoom layer. Note for plans 37 to 39: until the widgets are migrated, that second finger still also operates its control through the widget's own handler (the machine only governs the view); plan 37 onward makes the control wait for the machine.

Everything else in the zoom layer behaves as before: the unchanged whole of `e2e/zoom.spec.ts`, `e2e/neutral.spec.ts`, `e2e/avionics.spec.ts` and `e2e/rotary.spec.ts` passes on the three chromium projects.

## Assumptions taken

- Effect payload beyond Table A3 (executor's choice): `tap` also carries `id` and `at` (lift time); `pan` carries the pointer `id`; `press` carries the down point as `at`; every effect carries the pointer id and key where it concerns one control.
- Latch ends always emit `pending` off before `discard`, so a control that listens only to `pending` sees balanced on/off pairs (order: pending off, discard, pair-break, pan).
- The cancel of an own pointer (owned or before its drag threshold) emits no effects (Task 2 wording) although Task 1 words cancel as emitting pair-break: the pair was already broken at the own pointer's down. A stale own pointer swept by a primary down gets discard (if still before its threshold) and pair-break.
- A held momentary press blocks pointers of every kind, not only its own kind (a mouse press blocks a touch pointer and the reverse); only the stale sweep is per kind.
- A momentary press that is lifted beyond the slop without a preceding move is a discard, same for a latch (the lift point decides).
- `tick` is implemented and tested in the machine but zoom.ts does not schedule a timer yet: no momentary target is registered in this plan, plan 39 adds the timer glue from `nextDeadline`.
- Widgets keep their local `TAP_SLOP_PX` constants (no widget changes in this plan); plans 37 to 39 replace them with `slopPx`.
- Pen and unknown pointer types count as touch in the glue (`kindOf`), as recorded in the plan.
