## Phase goal

The panel looks like the real DA50 RG with every control in kind-of real shape and colour, and the owner's live-site review (01.1-OWNER-FINDINGS-R2.md) is closed: a click or tap never zooms by accident, the Full panel button never covers a control, and the parking brake, the FAN, the engine strip and the light switches match the aircraft.

<objective>
Fix the layout defect found by the phase 01.2 final gates (R2-T1): while the MAIN breaker region is zoomed on a tablet in landscape, the Full panel button sits over the hit of cb.main_pump. Make the button choose a corner where it covers no control hit, in every zoom region on every landscape profile, and add a chromium tablet project so this case runs in the chromium gate.

Purpose: R2-T1, PNL-15, D-13 (the Full panel button must never be hidden or in the way). The planner measured on the 01.2 tree with the 112 x 44 box that the top right corner overlaps a control footprint in 9 of the region and profile pairs of the four landscape profiles (desktop under_panel_centre; tablet pfd_bezel_right, cb_main, under_panel_centre, env_control; phone pfd and mfd; Pixel phone pfd and mfd) and that every one of them has a free bottom corner. On the portrait phone two regions (deice_switches, engine_controls) have no free corner among the three.
Output: a pure corner rule, the button wired to it, CSS for three corners, the tablet chromium project, unit and e2e gates over every region and profile.
</objective>
