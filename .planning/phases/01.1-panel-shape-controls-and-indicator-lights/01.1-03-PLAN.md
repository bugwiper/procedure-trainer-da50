Created here (src/engine/registry.ts):
- RotaryStopDef { id: StateId; label: string; angle: number } (degrees clockwise from 12 o'clock; strictly increasing; last minus first below 360; values above 180 are allowed so a sweep can pass 6 o'clock)
- RotaryDef extends ControlBase { kind: 'rotary'; stops: readonly RotaryStopDef[] (2 to 5); default: StateId; r: number (visible knob radius); scale?: 'arc' | 'dots' | 'none' }
- ToggleDef.skin?: 'bat' | 'rocker' | 'softkey' (the Phase 1 value 'switch' is renamed 'bat', the default); size?: 'S' | 'M' | 'L' | 'W' | 'LIGHT'; colour?: 'white' | 'red'; ball?: boolean
- GuardedToggleDef.size?, colour? (the rocker under the guard)
- MomentaryButtonDef.states: readonly [StateDef, StateDef, ...StateDef[]] (rest first; 2 or 3 states); skin?: 'chrome-round' | 'blue-dome' | 'small-black' | 'spring-bat'
- DetentLeverDef.skin?: 'gear-knob' | 'flaps-block' | 'power-grip' | 'pull-t' | 'pull-tab' | 'pull-boot' | 'env-slider' | 'bat-small' | 'parking-lever' | 'cowl-slider'
src/engine/rotary.ts (pure, no DOM, RangeError on non-finite input):
- angleOf(dx: number, dy: number): number (screen coordinates, y down; 0 = up, 90 = right; result 0 <= a < 360)
- stopAngle(def: RotaryDef, id: StateId): number
- stepStop(def: RotaryDef, current: StateId, direction: 1 | -1): StateId (two stops: always the other stop; more: next or previous, clamped)
- nearestStop(def: RotaryDef, angle: number): StateId (smallest circular distance modulo 360; ties go to the lower index)
targetSide: rotary -> stops.length === 2 ? min(hit.w, hit.h) : min(hit.w / 2, hit.h); momentary-button -> states.length === 3 ? min(hit.w / 2, hit.h) : min(hit.w, hit.h).
