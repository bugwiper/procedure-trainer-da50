## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] lv.deice_mode geometry fails the pinch-zoom tap guarantee**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (full vitest run)
- **Issue:** Table D1 (hit 30 x 80, track 60) gives a smallest target of 20 units (detent spacing). The region test passes (47 px) but `tests/zoom.test.ts` "portrait via pinch" needs at least 22.4 units because the MIN_VIEW_WIDTH view scales 1.965 px per unit on a 393 px phone; it reached 39.3 px.
- **Fix:** hit 30 x 90 and track 67.5, so handle square and detent spacing are both 22.5 (44.2 px at the pinch limit). The footprint y 25..115 still fits region `deice` (height 115) and the reference row (y 345, tol 12) is unchanged.
- **Files modified:** src/aircraft/da50rg/controls/gear-flaps.ts
- **Commit:** 5eebaa4

**Total deviations:** 1 auto-fixed (1 bug). **Impact:** geometry of one control only; position, detents and defaults as planned.
