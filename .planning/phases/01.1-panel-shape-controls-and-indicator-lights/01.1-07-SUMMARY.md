# Phase 01.1 Plan 07: Single-tap zoom out and Full panel button Summary

**A single background tap while zoomed (also from a pinch level) zooms fully out through pure tap rules in tap-intent.ts, and the Full panel button now lives inside .panel-area above the footer with 14 px chrome text.**

## Accomplishments

- `tap-intent.ts`: `isBackgroundTap` (travel <= 10 px, <= 300 ms, one pointer, not on a target) and `tapIntent` (zoomed gives zoom-out, full view gives zoom-in, `none` within 350 ms and 24 px of a tap-driven change). No DOM, no bridge.
- `zoom.ts`: onUp builds the gesture record and applies `tapIntent`; the double-tap bookkeeping and its constants are gone; header arbitration rules (e) and (f) rewritten. Each pointer tracks the largest number of simultaneous background pointers so a pinch finger never taps. The module still imports no bridge and never operates a control.
- `App.tsx` / `stage.css`: `.panel-area` (relative, flex 1 1 auto, min-height 0) wraps the svg and the button; `.zoom-out` is at top/right 8 px plus safe-area insets, z-index 30, min 44 x 44, radius 8 px; `.footer` is relative, z-index 1, padding-bottom for the safe area; every `font-size` in stage.css is 14 px.
- Tests: 9 new unit tests (tap rules, 14 px-only stylesheet); e2e covers single tap, double tap while zoomed, tap from a pinch level (phone-chromium), drag pans without zooming out, the `@hooks` zero-event gesture test, and a new hit-test / non-intersection check of the Full panel button against footer and rotate hint on every project including phone-portrait.

## Deviations from Plan

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Suppression window blocked a legitimate zoom-in after the Full panel button**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (the "every region reaches full tap size" e2e regressed on phone-chromium and phone-webkit)
- **Issue:** A zoom-in tap followed within 350 ms by the Full panel button and a tap at a nearby point was ignored as a "second tap".
- **Fix:** The last tap-driven change remembers the view object it produced; if the view changed since (button, pinch, pan), the suppression is dropped.
- **Files modified:** src/ui/panel/zoom.ts
- **Commit:** 5987284

**2. [Rule 3 - Test hygiene] Removed the older "mouse drag on the background pans" e2e**
- It duplicated the new "a background drag while zoomed pans and does not zoom out" test (which also asserts the Full panel button stays visible).

**Total deviations:** 2 (1 bug fix, 1 test consolidation). **Impact:** none on scope.
