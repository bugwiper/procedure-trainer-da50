## Phase goal

The panel looks like the real DA50 RG with every control in kind-of real shape and colour; the owner's first-render review adds: parts must not stick out of their panel region, and where a part does not fit the region is enlarged.

<objective>
Make the visible plates data, add a built-panel gate that measures every region's art against its region and its plate, and use it to fix the owner's overflow reports for FADEC, the flap block and LEVEL plus any other region the gate flags that no later plan rebuilds.

Purpose: owner findings G-0.5 (parts must not stick out; enlarge the region, make neighbours give way), G-4 (flaps too high, enlarge upward), G-5 (FADEC too high) and the LEVEL part of G-6 (not on a border). In the owner's words a "region" is the dark plate seen on screen; the invisible zoom regions of the registry are checked as well.
Output: plates.ts, plate-driven shell, e2e/art-fit.spec.ts, corrected plates and regions.
</objective>
