# Phase 01.1 Plan 34: Pulled parking brake as the sketched plate Summary

The pulled PARKING BRAKE is now one four-point plate hinged on the same level bottom line as the released flap, tilted back with a shorter parallel top edge shifted to the right (owner finding R3-4.1); the old wedge (side wall plus lifted face) is gone.

## What changed

- `Flap.tsx`: removed `flapWall`, `flapFace`, `FLAP.lift` and the wall, wall-edge and lifted tones. Added `PLATE` (`topFraction` 0.7, `shiftFraction` 0.5, `rise` 22) and the pure `flapPlate()` returning A, B, C, D. `FLAP.angleDeg` (60) stays as the documented standing-angle model; it now only bounds the rise (gate). The pulled drawing is the recess, one `polygon data-flap-plate` (fill `#2b3036`, the released face grey, edge `#4a525c`) and one `line data-flap-free` (1 unit highlight along the top edge, inset 1 unit at each end). The released drawing, the pressed ring, the recess, id, states, default, nameless flag, tapToggle, hit rectangle and the PARKING / BRAKE placard are untouched. DetentLever.tsx, the registry and the layout reference were not touched.
- `tests/lever-skins.test.ts`: the wall, lifted-face and true-rotation cases were removed with the code; new describe "the pulled plate follows the owner sketch (R3-4.1)" pins the relations (written first, 11 failures before the change, all green after).
- `e2e/lever-skins.spec.ts`: the brake test counts `data-flap-plate` per state (0, 1, 0) and asserts `data-flap-wall` never exists; text count stays 0.

## Gate for the sketch relations

Unit cases: (1) bottom edge level, at the hinge y 22, from -15 to 15; (2) top edge level and at least 15 above it; (3) top length 0.5 to 0.9 of the base; (4) top left end at least 0.3 of the base right of the base's left end; (5) top right end right of the bottom right end by more than 0 and at most 0.35 of the base; (6) convex with one winding; (7) rise within length x cos and length x sin of the standing angle (angle itself 45 to 75); (8) all art inside the footprint, at most 14 elements, no text, in both states and both pressed values; (9) plate fill and stroke equal the released face's; (10) no `data-flap-wall` in any state; plus the drawn polygon equals `flapPlate()` point for point.

## Sketch measurement and the disagreement (assumption for the owner)

The private sketch (`.input/parking-brake-pulled-sketch.jpg`, viewed only, not committed, linked or embedded) was measured by the planner in sketch pixels with the released flap 125 px wide: base 156 px, top edge 130 px (0.83 of the base), top edge starts 89 px (0.57 of the base) right of the base's left end and ends 65 px (0.42) right of the base's right end, rise 112 px (0.72 of the base); left side up to the right (89 over 108 px), right side up and right (66 over 106 px). The owner's text says the top edge is roughly 55 to 60 percent of the base. The two disagree on the top width, and the sketch's numbers would put the top right corner at x 27, outside the 44 wide footprint. The build takes the middle, 0.7, and fits the footprint. The gate accepts 0.5 to 0.9, so the owner can move `PLATE.topFraction` (and `shiftFraction`) toward the sketch inside the gate by editing the constants.

It reads as a plate hinged at the bottom and tilted back, seen from the front left; the grey is the released flap's grey and clearly different from the dark recess, so pulled and released differ at a glance (T-01.1-61). The recess and the PARKING / BRAKE placard look as before. The design constants were not changed after the visual check.
