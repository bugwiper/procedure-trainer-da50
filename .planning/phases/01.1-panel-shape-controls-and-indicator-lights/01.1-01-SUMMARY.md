## Planner decisions carried out (for the owner list in plan 01.1-10)

- **PD-01:** pedestal split into `env_control` (957, 750, 211 x 265) and `pedestal` (957, 1015, 211 x 275). Reason: in a 540-tall region the 734 x 315 phone shows 0.56 px per unit and POWER's handle would reach 34 px, below the 44 px guarantee. Both stay zoom targets.
- **PD-02:** de-ice split into `deice` (85, 275, 175 x 115) and `deice_switches` (85, 390, 175 x 75). Reason: one 190-tall region cannot give the four-position selector 44 px per detent. `deice_switches` has no controls yet.
- **PD-06:** hit rects narrowed so each region keeps 7 units of free background: `sw.fadec` 74 to 66 wide, `lv.alt_static`, `lv.emerg_gear` and `lv.alt_air` 60 to 44 wide. Centres unchanged, the visible part stays inside the hit rect, `lv.emerg_gear` still `dragOnly`. The full-view phone scale drops from 0.312 to 0.242 px per unit with the taller viewBox.
- The clearance gate forced no narrowing beyond these Table C1 rows.
