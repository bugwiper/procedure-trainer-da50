# Phase 01.1 Plan 04: Gear and flap lights Summary

**Seven derived lamps (three green gear lamps, a red UNSAFE lamp that is off in every reachable state, UP/T/O/LDG flap lamps) and two text readouts follow the GEAR and FLAPS levers through computed signals, outside the control registry so they can never be operated or graded.**

- **Duration:** about 9 min (2026-10-20T15:35Z to 15:44Z)
- **Tasks:** 3 (Task 1 tracer, Task 2 auto tdd, Task 3 auto)
- **Files:** 5 created, 4 modified

## Accomplishments

- `src/engine/indicators.ts` (pure): `IndicatorSpec`, `IndicatorDef`, `IndicatorGroupSpec`, `IndicatorSet`, `defineIndicators` and `evaluateIndicator`. Invariants raise `IndicatorError` naming the id: `lt.` prefix and id pattern, unique ids, never equal to a control id, region exists, non-empty reads that are all registry controls, `r > 0`, lamp disc plus bezel inside its region, group members exist and share the group's region. `evaluateIndicator` passes the lit function only the controls named in `reads`.
- `src/aircraft/da50rg/indicators.ts`: `gearLamps(gear, inTransit)`, `flapLamps(flaps)`, `gearReadout` (GEAR UNSAFE, GEAR DOWN LOCKED, GEAR UP), `flapReadout` (FLAPS UP, FLAPS T/O, FLAPS LDG), `GEAR_IN_TRANSIT`, and `DA50RG_INDICATORS` with the seven lamps of Table I1 and the two groups. In transit lights only UNSAFE; in this phase the flag is always false.
- `bridge.ts`: `createPanelBridge(registry, indicators?)`, `bridge.indicators` and `bridge.indicatorOf(id)` (a computed signal over the lever signals; throws for an unknown id); the e2e hook gains `indicators()`. `App.tsx` changed one line.
- `IndicatorLight.tsx`: lit lamp has a 2-unit bright ring, a 1-unit white inner ring at 70 percent and the r 13 halo from `paint()`; unlit lamp is a dark disc with a thin ring; bezel r 9. The group carries `data-indicator-id`, `data-kind="indicator"`, `data-lit`, `role="img"`, `aria-label` and `pointer-events="none"`, with no `data-hit` and no handler. `IndicatorReadout` writes the group text (`data-indicator-group`, `data-state-text`, Micro 6). Nothing animates.
- `Panel.tsx` renders the lamps and readouts of each region before the control widgets.
- Tests: 26 unit tests in `tests/indicators.test.ts` (derive, readouts, store answers unknown, definition invariants, contrast of 3:1 or better for all three colours, at most 40 nodes lit and unlit, thick versus thin ring, font sizes 6, 8, 10, 14 only); layout test resolves `lt.*` ids and adds the seven position rows, the ordering rows (now running), bezel-in-region and bezel-clear-of-footprint checks, and a readout-clearance check; 7 e2e tests in `e2e/indicator.spec.ts` run in all six projects.
