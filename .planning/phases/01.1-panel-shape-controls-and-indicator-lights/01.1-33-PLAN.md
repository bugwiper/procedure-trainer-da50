## Data tables

Table B4, the cut rectangle of a title (global units; the title anchors and the outline edges are those of bus-boxes.ts; the baseline lies 3 below the edge it cuts; these are design numbers, adjust inside the gates and record the final numbers in the SUMMARY):

| Quantity | Value | Reason |
|----------|-------|--------|
| estimated advance per character at size 8 | 6 | the panel font rule is 0.62 of the size (4.96) plus the 0.06 em letter spacing of stage.css (0.48), rounded up for wide capitals |
| horizontal margin each side | 4 | at least 3 units of clear edge gap beside the glyphs |
| height above the baseline | 8 | covers the cap height 5.6 and the edge line 3 above the baseline |
| height below the baseline | 3 | the edge line is 3 above the baseline, so the rectangle reaches 6 below the edge |
| width | 6 per character plus 8 | ESSENTIAL BUS 86, MAIN BUS 56, AVIONICS BUS 80 |
| x, y | x = anchor x minus half the width, y = baseline minus 8 | centred on the anchor |
