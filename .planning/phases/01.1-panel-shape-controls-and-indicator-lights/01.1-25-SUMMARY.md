# Phase 01.1 Plan 25: Double click or double tap zoom on the background Summary

A lone click or tap no longer changes the view; a pair of short, still taps on the background (lift to lift within 350 ms and 24 px) zooms into the region under it or fully out while zoomed, and a double tap on a control stays two control taps.

## What was done

- Task 1 (tracer, commit ef5f4ef): unit tests first (7 red tracker cases), then `tap-intent.ts` lost `tapIntent`, `SUPPRESS_MS` and `SUPPRESS_PX` and gained `DOUBLE_TAP_MS`, `DOUBLE_TAP_PX`, `DoubleTapIntent` and `createDoubleTapTracker()` (`tap(point, zoomed)`, `reset()`). `zoom.ts` creates one tracker per `attachZoomGestures` call, feeds it every background tap (the pointerup of a gesture that passes `isBackgroundTap`), applies `zoom-in` with the same region lookup as before and `zoom-out` through `zoomOut`, and resets the tracker when a control pointer goes down, when a second background pointer starts a pinch, when a background pointer pans past the slop, when a background pointer ends as a non-tap or is cancelled, and when the view changed since the remembered tap (Full panel button). The header comment lists the new arbitration (a) to (g). `e2e/zoom.spec.ts` was rewritten to the new contract (see below).
- Task 2 (commit 0496cac): `zoomIntoRegion` lost its compatibility branch; it now asserts the full viewBox after the first tap and then sends the second. `avionics.spec.ts`, `neutral.spec.ts` follow the new contract; `avionics.spec.ts` now imports its helpers from `e2e/support/zoom.ts`.

## New and changed e2e coverage

- zoom.spec.ts: a double click or double tap on switch_bank zooms at full tap size, a single tap on sw.engine_master records one change, Full panel returns; a single tap never zooms at the full view or while zoomed; a double tap while zoomed zooms fully out and the pair is consumed; a second tap later than the pair window does nothing; a double tap in no region does nothing; from an intermediate pinch level a double tap zooms fully out (phone-chromium); @hooks: gesture sequence emits nothing, a double tap on sw.engine_master is exactly two events at the full view and zoomed with the view unchanged, a control tap and a background tap 3 px beside it are not a pair (also background, control, background), a pan between two taps ends the pair.
- Mutation check: removing the tracker reset on a control pointer down makes the control-between-two-background-taps case fail; the reset was restored.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug in test design] Helper first tap paired with a preceding tap**
- **Found during:** Task 2 whole-suite run
- **Issue:** in "a single click or tap on the background never zooms", the helper's first tap came right after a single tap at the same point and formed a real double tap, so the strict helper assertion failed.
- **Fix:** wait out the pair window (450 ms) before `zoomIntoRegion` in that test. The helper stays fast and strict.
- **Files modified:** e2e/zoom.spec.ts
- **Commit:** 0496cac

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Neutral single-tap walk too slow**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** waiting 400 ms after each of about 90 neutral points exceeded the 30 s test timeout.
- **Fix:** wait out the pair window only between points less than 60 px apart (distant points cannot pair) and give the test a 90 s timeout (it takes about 20 s).
- **Files modified:** e2e/neutral.spec.ts
- **Commit:** 0496cac

## Assumptions taken

- A double tap whose point lies in no region does nothing (same as a single tap in no region did); the pair is measured lift to lift. Both as the plan asked to record.
- The "view changed by the Full panel button between the taps ends the pair" rule is an addition inside the plan's arbitration (g); no decision was needed.
