# Phase 01.1 Plan 32: Two WebKit findings of plan 31 Summary

The Full panel button no longer undoes a double-tap zoom on WebKit, and the FAN e2e test points no longer sit on a spot edge; the full WebKit run is now 0 failed.

## What and why

1. **Real bug, Full panel button (8 WebKit failures, phone-webkit).** After the second tap of the double tap zoomed in, WebKit sent a synthesized `click` to the Full panel button that had just appeared about 9 px from the tap point, and the view returned to full. Fix: `isStrayFullPanelClick({ shownAt, pressedAt, clickAt })` in `tap-intent.ts`, a pure function of `event.timeStamp` values with no timer. A click is dropped when it comes less than `FULL_PANEL_GUARD_MS` (400) after a view change made by a double tap (`viewChangedByTapAt()` in `zoom.ts`, stamped with the lifting pointer's timeStamp) and no pointer was pressed on the button since that change (`onPointerDown` in `App.tsx`). A real tap presses the button first, so it still works at once; only the stray click is dropped. After 400 ms every click works. No e2e spec was changed or weakened (the spec helpers tap the button right after zooming, which a pure time guard would have broken).
2. **Fragile test, FAN (4 failures, all WebKit projects).** The f1 and f3 glyph-centre tap points (+-19.1, -11) were 0.6 units from the f2 spot edge. They are now x +-24, 5.5 units inside the spot.
3. **Second fragility found behind it (deviation, Rule 1, test only).** With the glyph fixed, `phone-webkit` failed one step later: the "exact centre does nothing" tap. At the full phone view a unit is 0.24 px and WebKit rounds touch points to whole pixels, so the rounding moved the tap out of the 2-unit dead zone onto f2. That tap now runs only where 2 units span more than 1 px (every mouse project and the larger touch views); `tests/rotary.test.ts` (`tapStop(fan, 0, 0)` is null) still checks the dead zone exactly. Disclosed in the test comment and in Round 4 item 7.
