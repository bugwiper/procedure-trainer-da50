# Phase 01.1 Plan 27: Parking brake as a hinged flap Summary

The PARKING BRAKE is now a hinged flap drawn in pseudo-perspective: flat in a console recess when released, and, when pulled, standing up 60 degrees about its lower hinge as a wedge (dark side wall, lighter lifted face, highlight on the free edge). One tap anywhere on its footprint toggles it; there is no drag and no marker.

## What was done

- Tests first for both tasks (registry rules, `targetSide`, brake data, tap logic, e2e tap toggle, then the geometry gates).
- Task 1 (tracer, commit a6bad9d): `LEVER_SKINS` renames `parking-lever` to `parking-flap` (still nine entries); `DetentLeverDef` gains `tapToggle?: true`; `defineRegistry` rejects tapToggle on a lever without exactly two detents, a drag-only lever, a lever with markers, and enforces skin and option as a pair. `targetSide` returns the smaller footprint side for a tap-toggle lever. `Flap.tsx` (pure FlapBody) and the widget branch (one hit rect over the whole footprint, `makeTapToggleHandlers` with a 10 px tap slop, cancel and lost capture clear without actuating). `lv.parking_brake` changes only its skin and gains tapToggle. The old brake lever art is gone from `LeverHandles.tsx`. The tracer gate (`npx vitest run`, the three-project lever and inventory e2e, typecheck) was green before any further work.
- Task 2 (commit 0dd614c): geometry, text and replacement tests, then the visual check and tone adjustment (below).
