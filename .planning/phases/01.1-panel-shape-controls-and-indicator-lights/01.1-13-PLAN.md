## Data tables

Table R1, rocker perspective (executor designs the exact polygons within these rules):

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Viewpoint | slightly from the right: the raised half shows a visible side wall on its right edge and its outer end; the wall is a darker tone than the face |
| Pressed half | flat against the panel: no wall, the darker face tone, as today |
| Raised half | lighter face tone with the 1-unit highlight, drawn up to 3 units displaced toward the viewer (face shifted left and away from the groove) with the wall filling the gap |
| Middle position | both halves level: each carries a short wall of half the lift, both tagged, neither as tall as a raised half |
| Wall tones | white rocker: the existing shadow tone `#8a897f`; red rocker: `#5a0a0f` |
| Tags | each wall polygon carries `data-rocker-wall` with the value `upper` or `lower` (the half it belongs to) |
| Elements | the two half faces stay `path` elements in the order upper, lower; walls are `polygon` elements after them |
| Extent | `rockerExtent` includes the lift; every rocker of DA50RG fits its hit rect; at most 40 nodes |

Table T1, toggle body inputs after this plan: `position` is `low`, `mid` or `high`; for a two-state toggle the second state is `high` and the first state `low`; `mid` is never produced by a two-state toggle (plan 18 adds the three-state toggle). A bat handle points up for `high`, down for `low`, and for `mid` shows a short stub toward the viewer (tip at the pivot, ball tip visible).
