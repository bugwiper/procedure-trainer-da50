# Phase 01.1 Plan 20: Environmental control rebuild Summary

The environmental control plate now matches the owner's side-view photo: DIRECTION and TEMPERATURE side by side on top, FAN under DIRECTION, CIRCULATING AIR across the bottom, each in an outlined box whose title cuts the top edge, with no state text and nothing overlapping.

## What was built

- **Task 1 (tracer, commit 90f4c4b):** region `env_control` (DIRECTION, TEMPERATURE, FAN) and new region `env_circ` (CIRCULATING AIR); plate `environmental` enlarged to 962, 752, 201 x 284 and carrying both regions; `ENV_BOXES` and `ENV_TITLE` in plates.ts; the shell draws the plate title, the four boxes (`groupBoxPath`: outline open under the title) and the temperature triangle (`temperatureTriangle(registry)`). The four controls moved to the Table V1 positions and became nameless (the Rotary body honours `nameless` now); `lv.env_temp` has legends cold and warm only, the other two sliders empty legends lists. Reference rows, Table V3 ordering rows, the region list of layout.spec (19 regions) and the footprint gate (env_control left `FOOTPRINT_KNOWN_OPEN`) updated.
- **Task 2 (commit 180a806):** `sw.env_fan` stops off 180, f1 300, f2 360, f3 420 (240 degree sweep, hit 80 by 40, tap half 40), scale `fan` replaces `dots` in the registry vocabulary; `RotaryBody` prints the first stop's label (OFF) and draws one four-blade glyph (`fanGlyphPath`, a single path with four subpaths) per further stop at its angle, sizes 3, 4.5, 6. Tests cover stops, snap across the 120 degree gap, stepping with clamps, only-OFF text, no dots, at most 40 elements, and an e2e tap and drag case.
- **Task 3 (commit 7055bff):** fresh-air glyph (rectangle open on the left with an inward arrow) and recirculation glyph (rectangle with a circling arrow), each one path with its `data-icon`; vertical sliders print pictograms right of the slot (never under the handle); `env_control` left `KNOWN_OPEN` of the art-fit gate and `TEXT_OVERLAP_KNOWN` is empty.
- **Docs (commit 190cfd5):** the environmental rows of `docs/PANEL-REVIEW.md` follow the new positions (not in the plan's file list; the full document pass stays with plan 23).
