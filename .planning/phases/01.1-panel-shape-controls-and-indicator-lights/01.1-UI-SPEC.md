## Spacing Scale

All values are viewBox units for panel art unless marked px (HTML chrome). Declared values are multiples of 4.

| Token | Value | Usage |
|-------|-------|-------|
| xs | 4 | Gap label-to-control, bezel padding, state-text gap |
| sm | 8 | Gap between a control and its bezel ear, lamp-to-caption gap |
| md | 16 | Gap inside a control group (e.g. gear lights cluster), plate inner margin |
| lg | 24 | Plate corner radius, gap between control groups on a plate |
| xl | 32 | Coaming shoulder radius |
| 2xl | 48 | Glareshield rocker pitch: exactly 48 (centres 1047, 1095, 1143, 1191) |
| 3xl | 64 | Hump corner radius |
| chrome px | 8 / 16 / 44 | Full panel button offset 8 px, footer horizontal padding 12 px (existing), minimum touch target 44 px |

Exceptions (a closed, bounded list; everything NOT named here must be a multiple of 4):
1. **Real-panel geometry from the photos.** Control centres, region boxes, shell and coaming path values, strip and plate widths and heights, console and knee-cavity coordinates, display bezel growth, placard positions and similar are measurements of the real aircraft panel (layout fidelity is the core value, D-04). They are exempt from the multiple-of-4 rule. Rule: every such value must be recorded in a geometry table of this spec (Coordinate Space and Regions, Panel Surface, Control Inventory) or in the registry; an unrecorded free-hand number is not covered by this exemption.
2. **Hairline-class skin detail below the scale.** Inside control skins and bezels: press shift 1, bezel-to-cap insets 2 and 3, chrome ring 3, strokes 1, 1.5 and 2, engraved placard lines 1, stitching dash 3 2, and corner radii rx 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12 and 18 (the larger radii belong to bezels and plates). Bound: each such value is at most 12 units, except plate and console corner radii (rx 18, 24, 40) which are shell geometry under exception 1.
3. **Hit rectangles** grow only up to the neighbour gap (P1 D-21) and are never smaller than the visible control. The hit rectangle is a registry property (`hit`), not part of the art.
4. **Footer height** 28 px (existing, multiple of 4, listed for completeness).

Everything else is on the scale: gaps between a control and its label or state text (4), bezel padding (4), group gaps (8, 16, 24), stitching inset (8), HTML chrome offsets and paddings (8, 12, 16, 44), the Full panel button radius (8 px, was 6 px), the rotate-hint padding (8 px 12 px).

## Typography

Panel text is SVG text in viewBox units; the existing `.panel-svg text` rule (B612, weight 700, letter-spacing 0.06em, default fill `#e6e9ed`) stays. The whole spec uses exactly four font sizes (6, 8, 10, 14) and two weights (400, 700). Panel text is in viewBox units; HTML chrome uses the same four numbers as CSS px (only 14 is used there). No other size appears anywhere.

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height |
|------|------|--------|-------------|
| Micro (detent legends, bracket sub-captions, KIAS values, ESS BUS NOT-FOR-NORMAL lines, group readouts) | 6 | 700 | 1.2 (7.2 units between baselines) |
| Placard (control labels, bus and group headings, light captions, GO-AROUND on bezels) | 8 | 700 | 1.2 (9.6 units between baselines) |
| State text (`data-state-text`: ON/OFF, UP/DOWN, RELEASED/PRESSED) | 10 | 700 | 1.2 (12 units between baselines) |
| Brand (RUBIN wordmark, DA50RG nameplate) | 14 | 700 | 1.2 (16.8 units between baselines) |
| Glass digits (B612 Mono, PFD and MFD frames, ArcGauge, BarGauge) | 6, 8, 10 or 14 only (snapped, see below) | 400 | 1.2 |

HTML chrome (footer, rotate hint, Full panel button, About) uses 14 CSS px only: weight 400 for footer, rotate hint and About text, weight 700 for the Full panel button and About close. This replaces the Phase 1 12 px footer and hint. Line height: 1.5 for running text (About, existing); the footer is a fixed 28 px bar with line-height 28 px and the rotate hint a single-line bar (padding 8 px 12 px, replacing the Phase 1 6 px 10 px), both exempt from the ratio because they are bars, not text flow (min-height 28 px lets the footer wrap on a narrow portrait phone).

Glass-digit snap (mechanical edit of the existing frame code, which today uses 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 16, 20 and 22): every `font-size` in `PfdFrame.tsx`, `MfdFrame.tsx`, `ArcGauge.tsx` and `BarGauge.tsx` is mapped to the nearest declared size: 9, 10, 11, 12 become 10; 13, 16, 20, 22 become 14; any 6 or 8 stays. The large gauge readouts therefore shrink (`unconfirmed` visual impact, owner reviews the first render; glass content becomes live in Phase 3 and 01.2 redraws the G1000 shapes).

Rules:
- Placard text is ALL CAPS, B612 700, tracking 0.06em, colour `#aab3bd` for control labels and `#c9d0d8` for engraved plate legends. Text never uses `textLength` stretching below 85 % of natural width; if a label does not fit, wrap to two lines (Micro or Placard size) with the 1.2 line height of the role, never shrink below 6.
- State text is 10, except on glareshield rockers and ELT where it is 8 (footprint under 36 wide). It is always visible (not hover-only) and is always the same string as `StateDef.label` (D-07, D-13).
- Contrast: all text at least 4.5:1 against its plate (`#aab3bd` on `#1a1c20` is about 8:1; `#e6e9ed` on `#1a1c20` about 14:1).

Shading rules (flat SVG gradients only):
1. Light comes from the top left. Raised parts get a 1-unit light edge on top and left (`rgba(255,255,255,0.18)`) and a 1-unit dark edge on bottom and right (`rgba(0,0,0,0.55)`).
2. All gradients and patterns are defined once in a root `<defs>` (`Defs.tsx`) with ids prefixed `g-` and `p-` and referenced by `url(#...)`. Widgets never define their own (the Phase 1 per-instance hatch pattern in GuardedToggle moves to the shared defs).
3. Max three stops per gradient; no animated gradients.
4. Lit lamps use a radial-gradient disc as halo (radius 1.8 x lamp radius), not a blur filter.
5. Pressed parts shift 1 unit down and darken one step (never change size by more than 2 units).
6. Decorative nodes carry `pointer-events="none"`. Per control at most 40 SVG nodes; the node count of every widget fixture is asserted in a Vitest test (guards phone performance).
7. Everything is original vector art. No tracing, no auto-vectorising, no embedding of the photos (D-06).

## Zoom UX (PNL-15, D-12, D-13)

### 11.1 Single tap zooms fully out (D-12)

Existing rules stay: drags and pinches never produce taps, controls own taps that start on `[data-hit], [data-detent]`, region taps from the full view zoom in.

| Situation | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| Full view, background tap | zoom to the tapped region (existing) |
| Zoomed (any level, including intermediate pinch levels), a tap on the background: one pointer, travel at most 10 px, down-to-up at most 300 ms, never touched a hit target, no second pointer during the gesture | `zoomOut(viewBox)` at once on pointerup (no 300 ms wait for a possible double tap) |
| Zoomed, drag (more than 10 px) or any two-pointer gesture | pan or pinch only, no zoom-out |
| Zoomed, tap on a control | control actuates; view unchanged |
| Second tap of a double-tap pair that began as a zoomed background tap | ignored for zoom-in: a 350 ms / 24 px suppression window after any zoom-out stops the second tap from immediately zooming into a region again |
| Double-tap, pinch-out to full, Full panel button | still work (unchanged) |

> Superseded on 2026-10-21 by owner finding R2-Z1 (plans 25 and 26): the single-tap rows above no longer hold. A single click or tap never zooms; a double click or double tap on the background (never on a control) zooms into the tapped region or, while zoomed, fully out. The table is kept for the record.

> Refined again on 2026-10-21 by owner findings R3-Z2 and R3-Z3 (plans 36 to 40): "never on a control" above no longer holds. A double click or double tap also zooms on a latching control and retracts its first actuation (R3-Z3); control presses are arbitrated, so latching controls act on release, a press that moves or gains a second finger pans or pinches the view, and momentary controls commit after 100 ms held still (R3-Z2).

Mouse behaves the same (single click on the background while zoomed). `isZoomed` stays the single predicate. No animation is added (existing instant viewBox change); honour `prefers-reduced-motion` by construction.

Tests to update or add: `tests/zoom.test.ts` (single tap from full-zoom and from an intermediate level zooms out; drag, pinch, hit-target tap and a slow tap over 300 ms do not; the second tap of a double tap does not zoom in; background tap at full view still zooms in) and `e2e/zoom.spec.ts` (replace the "only double tap zooms out" case; keep a double-tap case asserting the end state is the full view).
