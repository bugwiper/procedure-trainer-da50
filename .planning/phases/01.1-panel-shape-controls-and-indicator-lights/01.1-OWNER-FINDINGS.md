## G-0 Global rules (apply to every area)

1. **No dynamic text.** Nothing that changes with state: no ON/OFF/state words under or on any switch, lever, knob or button, no value readouts (e.g. FACE, 50, FRESH, RELEASED, NEUTRAL, GEAR UP). Only the static placard text of the real aircraft. Reason: a real placard under a switch says what the lower position means; a changing word contradicts it.
2. **Labels as in the real aircraft**, static. Unknown labels: list "control, assumed label" to the owner for correction before building.
3. **Indicator lamps only where the original has one.** No green lamps above switches; the position alone shows the state.
4. **Rocker switches, all sizes and colours:** no ON/OFF text on the rocker. Keep the shading and additionally draw the rocker slightly in side perspective (as if seen a little from the side): the pressed half lies flat against the panel, the other half visibly stands up. Goal: position readable at a glance. Toggles, rotary knobs and levers are already readable; keep them.
5. Parts must not stick out of their panel region. Where a part does not fit, enlarge the region (preferred over shrinking tap targets) and make neighbours give way.

## G-3 Environmental control and pedestal (layout is wrong)

- Whole layout was displaced; rebuild it as in the side-view photo: DIRECTION and TEMPERATURE side by side on top, FAN below, CIRCULATING AIR below that, then PARKING BRAKE, THROTTLE (POWER) and COWL FLAP; nothing overlapping (title, DEFROST label, FAN knob collided).
- TEMPERATURE: no 0-100 scale, no 75/50/25 numbers. Only WARM at top, COLD at bottom, between them a long thin pictogram-style triangle (wide end up, point down; stands for how much warm air is blown in). Keep the in-between steps in the app, without any scale.
- DIRECTION: keep the pictograms (defrost, face, feet) but no dynamic state text.
- FAN: OFF at the bottom, a small-to-large fan symbol running from upper left to upper right, no dots. Real knob: OFF at the bottom, turn clockwise, continuously adjustable up to about 2 o'clock for maximum. In the app: OFF plus three fixed stops across that sweep, pointer angle matching.
- CIRCULATING AIR: no words. Left the fresh-air pictogram (rectangle with an inward arrow), right the recirculation pictogram (rectangle with a circling arrow). No FRESH/RECIRC text.
- PARKING BRAKE: only the placard "PARKING BRAKE". A small lever that is pulled up to set the brake; no state text.
- COWL FLAP: a small lever acting as a switch, with a white lamp beside each position (OPEN, CLOSED) showing the position (lamps exist in the original); no state text.
