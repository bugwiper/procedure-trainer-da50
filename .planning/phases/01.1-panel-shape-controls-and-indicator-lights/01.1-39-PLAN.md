## Phase goal

The panel looks like the real DA50 RG with every control in kind-of real shape and colour, and the owner's second live-site review (01.1-OWNER-FINDINGS-R2.md, section Round 3) is closed: the bus titles are legible on Safari, the pulled parking brake looks as the owner drew it, a finger or a mouse that lands on a control can still pan and pinch the view, and a double click on a switch zooms without leaving it toggled.

<objective>
Migrate the momentary controls onto the press arbiter with the 100 ms hold, and register the drag controls (lever handles, knob bodies) as own targets that keep their gesture but can still be pinched over before the drag starts (owner finding R3-Z2, items 2, 3 and 5).

Output: a momentary factory and registration, the hold timer in the gesture layer, own registrations with the shared drag threshold, e2e cases for the hold, the quick tap, the discards and the drag controls, and a classification gate over the built panel.
</objective>

2. Momentary controls (ENGINE START, FADEC TEST, STALL/GEAR TEST, DE-ICE MAX/WIND SHIELD, LEVEL): the press commits after about 100 ms of being held still (pressed state and event), discarded on movement beyond the slop or a second pointer before that.
