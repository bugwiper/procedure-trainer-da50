Planner decisions behind R1 (record each in the SUMMARY; plan 01.1-10 lists them for the owner):
- PD-01: the UI-SPEC single pedestal region (957, 750, 211 x 540) is split into env_control (top) and pedestal (bottom). Reason: zoomed into a 540-tall region the 734 x 315 phone shows 0.56 px per unit, so POWER's 60-unit handle square reaches only 34 px, below the binding 44 px guarantee (PNL-09, UI-SPEC "each control must still reach 44 px"). Both parts stay zoom targets.
- PD-02: the UI-SPEC de-ice region (85, 275, 175 x 190) is split into deice (mode selector and ALTERNATE) and deice_switches (WIND SHIELD, ICE LIGHT, PUMP 2, ANNUN TEST, PUMP 1). Reason: in one 190-tall region the four-position mode selector cannot reach 44 px per detent without overlapping the switch row below.
- PD-06: hit rects that leave less than 7 units of free background in a region are narrowed (never below the visible part). The full-view phone scale drops from 0.312 to 0.242 px per unit with the taller viewBox, and region tap needs a free background point.
